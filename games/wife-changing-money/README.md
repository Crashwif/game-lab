# Wife Changing Money

A kitchen at 3:07 am. A degen in a dressing gown hunches over a laptop lit only by the chart. Mugs multiply, the cat watches, texts from bed stack on the phone, and an open suitcase by the door fills with gold: the wife-changing-money meter, with LAMBO, LAWYER and BOAT showing up as it fills. On the laptop a SELL button sits under the chart, and the cursor drifts toward it and away with the fear, hovering and shivering right over it near the top without ever clicking. On the fridge the wedding photo tilts a little further with every ceiling thump, and the WAGMI letter magnets work loose one by one. The stair light comes on, the ceiling thumps, and a silhouette appears at the top of the stairs. An accepted cashout finally clicks SELL (the screen flashes SOLD), spills a handful of gold from the case, closes the lid, freezes the meter, and tiptoes him upstairs: SHE NEVER KNEW. The crash is her on the stairs: a hit-stop on the caught frame, the camera punching in, him jumping in his chair, then she comes down slow before time catches up. The photo drops off the fridge onto the counter and cracks, W and A fall after it and the rest slide over to spell NGMI, the SELL button greys out to CAN'T under a cursor that clicks and clicks, the ring hits the table, the cat rides the suitcase out the door, the dog follows, a card from Split & Co slides in, and the chart goes to zero.

- `audio.ts`: the shared page audio (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). Lo-fi beats that tighten with the multiplier; the crash is a slam, softened to a thud when he already closed the lid. Cues: her texts (`notify`), the ceiling thumps and her steps on the stairs (`stomp`), the mug going off the counter (`shatter`), her peek at the top of the stairs (`creak`), the ring on the table (`coin`), the magnets and the photo hitting the counter (`clang`, `thud`), the suitcase out the door (`door`), plus milestones on the caption ladder and the cash-out register.
- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `kitchen.ts`: the room, the laptop chart with its SELL button and cursor, the text stack, the stair light and ceiling thump, the fridge (the tilting photo, the WAGMI magnets and their fall to NGMI), the suitcase meter, the wife, the ring, the cat, the card, and the event edges the cues fire from.
- `trader.ts`: the hunched rig, the lid, the tiptoe up the stairs, the caught pose and the jolt out of the chair.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the screen glow, the crash choreography (hit-stop, slow motion, punch-in), the audio cues, the HUD (captions, multiplier, the meter, the secured badge, the outcome) and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round used by the gallery.

She is dressed, the joke is the chart, and nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. A scene that opens mid-round or on a crashed round settles into the aftermath (the photo already on the counter, NGMI already spelled) without replaying it or playing its cues. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the screen shake, the hit-stop, the punch-in and the shivering of the cursor and the loose magnets.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Wife Changing Money URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to close the lid. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/wife-changing-money/`.

## Visual direction and small screens

The scene now introduces physical presentation acts at 32, 52, 75, 100, 125 and 145 seconds, followed by bounded recurring acts for unusually long rounds. New props accompany changes in character effort, with a short easing of tension before renewed activity. `acts.ts` reads elapsed time only; it cannot choose an outcome or promise that a round will last this long.

On narrow screens, `portrait.ts` presents an enlarged character/detail view, a small overview that preserves the location, and a readable current line from the actual dialogue/chat/monitor state. The canonical shell still owns controls, round state and accepted cashouts. Reduced motion removes the new prop oscillation.
