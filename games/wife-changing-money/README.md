# Wife Changing Money

A kitchen at 3:07 am. A degen in a dressing gown hunches over a laptop lit only by the chart. Mugs multiply, the cat watches, texts from bed stack on the phone, and an open suitcase by the door fills with gold: the wife-changing-money meter, with LAMBO, LAWYER and BOAT showing up as it fills. On the laptop a SELL button sits under the chart, and the cursor drifts toward it and away with the fear, hovering and shivering right over it near the top without ever clicking. On the fridge the wedding photo tilts a little further with every ceiling thump, and the WAGMI letter magnets work loose one by one. Upstairs escalates with the multiplier: at 1.6× one thump and the stair light stutters on and off again, at 2× a creak and the cat stares at the stairs, from 2.2× the light stays on, from 2.5× the ceiling thumps closer and closer together, at 3.3× his elbow sends a mug off the counter, and from 4× she peeks down from the stairs, ducks back, peeks again, and from 8.5× stays. He reacts at the desk: a look down at the phone for each of her texts, a jump and a glance over his shoulder at every thump, a frozen listen at the first one. An accepted cashout sends the cursor darting to SELL (the screen flashes SOLD), spills a handful of gold from the case, closes the lid, freezes the meter, and tiptoes him up the stairs and off along the landing: SHE NEVER KNEW. If the chart keeps going without him the caption keeps score (TOOK PROFITS, KEPT THE WIFE; JEETED. STILL MARRIED). The crash is her on the stairs: a held hit-stop on the caught frame, the camera punching in and holding, him jumping in his chair, then she storms down one tread per stomp, flings the ring off halfway, and hops off the last step past the suitcase. The photo drops off the fridge onto the counter and cracks, W and A fall after it and the rest slide over to spell NGMI, the cursor lunges for SELL and it greys out to CAN'T under a cursor that clicks and clicks, the ring hits the floor, the cat rides the suitcase out the door, the dog follows, a card from Split & Co slides in, and the chart goes to zero. A crash after a cashout is RUG DODGED: the light snaps on, he scurries if he is still on the stairs, and she only comes down to check once he is up, then goes back to bed. A short blink to black covers the kitchen resetting for the next round.

- `audio.ts`: the shared page audio (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). Lo-fi beats that tighten with the multiplier; the crash is a slam, softened to a thud when he already closed the lid. Cues: her texts (`notify`), the ceiling thumps and each of her steps on the stairs (`stomp`), the mug going off the counter (`shatter`), the floorboard at 2× and her peeks (`creak`), a heartbeat under the music while he sits exposed at the desk, quickening with the tension (`heartbeat`), the ring landing (`coin`), the magnets and the photo hitting the counter (`clang`, `thud`), the suitcase out the door (`door`), plus milestones on the caption ladder and the cash-out register.
- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `kitchen.ts`: the room, the laptop chart with its SELL button and cursor, the text stack, the stair light and ceiling thump, the fridge (the tilting photo, the WAGMI magnets and their fall to NGMI), the suitcase meter, the wife, the ring, the cat, the card, and the event edges the cues fire from.
- `trader.ts`: the hunched rig, his head turns and the elbow jab, the lid, the tiptoe up the stairs and off the landing, the caught pose and the jolt out of the chair.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the screen glow, the tension (1 − 1/x, so the 1×–3× window most rounds live in sweeps 0 to 0.67), the crash choreography (hit-stop, slow motion, punch-in), the audio cues, the HUD (captions, multiplier, the meter, the secured badge, the outcome) and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round used by the gallery.

She is dressed, the joke is the chart, and nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. A scene that opens mid-round or on a crashed round settles into the aftermath (the photo already on the counter, NGMI already spelled) without replaying it or playing its cues. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the screen shake, the hit-stop, the punch-in, the shivering of the cursor and the loose magnets, and the stair light's stutter (it comes on once instead).

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Wife Changing Money URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to close the lid. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/wife-changing-money/`.

## Visual direction and small screens

The scene now introduces physical presentation acts at 32, 52, 75, 100, 125 and 145 seconds, followed by bounded recurring acts for unusually long rounds. Each act's props fade in and out, and mid-act he stops typing to listen; the acts never lower the tension, and their props fade out over a crash or a cashout. `acts.ts` reads elapsed time only; it cannot choose an outcome or promise that a round will last this long.

On narrow screens, `portrait.ts` presents an enlarged character/detail view, a small overview that preserves the location, and a readable current line from the actual dialogue/chat/monitor state. The canonical shell still owns controls, round state and accepted cashouts. Reduced motion removes the new prop oscillation.
