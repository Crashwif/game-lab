# Streak Stalker

**Keep your daily language streak alive before the green owl kicks your door in**

As the multiplier climbs, the unhinged green mascot creeps from your notifications straight into your living room window. Cashing out submits a quick matching lesson to trigger a protective streak freeze. Crashing lets the feathered terror breach your front door wielding a golden dictionary.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *Language app mascot spotted stalking user's ring camera after missed daily lesson* ([source](https://x.com/LinguaPanic/status/1894100124892110848)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- streak-stalker` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
