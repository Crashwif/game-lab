# Fallen Ref Flag

**Tumble onto the turf and fling penalty flags straight from your back**

A stunned referee gets knocked flat on the gridiron and furiously hurls yellow penalty laundry into the stadium rafters. Cashing out blows the official whistle and rolls straight into a timely commercial break. Crashing buries the entire field under a suffocating mountain of flags and thunderous booing.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *NFL referee kicked in midsection launches penalty flag high into the air from his back* ([source](https://x.com/MLFootball/status/2114483418146673089)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- ref-flag-launch` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
