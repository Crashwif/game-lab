# Wen Binance

A queue outside a nightclub called THE EXCHANGE. A coin in a hoodie waits in a velvet-rope line behind a bouncer whose clipboard reads INSIDERS ONLY; the line advances and the hype meter on the marquee climbs with the multiplier, the DJ's bass shakes the doors, insiders in suits are waved past, the LISTING FEE plaque by the door goes up while you wait, and the marquee reads LISTING SOON. Tension: the bouncer looks at his clipboard longer, the bass gets louder, the doors crack open to show the dance floor, the suits inside head for the back exit, the marquee flickers between LISTING and DELISTING and its letters work loose one at a time, dangle from a corner swinging on the bass, and drop to the pavement (LISTING SOON, LIST NG S ON, LI TNG S O), and a scalper in a long coat works the foreground with a sandwich board of FAST-TRACK LISTING tickets whose price climbs with the round (psst, skip the line? cash only). An accepted exit is leaving the line with your bag: a burst of confetti and a green flash, a taxi pulls up (the scalper runs from the witness) and the badge reads LEFT THE QUEUE. The crash is sell the news: the record scratches and the handles rattle for a beat, then the doors burst with a short freeze, a moment of slow motion and the camera punching in on the doorway; the bouncer flinches, the suits pour out with bags, every letter left on the marquee lets go and bounces on the pavement, the scalper bolts and drops his tickets, the marquee lights DELISTED, the plaque reads REFUNDS: NO, the coin is let in to an empty club with the lights on, and the bouncer flips the sign to DELISTED.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the page's sound, the shell's copy (see the repository README): opt-in through the Sound button, synthesised, nothing loaded. The scene runs it in `club` with a `scratch` crash, and cues it from what it already tracks: a `whistle` and the milestone ding for each suit waved past, `door` as one goes in, `buzz` for the bouncer's head-shake, `creak` as the doors crack and as a letter works loose, `ratchet` as one lets go and `clang` (a few tenths apart at most) as it lands, `engine` and `door` for your taxi, `door`, `crowd` and `boo` as the doors burst, and the cash-out fanfare.
- `club.ts`: the club front: the marquee, its flicker and its letters (loose, swinging, falling, down), the doors on springs with the dance floor behind them, the bouncer's rig (look-down, head-shake, wave-through, the flinch), the INSIDERS ONLY clipboard, the LISTING FEE plaque, the suits waved in and pouring out, the scalper and his tickets, the taxi and the sign flip.
- `queue.ts`: the velvet-rope queue of coins that advances on the curve, their chatter, your coin's exit to the taxi with its confetti, the panic run and the suit count.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the HUD (captions, multiplier, the suit count, the secured badge, the outcome pop) and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round used by the gallery.

The queue's advance, the marquee meter and its letters, the bass, the bouncer, the fee, the scalper's price and the suits all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. The club is fictional. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the bass shake, the marquee flicker, the strobe, the green flash, the confetti, and the crash's freeze, slow motion and punch-in.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Wen Binance URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to leave the queue. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/wen-binance/`.
