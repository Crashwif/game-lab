# Wen Binance

A queue outside a nightclub called THE EXCHANGE. A coin in a hoodie waits in a velvet-rope line behind a bouncer whose clipboard reads INSIDERS ONLY; a player's coin wears a small YOU tag (a spectator's does not). The line shuffles forward half a step at a time, the coin at the front first and each one behind a moment later, and the hype meter on the marquee climbs with the multiplier. The DJ's bass kicks on the score's 128 BPM grid (counted from the moment the round starts running, which is when the shell starts the full-length score over), harder as the hype climbs and on the half-beats too past 2×, and each coin in the line bobs to it a few milliseconds apart. The tension follows `1 − 1/x` (a third at 1.5×, half at 2×, two thirds at 3×), so the first dozen seconds, where most rounds end, carry the build: the bouncer checks his clipboard a second in, a scalper in a long coat walks in at 1.25× with a sandwich board of FAST-TRACK LISTING tickets whose price climbs with the round (psst, skip the line? cash only), the first marquee letter works loose at 1.35× and drops at 1.8×, the second at 1.6× and 2.3×, suits power-walk past the rope at 1.5×, 2× and 3× and the doors swing open to let each one in, the handles rattle at 1.9× and 2.75× and the line thinks it is going in (it is not), the marquee flickers to LISTING from 1.43× and to DELISTING from 2.2×, the doors crack open for good at 2.33×, each coin in the line loses its nerve somewhere between 2.4× and 5×, and from 3.5× the suits inside slip out of the back exit one by one. Longer rounds keep going: the gap in the doors widens, more letters fall (LISTING SOON, LIST NG S ON, LI TNG S O, down to I T N S by 200×), the LISTING FEE plaque goes up while you wait, and the captions run on to WEN BINANCE and SER, THIS IS A QUEUE. While you are in the line a tick on the off-beats quickens from every four beats to every half-beat. The feet of the line, the suits and the scalper step on the distance they cover, and the scalper slows and squashes through each turn.

An accepted exit is leaving the line with your bag: a burst of confetti and a green flash, the NO JEETS poster flashes, the neighbours point and shout JEET! and paper hands!!, a taxi pulls up (the scalper runs from the witness) and your coin's tag reads TOOK PROFITS while the line keeps moving without you (LEFT THE QUEUE, JEETED. NO REGRETS, THE LINE MOVED. SO?, PROFIT IS PROFIT). The crash is sell the news: the record scratches and the handles rattle for a beat, then the doors burst with a 0.15 s freeze, a moment of slow motion and the camera snapping 10% in on the doorway for 0.3 s; the bouncer flinches, the suits pour out with bags, every letter left on the marquee lets go and bounces on the pavement, the scalper bolts and drops his tickets, DELISTED stamps the screen, the marquee lights DELISTED, the plaque reads REFUNDS: NO, the house lights come up on an empty club and the bouncer flips the sign to DELISTED, all inside the two-second crash hold, as the coin at the front is finally let in, to nobody. A crash after you left reads DODGED THE DUMP; one at 1.00× reads NEVER EVEN LISTED to a spectator. The crash's last frame then dissolves into the next round's betting phase.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the page's sound, the shell's copy (see the repository README): opt-in through the Sound button, with the recorded score (128 BPM) and synthesised effects. The scene runs it in `club` with a `scratch` crash, passes it the tension, and cues it from what it already tracks: the off-beat `tick` pulse, a `whistle` and the milestone ding for each suit waved past, `door` as one goes in, `buzz` for the bouncer's head-shake, `creak` as the doors crack, as a letter works loose and as the handles rattle, `ratchet` as a letter lets go and `clang` (a few tenths apart at most) as it lands, `engine` and `door` for your taxi, `door`, `crowd` and `boo` as the doors burst, and the cash-out fanfare.
- `club.ts`: the club front: the marquee, its bulb chase, flicker and letters (loose, swinging, falling, down), the bass on the beat grid, the doors on springs with the dance floor and the dancers slipping out the back, the bouncer's rig (look-down, head-shake, wave-through, the flinch), the INSIDERS ONLY clipboard, the NO JEETS poster, the LISTING FEE plaque, the suits waved in and pouring out and the scalper on a distance-driven walk cycle, his tickets, the taxi and the sign flip.
- `queue.ts`: the velvet-rope queue of coins that shuffles forward on the curve, their bob to the bass, moods and chatter, the jeers and your coin's exit to the taxi with its confetti, the panic run and the suit count.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the tension and the beat clock, the HUD (captions, multiplier, the suit count, the secured badge, the outcome pop), the hit-stop and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round used by the gallery.

The queue's advance, the marquee meter and its letters, the bass, the bouncer, the fee, the scalper's price and the suits all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. The club is fictional. The published game uses full animation regardless of browser motion preferences.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Wen Binance URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to leave the queue. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/wen-binance/`.
