# Rug Rails

The lab's first skill game. A frog in a hoodie sprints down three neon subway lanes seen from behind, on the Trenches Line, where the walls are tagged WAGMI and NGMI and the billboards say MIND THE GAP (IN YOUR PORTFOLIO). Arrows, WASD or swipes swerve, jump and slide him past sell walls, KYC gates, rolled-up rugs and the Exit Scam Express (a ramped car is a road onto its roof and the coins up there); every $COPE, $WAGMI and $LAMBO coin he runs through swells the bag on his back, an INSIDER TIP pulls the coins in, 2× LEVERAGE doubles them, a HONEYPOT drains a fifth of the bag, a SLIPPAGE puddle loosens the steering for a second, and a streak pays a bonus. A stumble (or a car or wall clipped from the side mid-swerve, which bounces him back to his lane) spills a quarter of the bag and brings the Taxman in on his heels; a second stumble while he is close and he takes the lot; a rug or a car head-on is REKT, the whole bag gone, the course all but stopped and a roll into the nearest safe lane (a car with nowhere to roll glitches out as VAPORWARE). Jumps and slides pressed a moment early are buffered, a jump just after running off a roof still counts, and one swerve waits out a knockdown. The course speeds up with the multiplier, and each pattern is laid as hard as the public curve says the number will be when it reaches the runner (never the crash point), so the run gets busier through 1× to 3×.

The round escalates every couple of seconds: the caption ladder steps at 1.15×, 1.3×, 1.5×, 1.75×, 2×, 2.4×, 2.8× and on into the thousands; the Taxman steps off the platform shaking his fist and closes in as the number climbs (drawn only; his grab still needs two real stumbles); and from 1.3× the dev stands in the light at the end of the line holding the rug the rails are laid on. He looms larger and tugs, more often and harder, as the number climbs: a creak, and a red ripple of rug runs down the rails and lifts the frog as it passes. A heartbeat under the music quickens with the round until the bag is safe.

Cashing out is jeeting: the golden EXIT LIQUIDITY hoverboard lifts the frog off the rails with the bag, which is **banked** (the credits and the coins on separate lines), and the world keeps running under him with a short regret ladder ("DO NOT LOOK AT THE CHART"); the crash then reads as DODGED. The crash is the rug pull: a hit-stop and a punch-in, the dev yanks, the course runs down, the frog throws his arms up and looks back at the camera, and the rails roll up like a carpet from the far end of the tunnel and drop everyone still running into the void with their unrealized bag. The void and the outcome stay up until the next round, which fades in through black. A copy-trading bot (mid) steers the recorded round and any live round until the player's first input, so a bettor who never touches the keys still sees a run.

A spectator (no stake) runs a ghost of a frog tagged PAPER TRADING; when the rails go, half of that paper bag is stashed and the outcome says "YOU WATCHED A FROG GET RUGGED. FEW UNDERSTAND." The gallery's recorded round is nobody's paper: its frog stays solid and stashes nothing.

## The bag and the stash

The coins are a skill score and nothing else. A bag is banked by a cash-out accepted before the crash (a spectator's paper run banks half its bag as the rails go); a banked bag goes into the **stash**, which this browser keeps (`localStorage`, when the frame has one; a sandboxed frame keeps it for the page). The stash sets a rank title (EXIT LIQUIDITY up to DEITY OF DEGENERACY) and unlocks cosmetic drip on the frog (a gm cap, diamond gloves, a WAGMI cape, a crown). It is recognition only: it is never bought, never traded, never paid out, and never touches a bet, a credit or a round. The HUD says so ("WORTH: NOTHING").

- `course.ts`: the course in lane widths: the generator (patterns of walls, gates, rugs, trains, coin zigzags, traps, puddles and power-ups, always leaving a lane within one swerve), the runner's physics in substeps of at most 1/120 s (lanes, jumps, slides, ramps and roofs, input buffering and coyote time), pickups and the magnet, collisions (head-on, or a side swipe) and what each costs the bag, and the copy-trading bot with its own random generator, so the course never depends on the frame rate.
- `track.ts`: the tunnel projected onto the picture: walls with graffiti and billboards, ceiling ribs and lights, sleepers and rails (bent over the tug's ripple), the station, the dev at the end of the line, every obstacle and pickup, the void and the rug roll, all in depth order around the runner.
- `runner.ts`: the frog rig (a stride driven by the distance run, lean, jump, slide, stumble, the knockdown roll, the grab, the hoverboard, the look back, the fall, the breathing at the station, the drip) and the Taxman rig (the foot tap, the fist, the chase).
- `input.ts`: the controls, wired from the game's own files: arrows or WASD, swipes and a tap on the picture. Space stays the shell's.
- `stash.ts`: the stash, the ranks and the drip thresholds.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the round-phase logic, the crash and cash-out choreography, the HUD (caption ladder, multiplier, bag, power-up timers, stash and rank, toasts, the outcome) and the sound cues.
- `art.ts` and `motion.ts`: Canvas helpers and the shared spring toolkit.
- `main.ts`, `audio.ts` and the first part of `style.css` are the canonical shared shell copies. The sound is drum and bass that quickens with the round, with coins, whooshes, thuds, the Taxman's siren and the rails' boom on top. `clips.json` is empty: everything is synthesised.
- `replay.json` is a verified 8.07× example the gallery plays.

Join with **Ape in**, steer, then **Cash out** before the rails go. Space triggers the currently available shell action (Join before a bet, the cash-out while a staked round runs), never a jump: the frame says so in readable type at the station and, while a staked round runs, in a pill at the bottom left (touch screens get swipe and button copy instead). The arrows never cash out. The embedded host owns joining and credits; only the cash-out is offered inside the frame. Steering, the bag and the bot change nothing about the committed result or the credits; the frame says that too. Credits have no monetary value.

The published game uses full animation regardless of browser motion preferences. A round met mid-way (a hidden tab, a late join) settles straight into its phase; a tab hidden a moment mid-round gets its course and bag back.

All visuals are procedural Canvas drawings with no external image, font or network dependencies. The runner is an original cartoon frog.

From the repository root, run `npm ci`, then `npm run dev`. Open `http://127.0.0.1:4500/bundle/rug-rails/index.html`; add `?mode=replay` for the recorded preview. `npm run build` creates the self-contained `index.html`, `style.css` and `game.generated.js` in `dist/rug-rails/`, ready for Studio import.
