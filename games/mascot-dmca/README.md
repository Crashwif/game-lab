# Mascot DMCA

**Scrub your viral green-owl fan art before corporate legal busts your door**

As the multiplier climbs, an indie illustrator feverishly pumps out viral goth mascot sketches while a giant neon green owl stalks the hallway outside. Cashing out submits your portfolio and logs off just before the copyright strike hits. Crashing unleashes a flock of corporate attorneys serving a devastating cease-and-desist subpoena.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *Duolingo orders artist to delete viral fan art of goth mascot Lily after 30M views* ([source](https://x.com/Dexerto/status/2118108592695922778)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- mascot-dmca` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
