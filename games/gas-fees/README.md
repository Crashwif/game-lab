# Gas Fees

A man in a suit holds one in inside a packed glass lift. The floor indicator is the multiplier. Every milestone the doors open and someone else squeezes in from a fixed roster: a Chad (1.25×), a grandma with a pug, a Shiba in the wif hat, an MEV bot that zips in on one wheel to front-run Karen through the same doors (2.4×), a bro filming vertical, a nun, a whale in a suit that shoves everyone toward the middle (they shuffle their feet), a bride, a delivery guy with a stack of boxes. The load plaque says MAX 4 PERSONS, so it flashes red from 2.4× while ON BOARD keeps climbing. Each says something on the way in ("is this the DAO meeting", "i need to speak to the dev", "pizza for floor 69, paid in btc"), and each smells it at a number of their own, from 1.6× for the Chad to 8.6× for the pizza guy: their eyes turn to him first, then they sniff, say so ("the pug smells a rug", "who approved this tx", "forgive him, he's leveraged") and do their thing: the pug sniffs before grandma does, the Shiba's ears go flat, Karen glares with her phone to her ear, the bro turns his phone to landscape, the nun crosses herself, the bride holds the bouquet under her nose. Everyone sways and breathes while they stand, and fidgets more as the air goes. He trembles from the first floor and clenches in a pulse that quickens from once to two and a half times a second; his face goes from pale (1×) through red (about 2.2×) to purple, his cheeks puff, his knees press together, feet apart, as he sinks, a vein appears past 3.3×, the cable creaks and the cabin sags on it at 1.2×, 1.42×, 1.75×, 2.15×, 2.8×, 4.2× and 6.5×, a green haze gathers at the ankles from 2.5×, and the light starts to go from 5×. On the back wall a CH4 AIR QUALITY readout (400 ppm × the multiplier squared) beeps at 1.58×, 2.5×, 3.54× and 4.74×, turns amber at 2.24× and red at 3.54×, and keeps counting in K, M, B and T in a long round. A canary in a cage hung from the ceiling sways harder and harder on its perch, its eyes spiralling from 1.8× and sweating from 2.5×. An accepted cashout is his floor: the bell, the indicator turns to YOUR FLOOR, the doors open, he strides out into the lobby, shades drop, the colour drains from his face, and the doors close on everyone else while the lift goes on climbing; the caption follows the number up without him (THIS IS MY FLOOR, TOOK PROFITS, TOOK THE STAIRS, JEETED. FRESH AIR THOUGH, PAPER HANDS, CLEAN LUNGS). The crash is the release: the picture holds for 0.16 s while the camera punches 10% in on him and stays a beat, then in slow motion a green cloud fills the cabin, the glass fogs, the light dies, the cabin bounces on its cable and the cage swings, the readout goes off the scale with EVACUATE, the canary drops off its perch onto its back, the pug faints, the wig lifts, the nun crosses herself, the whale blows, the load plaque flips over to MAX CAPACITY: 69 · NICE, and everyone stares at him while up to four of them speak up one after another ("my wig!!", "I'M CALLING THE SEC", "i got sandwiched"). If he had already got off, the cabin is crop-dusted behind him: DEALT IT, DODGED IT. A spectator without a bet sees TX FAILED, GAS STILL CHARGED. When the next betting opens, the last of the round fades out over the fresh lift.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `cabin.ts`: the shaft and cables, the cabin shell with its doors and the lobby behind them, the indicator and the load plaque with its flip, the methane readout, the canary and its swinging cage, the haze, the seeded gas cloud, the fogged glass, the flicker and the bounce.
- `riders.ts`: the suit (colour, cheeks, tremble, clench, knees, sweat, the walk out, the relief and the innocent whistle) and the passenger roster with their arrivals, steps (driven by the ground they cover), idle sway, the multiplier at which each smells it, their quirks and crash reactions, and their speech bubbles, placed clear of faces, each other, the cage, the readout and the outcome stamp.
- `scene.ts`: composition in depth order (lobby, doors, riders, air), the release choreography (hit-stop, slow motion, a camera punch toward the culprit), the HUD (captions, multiplier, GWEI readout, the secured badge, the outcome pop) and the round-phase logic.
- `audio.ts`: the shared page audio (a copy of `scripts/shell/audio.ts`): lift muzak whose filter opens and riser comes in as the strain builds, his heartbeat (from `scene.ts`) every 1.4 s at the start, 0.88 s at 2×, 0.7 s at 3× and 0.35 s at the top until he gets off, milestone dings and airhorns on the caption ladder, a bell for every arrival and for his floor (with the doors), creaks as the cable takes the strain, the readout's alarm beeping higher at each threshold, a hiss and a trombone with the tape stop at the release, a gasp from the cabin, a thud as the canary lands, his innocent whistle, and the cash-out fanfare.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round (11.62×) used by the gallery.

Arrivals, colour, haze, flicker, the readout, the canary and the indicator all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. The strain is `1 - 1/multiplier` (a third at 1.5×, half at 2×, two thirds at 3×), and it is what the music hears; a slower, logarithmic surge keeps the floors speeding past and the light going in rounds past 10×. The burst is seeded from the crash point so a replay fills the cabin the same way every time. A round met late settles into place quietly: the crowd aboard and already looking, the readout at the number, the canary already down and the plaque already flipped after a crash. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the shake, the flicker, the readout's flashing, the tremble and the clench, the riders' sway and breathing, the cable's sag, the hit-stop and slow motion and the camera punch. Sound is opt-in through the Sound button.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Gas Fees URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to get off at your floor. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/gas-fees/`.

## Visual direction and small screens

The scene now introduces physical presentation acts at 32, 52, 75, 100, 125 and 145 seconds, followed by bounded recurring acts for unusually long rounds. Each act's prop has its own effort beat (the vent's draught, the fan spinning up five seconds in), which never eases his strain or the crowd's. The props fade in and out, and fade away at a crash or a cashout. `acts.ts` reads elapsed time only; it cannot choose an outcome or promise that a round will last this long.

On narrow screens, `portrait.ts` presents an enlarged character/detail view, a small overview that preserves the location, and a readable current line from the actual dialogue/chat/monitor state. The canonical shell still owns controls, round state and accepted cashouts. Reduced motion removes the new prop oscillation.
