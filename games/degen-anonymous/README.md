# Degen Anonymous

A church basement, Thursday, folding chairs in a circle, a coffee urn, a folding table with supermarket cookies, and a banner that reads **DEGEN ANONYMOUS** over a smaller one: **WE ARE POWERLESS OVER THE CHART**. Twelve members around the circle, all generic cartoons: **the sponsor** (Terry, retired, holding a cup of snacks that grows as the round runs), **the newcomer** (Jules, 3 days clean, nametag facing inward), **the old-timer** (Big Ron, sober since the last bear market), **the guy with the guitar**, **the nurse**, **the guy who sold Apple**, **the twin**, **the other twin**, **the podcast guy**, **the vegan**, **the one who only came for the cookies**, and **you** — a folding chair seen from behind with a YOU tag bobbing over it, nearest the coffee urn, the player's seat.

The multiplier is the round. Every step up the curve is a new share, a new relapse, or a new reason to stay. The big book of grievances on the podium fills with the room's top pumps as the multiplier climbs.

## Direction

The comedy is the collision of a support group's language (shares, sponsors, chips, higher power) with the portfolio's (positions, entries, liquidations, the chart as a higher power that does answer prayers, just not in the direction you prayed). The punchlines land on the player too: your own share is the cash-out. Addiction is the frame, never the punchline; the punches land on the chart, the community's jargon, and the group's elaborate denial. Every member is a generic cartoon, and every member's relapse is a gag with a comic beat, not a tragedy beat — no withdrawal imagery, no suffering, the songs stay upbeat, and nobody is mocked for being at the table.

- Terry the sponsor speaks from the snack cup: the fuller the cup, the more the room is trending up.
- Jules is a heartbeat: 3 days clean, 12, 30 — the nametag flips with the multiplier and the whole room checks it.
- Big Ron is the group's weather. When he leans in, the room leans in.
- The twins speak in one voice, alternating words.

## Dialogue ladder

Keyed to the displayed multiplier. Every share is a speech bubble and a cue, queued so a bubble stays up at least 2.6 seconds before the same speaker's next line replaces it. The caption is the last line said.

- **Betting:** Terry, no announcement, straight to the room: "Who relapsed this week? … Nobody? Great meeting, see you Thursday." Coffee urn on.
- **1.15×:** Jules, first share: "Hi, I'm Jules, I've been clean for 12 days. I deleted the app, I put the phone in the drawer, I — I'm getting a text." (He does not look at it.)
- **1.35×:** Big Ron: "The drawer is not a plan, Jules. The drawer is where the plan goes to die." General nodding.
- **1.55×:** The nurse: "Mine went up 40% this week. My portfolio. No — sorry — my *cholesterol*." Laughter, relief.
- **1.8×:** The podcast guy: "I'm doing an episode on this exact feeling. It's called 'Clean But Watching.' Nobody move, I'm getting a level." He pulls out a mic.
- **2.1×:** The guy who sold Apple: "I sold in 2009. I've been coming here since. Different thing. Anyway — the new coin is called APPLE." The room goes still.
- **2.4×:** Jules's phone is out of the drawer. He is reading it. He says he isn't. Terry fills the snack cup to the top.
- **2.8×:** The vegan: "I didn't come here to talk about coins, I came for the cookies. These are not vegan cookies. I'm having three."
- **3.2×:** The guitar guy, quietly, tuning: "This one's called 'Number Go Up.' It's about my ex-wife." He plays. It is a pump anthem.
- **3.8×:** Jules, standing, chair scrape, full room turn: "My sponsor says a relapse is a lie you tell yourself. So — I'm not telling you anything. I'm just going to check on something." He leaves the circle. He does not leave the room.
- **4.5×:** Big Ron, to the door: "Jules. JULES. The chart is not your higher power." The chart, on the TV in the corner, disagrees.
- **5.5×:** The twins, one voice: "We did a group buy." The other twin: "We did a group hug." The first twin: "We did both."
- **7×:** Jules returns to the circle. He does not sit. He says: "I opened the exchange." The room inhales. Everyone watches him the way you watch a car crash: the crash is live on the corner TV, and his thumb is on the button.
- **9×:** Big Ron, standing, to the whole room, the round's thesis: "We are not powerless. We are *un*powerful. There is a difference, and the difference is the chart." The TV behind him shows the multiplier.
- **12×:** The nurse, checking the corner TV: "That's my entry." The podcast guy, into the mic: "That's everyone's entry."
- **18×:** The one who only came for the cookies: "I don't know what anyone is talking about. These are excellent cookies. What is an entry?"
- **25×:** Jules, thumb still on the button, to the circle, the room's whole problem in one sentence: "It's going to zero and I think I'm in early."

## Suspense layer

Tension is `1 − 1/x` on the displayed multiplier (a third at 1.5×, half at 2×, two thirds at 3×), so it builds through the 1×–3× window where most rounds end, with a slower log driver keeping long rounds changing. All suspense is keyed to the displayed multiplier and elapsed time, never to the outcome.

- **Jules's thumb** hovers over his phone's button the whole round. It is the game's tell. When it touches, the room goes silent.
- **The corner TV** shows the chart, unacknowledged, for the whole round. It is the only screen the meeting does not talk about. It grows more prominent with tension.
- **The snack cup empties** as the room concentrates. Terry refills it at milestones.
- **The coffee urn's steam** reads like a smoke signal, and its drip quickens with tension.
- **Jules's nametag** flips from 12 days to 3, then to a label that reads **DAY 0**. The room checks it at every share.
- **The folding chairs** creak as the room leans in toward Jules, and lean is the round's own measure of suspense.
- **Big Ron's watch** ticks. He checks it at every share.
- A quiet heartbeat under the fluorescent hum quickens with tension until the player cashes out.

## Accepted cash-out

The shell confirms the exit; only a confirmed `cashoutX100` triggers this.

Your share: the player's chair speaks. A speech bubble above the YOU tag reads, in full: **"Hi. I'm the reason we're all here. I took profits."** The badge reads **TOOK PROFITS · STAYED FOR THE COOKIES**.

If the chart keeps going without you, the caption keeps score: **THE ROOM STILL STANDS. COOKIES STILL GOOD.** The meeting continues without the player's chair, the shares continue, the corner TV keeps going, and the room does not notice you left. A crash after a cash-out reads as dodged: **RUG DODGED — CHAIR FOLDED, CUPPA IN HAND.**

## The crash

Keyed to `view.crashAge`, deterministic from the crash point so a replay collapses identically.

1. Jules's thumb comes down. **BOUGHT THE TOP** flashes on his phone. He looks at the circle. He says nothing.
2. The corner TV goes red. Every folding chair in the circle is on its feet at once — the room's whole geometry inverts in one frame.
3. The coffee urn tips over, and its drip becomes a pour, and the pour becomes a puddle, and the puddle reaches Jules's shoe.
4. The guitar guy plays the only minor chord in the game's entire score, once.
5. Big Ron, flat on his back under the banner, stares at the ceiling: "Higher power, one time. One time."
6. The banner comes down, one pin at a time, and lands on the cookie table. It reads, face-down: **WE ARE POWERLESS OVER THE CHART**.
7. The room, on the floor, in the cookies and the coffee and the banner, does the only thing it knows how to do: it reaches for each other. **The group hug**, on the ground, at the crash, is the round's final image — twelve generic cartoons and one YOU chair, holding on.
8. Jules's nametag, visible in the middle of the pile, reads **DAY 0 AGAIN**. Terry's cup of snacks, upended, rains supermarket cookies over the whole circle like confetti.

A short blink to black covers the basement resetting for the next round. A crash after a cash-out is the same collapse seen small from the doorway, chair under your arm.

## Sound

- `audio.ts`: the shared page audio (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). Acoustic guitar and a fluorescent hum that opens up with the multiplier.
- Cues: the coffee urn's drip (`fx`), chair creaks as the room leans (`creak`), nametag flips (`notify`), the corner TV's market data as a rising tone under everything (`fx`), milestone chips (`milestone`), the door scrape on Jules's exit, the guitar's minor chord, the urn's pour, and the crash: a chorus of folding chairs and one long group sigh.

## Lines not to cross

- Every member is a generic cartoon with no likeness to any real person. Degen Anonymous is fictional; no real twelve-step program is named, quoted or parodied, and no real recovery literature appears.
- Addiction is the frame, never the punchline: no withdrawal, no delirium, no suffering, no drug or alcohol reference — the "relapse" is only opening an exchange app, and the humor targets the community's jargon and the chart's cruelty, never the members.
- The relapse is a choice the character makes and the room witnesses with warmth, not judgment; the group hug is the point of the game.
- Credits have no monetary value. Nothing in the game implies the player's own money is at stake, and the label names no opponent.

## Builder's notes

This skeleton copies the [Hello World](../hello-world) template verbatim: `main.ts`, `audio.ts` and the shared CSS block are unmodified shared-shell copies; `scene.ts` is the commented template with its circle and greeting, fully functional for every phase. The build, replay fixture (`replay.json`'s `gameId` names this slug) and recorded example are already wired.

To build the game on this skeleton, work in `scene.ts` against the `SceneView` contract documented in the template's [README](../hello-world/README.md#where-to-work): render `waiting`, `betting`, `running` and `crashed` from `view.phase`, use `view.currentX100` for the multiplier (150 means 1.50×), `view.elapsed` for running animation and `view.crashAge` for the ending. Pacing matters more than anything else: about half of all rounds end before 2× (9 seconds in) and two in three before 3×, so the early shares arrive every few seconds through 3× and a slower `log10` driver keeps long rounds changing. Tension is presentation only — never derive it from the crash point. Every animation is a pure function of the view, so replays, seeks and late entries draw the same frame; cues fire on confirmed changes only, and a late entry shows the current state without replaying an old cash-out or crash. The crash keys off `view.crashAge`. Keep `main.ts`, `audio.ts` and the shared CSS block untouched; shared changes belong in [`scripts/shell/`](../../scripts/shell/).

Read [the animation reference](../../docs/animation.md) before adding or changing character animation: the circle is a twelve-member layered crowd with per-member lean springs, Jules is a two-bone standing rig with a hovering thumb, the banner is a cloth sim on two pins, and the group hug is a seeded pile-up with squash on every member. Check waiting, running, crash, accepted cash-out, replay and small-screen behavior when relevant, then run `npm run build`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run check` and `npm test`.
