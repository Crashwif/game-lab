# Degen Anonymous

A church basement, Thursday: a drop ceiling with three fluorescent tubes (the right one flickers), cinderblock walls painted a tired green, speckled lino with a braided rug in the middle, a coffee urn on a little table in the corner, a folding table of supermarket cookies, a podium with the big book of grievances, a door with an EXIT sign, a corner TV on an AV cart, and a banner that reads **DEGEN ANONYMOUS** over a cloth on two pins: **WE ARE POWERLESS OVER THE CHART**. Twelve folding chairs stand in an ellipse in perspective, the back of the circle smaller and higher, the front row seen from behind, and every member is a generic cartoon: **the sponsor** (Terry, retired, grey hair and a mustard cardigan, holding a cup of snacks that grows as the round runs), **the newcomer** (Jules, 12 days clean when the round starts, a big HELLO nametag on his chest and a phone in his hand), **the old-timer** (Big Ron, bald, moustached, in flannel, clean since the last bear market, a gold watch on his wrist), **the guy with the guitar**, **the nurse** (in scrubs), **the guy who sold the stock** (a sweater vest and a trade slip from 2009), **the twin** and **the other twin** (matching bowl cuts and striped jumpers), **the podcast guy** (a backwards cap, headphones, a mic), **the vegan** (a green beanie, a napkin of cookies), **the one who only came for the cookies** (a plate on his lap), and **you**, seen from behind on the chair nearest the coffee urn, cuppa in hand, with a YOU tag bobbing over your head.

The multiplier is the round. Every step up the curve is a new share, a new relapse, or a new reason to stay. The big book of grievances on the podium fills with the room's top pumps as the multiplier climbs: a line of cramped handwriting per share, a page turning over each time, the page block thickening, and loose pages on the floor once it overflows.

## Direction

The comedy is the collision of a support group's language (shares, sponsors, chips, higher power) with the portfolio's (positions, entries, the chart as a higher power that does answer prayers, just not in the direction you prayed). The punchlines land on the player too: your own share is the cash-out. Addiction is the frame, never the punchline; the punches land on the chart, the community's jargon and the group's elaborate denial. Every member is a generic cartoon, and every member's relapse is a gag with a comic beat, not a tragedy beat: no withdrawal imagery, no suffering, the song stays upbeat, and nobody is mocked for being at the table.

- Terry the sponsor holds the snack cup through every share: it empties as the room concentrates, he reaches back to the cookie table and tops it up at every milestone, and the cup itself grows as the round runs.
- Jules is the room's heartbeat: 12 DAYS, then 3 DAYS, then DAY 0 — the nametag flips over with the multiplier, and the whole room glances at it at every share.
- Big Ron is the group's weather. His lean leads; everyone else's lean spring follows his, a little behind, so when he leans in, the room leans in.
- The twins speak in one voice: their bubbles alternate words in two colours.

## Dialogue ladder

Keyed to the displayed multiplier. Every share is a speech bubble with the speaker's name under it and a cue, queued so a bubble stays up at least 2.6 seconds before the same speaker's next line replaces it, with at most two shares up at once. The caption is the last captioned line said.

- **Betting:** Terry, no announcement, straight to the room: "Who relapsed this week? … Nobody? Great meeting, see you Thursday." The coffee urn's light comes on and it starts to steam.
- **1.03×:** Terry: "GM, degens. Let's go round the circle."
- **1.15×:** Jules, first share: "Hi, I'm Jules, I've been clean for 12 days. I deleted the app, I put the phone in the drawer, I — I'm getting a text." The phone in his lap buzzes and lights up. He does not look at it.
- **1.35×:** Big Ron: "The drawer is not a plan, Jules. The drawer is where the plan goes to die." General nodding.
- **1.55×:** The nurse, a hand on her chest: "Mine went up 40% this week. My portfolio. No — sorry — my cholesterol." Laughter, relief.
- **1.8×:** The podcast guy: "I'm doing an episode on this exact feeling. It's called 'Clean But Watching.' Nobody move, I'm getting a level." He pulls out a mic.
- **1.95×:** Jules, thinking it: "It's probably just the weather app." His nametag flips to 3 DAYS.
- **2.1×:** The guy who sold the stock, unfolding his old trade slip: "Sold the stock in 2009. You know the one. Been coming here since. Different thing. Anyway — new coin, same ticker." The room goes still; Terry, after a beat: "…Thank you for sharing."
- **2.4×:** Jules's phone is up near his face. He is reading it. He says he isn't: "I'm not reading it. I'm holding it near my face." His nametag flips to DAY 0, and Terry fills the snack cup to the top.
- **2.8×:** The vegan: "I didn't come here to talk about coins, I came for the cookies. These are not vegan cookies. I'm having three." She eats three, one after another.
- **3.2×:** The guitar guy, who has been tuning all along: "This one's called 'Number Go Up.' It's about my ex-wife." He plays. Notes float up. It is a pump anthem.
- **3.8×:** Jules, standing, chair scrape, full room turn: "My sponsor says a relapse is a lie you tell yourself. So — I'm not telling you anything. I'm just checking on something." He leaves the circle and walks to the door. He does not go through it.
- **4.5×:** Big Ron, to the door: "Jules. JULES. The chart is not your higher power." Jules looks back over his shoulder. The chart, on the TV in the corner, disagrees: HIGHER ↑.
- **5.5×:** The twins, one voice: "We did a group buy." The other twin: "We did a group hug." The first twin: "We did both."
- **7×:** Jules walks back into the middle of the circle. He does not sit: "I opened the exchange." The room inhales, and from here everyone watches him and the phone in his hand, the chart live on the corner TV behind them.
- **9×:** Big Ron, on his feet, to the whole room, the round's thesis: "We are not powerless. We are UNpowerful. There is a difference, and the difference is the chart."
- **10.5×:** Terry: "Nobody's holding hands yet. But keep your hands where I can hold them."
- **12×:** The nurse, turning to the corner TV: "That's my entry." The podcast guy, into the mic: "That's everyone's entry."
- **15×:** The vegan: "I'm on cookie six. Nobody's counting. I'm counting."
- **18×:** The one who only came for the cookies: "I don't know what anyone is talking about. These are excellent cookies. What is an entry?"
- **21×:** Big Ron, at his watch: "Watch says we're over time. The watch is also up 30%."
- **25×:** Jules, thumb still over the button, to the circle, the room's whole problem in one sentence: "It's going to zero and I think I'm in early."

Past 25× the circle goes round again, one share every 1.35× of multiplier (about every four seconds) for as long as the round runs, cycling fourteen lines: Terry's "Okay. Let's go round again. Everyone's shared. Share again.", Big Ron's "Meeting was meant to end an hour ago. The chart doesn't end. That's the whole problem.", the podcast's episode four hundred, "Still Watching.", the nurse's breathing exercise ("Don't look at the TV for four."), the twins buying and hugging more, Jules's "I'm not checking it. My thumb is checking it.", cookie nine, the guitar's new song "Higher" (same three chords), the stock guy selling this one too ("In my head. Twice."), Terry's "One day at a time. Today is going really, really fast.", the cookie guy asking someone to tell the TV the meeting's over here, the twins' "Same chart. Same chairs. Same us.", Big Ron's higher-power check-in and Jules's "Day zero is a long day."

## Suspense layer

Tension is `1 − 1/x` on the displayed multiplier (a third at 1.5×, half at 2×, two thirds at 3×), so it builds through the 1×–3× window where most rounds end, with a slower `log10` driver keeping long rounds changing. All suspense is keyed to the displayed multiplier and elapsed time, never to the outcome.

- **Jules's thumb** hovers over the BUY button on his phone the whole round, its shadow on the screen; it comes closer and trembles harder as the tension rises. It is the game's tell. It touches only at the crash.
- **The corner TV** shows the chart, unacknowledged, for the whole round: a climbing green line from 1× to now and the multiplier. Nobody talks about it. It grows and glows more with the tension (and, slowly, with the depth of a long round).
- **The snack cup empties** as the room concentrates. Terry refills it at milestones.
- **The coffee urn's steam** rises in round puffs like a smoke signal, faster with the tension, and its drip into the cup quickens.
- **Jules's nametag** flips from 12 DAYS to 3 DAYS, then to **DAY 0**. The room checks it at every share: heads turn to him as each line lands.
- **The folding chairs** creak as the room leans toward Jules; the lean is the round's own measure of suspense, shown as ROOM LEAN % in the corner of the HUD (and JULES · 12 DAYS in the other).
- **Big Ron's watch** ticks. He checks it at every share.
- A quiet heartbeat quickens with the tension until the player cashes out, and the TV's market data beeps under everything, more often as the round climbs.

## Accepted cash-out

The shell confirms the exit; only a confirmed `cashoutX100` triggers this.

Your share: you stand up, cuppa in hand, and the player's chair speaks. A speech bubble over the YOU tag reads, in full: **"Hi. I'm the reason we're all here. I took profits."** Then you fold the chair, tuck it under your arm and walk out past the coffee urn. The badge reads **+payout · 1.50× · TOOK PROFITS · STAYED FOR THE COOKIES**, and the caption cycles TOOK PROFITS, STAYED FOR THE COOKIES and SEE YOU THURSDAY.

If the chart keeps going without you, the caption keeps score: **THE ROOM STILL STANDS. COOKIES STILL GOOD.** The meeting continues without the player's chair, the shares continue, the corner TV keeps going, and the room does not notice you left; the asides that follow are about someone, not you ("Lovely share. Who was that? Anyway — Jules." at 1.4× your exit, "Somebody sold. I know that feeling." at 2×, Big Ron counting the chairs at 3×, the podcast's note "someone left with profits AND a chair" at 5×, more cookies for the room at 10×). A crash after a cash-out reads as dodged: **RUG DODGED — CHAIR FOLDED, CUPPA IN HAND**, and it is the same collapse seen small through the basement door from the stairwell, with you in the foreground, chair under your arm, under a SEE YOU THURSDAY stamp.

## The crash

Keyed to the scene's crash clock (seconds since the crash: the crash age while the shell shows the crash, then real time through the encore below), front-loaded so every beat lands within about five seconds and the final image is whole at six, and deterministic from the crash point so a replay collapses identically.

1. **0 s.** Jules's thumb comes down. **BOUGHT THE TOP** flashes on his phone and on a close-up of it beside him, under a flash, a 0.15 s hit-stop and a camera punch on him. He looks at the circle. He says nothing.
2. **0.3 s.** The corner TV goes red and the chart drops off its edge. Every member is on their feet at once and every chair is kicked over: the room that was leaning in is leaning away, the whole geometry inverted in one frame. All three tubes stutter. Terry: "Okay. Okay. Everybody stay seated."
3. **0.55 s.** The coffee urn rocks and tips over, its drip becomes a pour, the pour becomes a puddle, and by 2 s the puddle has run across the floor to Jules's shoe.
4. **1.3 s.** The guitar guy plays the only minor chord in the game's entire score, once (a flat drifts up off his guitar).
5. **1.15–1.85 s.** Big Ron staggers into the middle of the circle and goes down flat on his back, staring at the ceiling: "Higher power, one time. One time."
6. **2.1 s and 2.7 s.** The banner's cloth comes down one pin at a time: the left pin goes and it swings from the right, heaping on the cookie table; the right pin goes and it lands, face-down, over the cookies, **WE ARE POWERLESS OVER THE CHART** showing through backwards.
7. **2.9–4.5 s.** The room, on the floor, in the cookies and the coffee and the banner, does the only thing it knows how to do: it reaches for each other. **The group hug** pile-up: one after another, in a seeded order, everyone hops into the heap around Big Ron with a squash on every landing, eyes shut, smiling, arms round each other, and the pile breathes in a slow squeeze. Twelve generic cartoons, you among them (your chair at the edge of the pile, the YOU tag bobbing over you), holding on. The twins: "Group hug."
8. **4–6 s.** Jules lands on top, in the middle of the pile, and his nametag, grown big enough to read, says **DAY 0 AGAIN**. Terry's cup of snacks, upended over his head, rains supermarket cookies over the whole circle like confetti, the last of them landing by six seconds. Terry: "Great meeting, everyone. See you Thursday." The stamp comes down: **YOU'RE IN THE PILE** (or **JUST HERE FOR THE COOKIES** for a spectator).

Live, the shell moves on to the next betting phase about two seconds after a crash. A crash this scene saw live plays on into it as an encore, on real time, with the crash's caption, stamp, multiplier and lines and the music still stopped, until the ending is whole at six seconds (at most 3.5 s into betting, and at once if the next round starts running); then a short blink to black covers the basement resetting for the next round. A scene opened on a crashed round shows the aftermath for its age and never encores. An instant crash at 1.00× plays the same beats from the seats.

## Sound

- `audio.ts`: the shared page audio (a copy of the shell's, never edited here), in the `lofi` style; the crash stinger is a `thud`.
- Cues: a pop for every bubble (a bubble sound for a thought), the urn's drip (`glug`, quickening with the tension), chair creaks as the room leans (`creak`), the phone buzzing with the text (`buzz`), nametag flips (`notify`), Big Ron's watch at every share (`tick`), milestone chips (`milestone`), the laughter (`laugh`), the mic (`click`), Jules's chair scrape (`squeak`) and his hand on the door (`door`), the TV disagreeing and its market data under everything (`beep`), the room's inhale (`gasp`), a page turning in the book (`whoosh`), the flickering tube (`buzz`), the heartbeat until your share, the register for your share, the chair folding (`clang`) and the door as you go. The song's strums and the crash's one minor chord are plucked chords synthesised on the shared bus. The crash: the stinger, a chorus of chairs (`stomp`, `thud`, `creak`), the urn (`clang`, `splash`, `glug`), Big Ron landing (`thud`), the two pins (`pop`), the cloth (`whoosh`), a soft `thud` per landing in the pile, and one long group sigh (`crowd`, soft).

## Lines not to cross

- Every member is a generic cartoon with no likeness to any real person. Degen Anonymous is fictional; no real twelve-step program is named, quoted or parodied, and no real recovery literature appears. The stock the old-timer sold, and the coin, are unnamed.
- Addiction is the frame, never the punchline: no withdrawal, no delirium, no suffering, no drug or alcohol reference — the "relapse" is only opening an exchange app, and the humor targets the community's jargon and the chart's cruelty, never the members.
- The relapse is a choice the character makes and the room witnesses with warmth, not judgment; the group hug is the point of the game.
- Credits have no monetary value. Nothing in the game implies the player's own money is at stake, and the label names no opponent.
