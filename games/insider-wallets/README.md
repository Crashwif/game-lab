# Insider Wallets

A coin launched at a rally. A fictional candidate in a red tie and hair that does not move announces the token from a podium in front of flags, a seal and a teleprompter; the crowd swells with the displayed multiplier (each fan pops in, bobs at their own tempo and hops in a wave when a confetti cannon fires), and a wallet tracker beside the stage lists eight insiders (COUSIN, DONOR #1, GOLF BUDDY, LOBBYIST, BARBER, DOG WALKER, ANON.SOL, TREASURER) with balances that bloat on the same curve. Tension runs on 1 − 1/x, so it is half way at 2× and the first fourteen seconds carry the build. Each insider flips to PENDING at its own multiplier, COUSIN at 1.15× through TREASURER at 3.1×, and the tracker pings. The teleprompter scrolls from "GM PATRIOTS" through "MY FRIENDS BOUGHT EARLY" and "I DO NOT OWN ANY OF IT" to "NEVER HEARD OF THIS COIN". The helicopter peeks out from behind the tracker before the round and closes in; from 1.4× it lowers a hook toward him, swipes at his collar and misses around 1.8×, and hooks on near 2.6×. His thumb shakes from 1.4×, he sweats from 1.6× (a second bead from 3×), the podium leans (3° by 2×) and trembles from 2×, and his tie grows: peeking out by 1.5×, out from under the podium by 2×, dragging on the floor by 3× and flapping in the downdraft. The press pool shouts questions that get worse as the number climbs (SIR, IS THIS LEGAL? · SIR, DID YOUR BARBER BUY? · SIR, IS THAT YOUR HELICOPTER?), the allocation pie's FAMILY & FRIENDS slice grows from 51% (60% at 2×) toward 69%, and the seal reads DEPT OF DEGENERACY. Long rounds keep moving: captions run on to STILL NOT A SECURITY at 40× and THE PILOT IS AN INSIDER at 150×, the helicopter's hover widens and the crowd gets rowdier.

An accepted cashout is you leaving the rally: a register, a green wash, a puff of green confetti, you stand and walk off, your two neighbours turn to watch, and the nearest sign flips to JEET, then the one behind it to PAPER HANDS. As the number keeps running the caption climbs a jeet's regret ladder (JEETED, RESPECTFULLY · PAPER HANDS, REAL PROFITS · DO NOT CHECK THE CHART); a dump after you left reads DODGED and a sign says JEET WAS RIGHT. A spectator has no figure in the gold seat and no YOU row, and at the dump the crowd's signs ask them SOLD THE TOP?

The crash is the dump: every insider row flashes SOLD in the same frame with a 0.15 s hit-stop and a 10% punch-in on the podium, the hook is on his collar, the yank starts in slow motion, the helicopter accelerates him up to the top of the picture where he dangles and swings with the tie trailing a beat behind, the podium goes over with a thud, the flags drop, the crowd surges at the stage and its signs turn RUGGED one after another, the pie reads SOLD / THE BAG, the press asks SIR, DID YOU JUST RUG? and the tracker total reads RUGGED. The next betting window opens with a short cut to a fresh rally. The candidate is a generic cartoon with no likeness of any real person, party or campaign.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the page's shared procedural music and effects (the same file in every game). Insider Wallets plays casino music that opens up with the tension, a tick that comes faster as the number climbs while you still hold (every 1.4 s at 1×, under 0.9 s by 2×), a ping as each insider goes PENDING, pops and cheers with every confetti cannon, clicks camera flashes from the press pool, brings the helicopter's engine in at 1.4× and a clang when the hook catches his collar, rings the register on your cash-out, and on the crash rings every insider's register at once, tape-stops into a turning crowd, then the yank's engine and yelp and the podium's thud.
- `rally.ts`: the stage: flags, seal, podium (its lean and tremble, its fall), teleprompter, the candidate's rig (thumbs-up, point, lean, the shake, the sweat, the swing from the cable), the tie that grows and trails, the crowd (seeded fans that join, bob and hop, drawn back to front, and the signs that flip), the press pool, its flashes and its questions, the confetti cannons, the helicopter, its hook and the accelerating lift-off, your seat and walk-off, and the one-frame events the scene turns into sound. The nervous loops whose speed follows the tension run on integrated phases.
- `tracker.ts`: the wallet tracker: insider rows on the multiplier curve, each row's PENDING multiplier and flip flash, the allocation pie, the same-frame SOLD flash, the RUGGED total and your row (none for a spectator). On a phone it shows the latest insider to flip and the next in line.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the tension mapping, the HUD over the stage (captions and the cash-out regret ladder, multiplier, the cannon count, the secured badge, the outcome pop), the crash choreography (hit-stop, slow motion, camera punch-in), the cut back to betting, the sound cues and the round-phase logic. Decorative motion runs on its own clock, so nothing jumps when a round starts.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round used by the gallery.
