# Insider Wallets

A coin launched at a rally. A fictional candidate in a red tie and hair that does not move announces the token from a podium in front of flags, a seal and a teleprompter; the crowd swells with the displayed multiplier, confetti cannons fire at milestones, and a wallet tracker beside the stage lists eight insiders (COUSIN, DONOR #1, GOLF BUDDY, LOBBYIST, BARBER, DOG WALKER, ANON.SOL, TREASURER) with balances that bloat on the same curve. Tension: the insider rows blink PENDING, the teleprompter scrolls from "GM PATRIOTS" through "MY FRIENDS BOUGHT EARLY" and "I DO NOT OWN ANY OF IT" to "NEVER HEARD OF THIS COIN", a helicopter's downdraft builds behind the flags, the press pool raises more cameras and the thumbs-up hand starts to shake. His tie grows with the tension: tucked behind the podium at 1×, out from under it by 2×, dragging on the floor and flapping in the helicopter's downdraft near the top. The press pool shouts questions in a bubble above the cameras that get worse as the number climbs (SIR, IS THIS LEGAL? · SIR, DID YOUR BARBER BUY? · SIR, IS THAT YOUR HELICOPTER?), the tracker's allocation pie shows the FAMILY & FRIENDS slice growing from 51% toward 69%, the podium leans and trembles, and the seal reads DEPT OF DEGENERACY. An accepted cashout is you leaving the rally: a register, a green wash, a puff of green confetti at your seat, your seat in the front row empties and a LEFT EARLY badge appears. The crash is the dump: every insider row flashes SOLD in the same frame with a hit-stop, the yank starts in slow motion with the camera punched in on the podium, the helicopter accelerates him up to the top of the picture where he dangles and swings with the tie trailing a beat behind, the podium goes over with a thud, the flags drop, the crowd surges at the stage, the pie reads SOLD / THE BAG, the press asks SIR, DID YOU JUST RUG? and the tracker total reads RUGGED. The candidate is a generic cartoon with no likeness of any real person, party or campaign.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the page's shared procedural music and effects (the same file in every game). Insider Wallets plays casino music that quickens with the multiplier, pops and cheers with every confetti cannon, clicks camera flashes from the press pool, brings the helicopter's engine in past the middle, rings the register on your cash-out, and on the crash rings every insider's register at once, tape-stops into a turning crowd, then the yank's engine and yelp and the podium's thud.
- `rally.ts`: the stage: flags, seal, podium (its lean and tremble, its fall), teleprompter, the candidate's rig (thumbs-up, point, lean, the shake, the swing from the cable), the tie that grows and trails, the crowd field, the press pool, its flashes and its questions, the confetti cannons, the helicopter and the accelerating lift-off, your seat and the LEFT EARLY badge, and the one-frame events the scene turns into sound.
- `tracker.ts`: the wallet tracker: insider rows on the multiplier curve, PENDING blinks, the allocation pie, the same-frame SOLD flash, the RUGGED total, the cannon count and your row.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the HUD over the stage (captions, multiplier, the cannon count, the secured badge, the outcome pop), the crash choreography (hit-stop, slow motion, camera punch-in), the sound cues and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round used by the gallery.
