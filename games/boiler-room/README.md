# Boiler Room

The boiler is a money printer at a shady brokerage (a brass `MONEY PRINTER` / `MODEL BRRR-3000` plate on the barrel, `NO KYC` / `NO REFUNDS` on the wall, `RUG` stamped on the valve cap, `APY` on the gauge), and the stoker shovels retail into it: the coal pile has a `RETAIL` sign stuck in it. Every chuff of the engine spits banknotes out of the exhaust, more as the multiplier doubles.

The pressure follows `1 − 1/x` (0.33 at 1.5×, 0.5 at 2×, 0.67 at 3×), so the window most rounds live in escalates from the first seconds: the room rumbles and the engine speeds up, the safety valve starts to flutter and vent at about 1.4× and chatters harder as it lifts, the whistle comes in with it, the needle reaches the amber at 1.8× and the red at 3×, and the stoker gets nervous at about 1.45× and panics at about 2.4×. Rivets pop into hissing leaks from 1.5×, each with a word (`FUD`, `SEC?`, `SUBPOENA`, ...), and the boiler groans at 1.8×, 2.7×, 3.9×, 5.8× and 8.8×: the needle jumps, the valve spits, he flinches, and it settles again. These fake-outs and every other warning follow the displayed multiplier, never the crash point. Long rounds keep moving: another seam bursts every ×1.6, the gauge glass cracks a little more each half decade past 30×, maintenance patches (`SAFU`) take turns on the seams, and the captions turn into a night-shift broadcast.

An accepted cashout has him drop the shovel and duck behind the `OFF` / `SHORE` shield with his goggles down: `JEETED OFFSHORE`, then a regret ladder as the printer keeps going without him, and `DODGED!` when it blows. A crash gets a 120 ms fuse (the needle slams past the stop and the whistle screams), then the valve blows clean off with a hit-stop, a punch-in and a moment of slow motion; a stoker still at the door is slammed into the shield he never got behind, slides down it and sits there sooty, X-eyed and seeing stars. A crash at 1.00× reads `RUGGED AT 0 PSI`. When the next round's betting opens he gets up, picks up the shovel and goes back to the door. A staked viewer's stoker has `YOU` on his cap; a spectator sees `SPECTATING · NO BAGS`.

- `motion.ts`: the same toolkit as the other games: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `engine.ts`: the boiler, its maker's plate and its machinery. An exact slider-crank moves the crosshead and piston from the crank angle (the piston stays inside the bore, and the chuffs fire at the true dead centres, 49° and 207°), the flywheel drives a belt (true external tangents) to a fan, and a flyball governor lifts its weights with speed. Fan blades and flywheel spokes cross-fade to a blur, and the belt's dashes hold a speed, before the frame rate could make them seem to turn backwards. Pressure moves the gauge, feeds the fire, flutters the safety valve, pops rivets into leaks, groans in the fake-outs, and the blow-out sends the cap and rivets flying with a seeded steam burst; a new cap drops into the seat for the next round.
- `particles.ts`: two-layer particles: steam and smoke that swell and drift, sparks and embers from the fire, coal off the shovel, soot, fluttering banknotes, the words a seam hisses, and bouncing debris that fades out rather than vanishing. A new round fades what is left in the air.
- `stoker.ts`: the stoker. A shovel cycle (a scoop thrust into the pile, a wind-up dip, the swing, a flick at the door, the return) at a pace that follows the multiplier, his weight shifting into each throw, both hands on the shovel through two-bone arms, torso and head on springs; sweat and soot with the pressure. Hands and feet blend between poses whenever he changes what he is doing. The run to the offshore shield and the walk back are planted by distance. The dropped shovel (let go at a cash-out, knocked loose by the blast) tumbles, pivots on whichever end lands and lies flat within about a second, on a fixed physics step that comes out the same at any frame rate.
- `audio.ts`: the page's shared procedural music and effects (the same file in every game): industrial techno that follows the tension, milestone stingers, the cash-out register, and the tape stop into the blast.
- `sound.ts`: the room's own sounds on the shared bus, under the same Sound button: a noise bed that hisses with the pressure, a whistle from about 1.4× that shrieks in the fuse, a chuff at every dead centre, a ping when a seam gives, and the swept blast.
- `scene.ts`: the engine room with its wall sign and the retail coal pile, the fuse, hit-stop, punch-in, slow motion and whiteout of the blow-out, vibration and shake, the HUD (captions, multiplier, pressure, the secured badge) and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round (8.74×) used by the gallery.

Everything follows the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. Sound stays off until the player turns it on, which is also the user gesture autoplay rules require; the choice is remembered and starts with the next click. The published game uses full animation regardless of browser motion preferences.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Boiler Room URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to go offshore. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/boiler-room/`.
