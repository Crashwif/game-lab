# Standup Stall

**Pretend your audio is breaking up before the scrum master asks for updates**

Dodge daily sprint accountability as your pixelated avatar stammers through fake static and buffer spinners. Cash out to seamlessly drop call with a crisp Wi-Fi disconnect excuse. Crash out when your mic unmutes in 4K crystal audio while you crunch potato chips.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *Remote engineer admits to pretending mic is broken for 6 months during standups* ([source](https://x.com/tech_workplace_confessions/status/1892837401928374011)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- standup-stall` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
