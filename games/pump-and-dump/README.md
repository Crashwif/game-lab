# Pump & Dump

A bench press at Degen Fitness, seen from the feet, on a bench that reads EXIT LIQUIDITY. A shirtless, oiled, square-jawed lifter works reps whose cadence follows the displayed multiplier; at each milestone an arm reaches in from the edge of the frame and slides another plate onto the bar, calling out the memecoin it is ($PUMP, $GYATT, $HOPIUM, $NATTY, $RIZZ, $SPOTME, $EGOLIFT, $SPINE). The bar bends (and twangs as plates go on), shakes and tilts, the face reddens and grits, the HNNG, veins and sweat start around 1.4×, the crowd along the mirror raises its phones and heckles every few seconds, and the caption changes every few seconds through the 1× to 3× stretch. The spotter behind his head is the dev: DEV on his hoodie, scrolling the phone he holds low in both fists. The warnings escalate with the shown load: a **NATTY OR NOT poll** on the phone overlay that tips to NOT around 1.9× (SUS around 2.4×); a **syringe tucked behind the spotter's ear like a pencil** that starts to show around 1.3×, is half out by 2× and nearly out by 3×; the spotter taking one hand off his phone to hover under the bar from about 1.9× and grabbing it with both from about 3× (bending over just as far as his reach needs); and, from 1.5×, every fourth rep sticking for a beat at 70% of the press with a creak. An accepted cashout is the jeet (TOOK PROFITS (JEET), then a caption that ribs him as the number keeps going without him): the bar racks with the spotter's help, a green flash, a chalk cloud so big the crowd coughs and puts its phones down (\*COUGH\*, CANT SEE), the lifter sits up in shades and shuffles off to the side flexing (his feet planted by distance walked), then the spotter steps round the head of the bench and lies down to take it with nobody spotting him (DEV TOOK THE BENCH). The crash starts on the spotter's phone, which turns red as a SELL FILLED notification pops over his head; the bar falls free onto whoever is under it, bounces once, and lands with a hit-stop, a slow-motion beat, a 10% camera punch-in and a jolt through the rack; the unloaded bar twangs, the plates bounce on their lowest corner, then roll out of frame on their rims or, landing too flat for that, topple and skid to a stop, the syringe flies out, lands with its label (DEFINITELY CREATINE) and rocks onto its side, and the poll reads NOT 100% · SEC WATCHING. An instant bust reads RUGGED AT THE RACK. A crash after the cashout reads DODGED THE DUMP: while the bar is still on the hooks the plates rip off the rack and nobody is under it, and once the spotter has the bench it comes down on him. A short dip from black covers the reset into the next betting phase.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the shared Web Audio engine (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). This game plays it in the `hardstyle` style with the `slam` stinger: the music drops with the first running frame and rises with the load; the music and its riser follow the tension (`1 − 1/x`, so 0.5 at 2× and 0.67 at 3×); cues are `chuff` on each rep (pitched up with the strain), a `heartbeat` that quickens with the load until the player takes profits, `creak` on a stalled rep, `clang` and a milestone ding as each plate goes on, `notify` when the poll tips to NOT, `whoosh` as the bar falls, the `slam` stinger and a `scream` from the crowd as it lands, a few spaced `clang`s as the plates hit the floor, and on the cash-out the fanfare with a `clang` for the rack and a `spray` for the chalk (`creak` as the spotter takes the bench). Sound is opt-in from the Sound button.
- `gym.ts`: the set shared with Bonding Curl (this game's own copy): the mirror wall that shows the round's chart, posters, rack, cooler, the crowd with heckles, hearts and coughs, and the live phone overlay.
- `bench.ts`: the bench, the bent bar and the edge-on plates, the lying rig and its face, the standing figures (the spotter with his reach and lean, the chad's walk-off), the syringe, plate arrivals with their tickers, the stalled reps, the re-rack and hand-over, the seeded dump, the free fall and the landing.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the tension mapping, the poll, the HUD (captions, multiplier, the load readout, the secured badge, the outcome pop), the crash choreography, the sound cues and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round (4.35×) used by the gallery.

Rep rate, plates, bend, tremor, the stalls, the face, the poll, the syringe, the spotter and the crowd all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome, and nothing reads the crash point before the crash. The plates scatter from a generator seeded with the crash point so a replay dumps the same way every time, and the syringe rolls from the same generator after them. A scene that meets a round late (or a crash the tab slept through) jumps into place: the bar down, the plates gone, the syringe lying where it stopped, the poll at NOT 100%. The published game uses full animation regardless of browser motion preferences.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Pump & Dump URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to re-rack. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/pump-and-dump/`.
