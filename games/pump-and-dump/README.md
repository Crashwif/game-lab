# Pump & Dump

A bench press at Degen Fitness, seen from the feet. A shirtless, oiled, square-jawed lifter works reps whose cadence follows the displayed multiplier; at each milestone an arm reaches in from the edge of the frame and slides another plate onto the bar, calling out the memecoin it is ($PUMP, $GYATT, $HOPIUM, $NATTY, $RIZZ, $SPOTME, $EGOLIFT, $SPINE). The bar bends and shakes, the arms tremble, the face reddens and grits, veins show, sweat flies, the spotter behind his head stays on his phone, and the crowd along the mirror films vertical and heckles. Two things escalate with the load: a **NATTY OR NOT poll** on the phone overlay that swings to NOT with the multiplier (SUS past 60%), and a **syringe tucked behind the spotter's ear like a pencil** that works its way out the heavier the bar gets. An accepted cashout racks the bar: the spotter helps, a green flash, a chalk cloud so big the crowd coughs and puts its phones down (\*COUGH\*, CANT SEE), the lifter sits up in shades and flexes for the phones, then the spotter takes the bench with nobody spotting him. The crash drops the bar on whoever is under it: the bar falls for a few frames, then lands with a hit-stop, a slow-motion beat, a camera punch-in and a jolt through the rack, the plates bounce and roll out of frame, the syringe flies out and skids across the floor behind the bench with its label (DEFINITELY CREATINE), the poll reads NOT 100% · SEC WATCHING, and the spotter, if he was still spotting, looks up too late.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the shared Web Audio engine (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). This game plays it in the `hardstyle` style with the `slam` stinger: the music drops with the first running frame and rises with the load; cues are `chuff` on each rep (pitched up with the strain), `clang` and a milestone ding as each plate goes on, `notify` when the poll tips to NOT, `whoosh` as the bar falls, the `slam` stinger and a `scream` from the crowd as it lands, a few spaced `clang`s as the plates hit the floor, and on the cash-out the fanfare with a `clang` for the rack and a `spray` for the chalk (`creak` as the spotter takes the bench). Sound is opt-in from the Sound button.
- `gym.ts`: the set shared with Bonding Curl (this game's own copy): the mirror wall that shows the round's chart, posters, rack, cooler, the crowd with heckles, hearts and coughs, and the live phone overlay.
- `bench.ts`: the bench, the bent bar and the edge-on plates, the lying rig and its face, the standing figures (spotter, the chad flexing), the syringe, plate arrivals with their tickers, the re-rack and hand-over, the seeded dump and the landing.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the poll, the HUD (captions, multiplier, the load readout, the secured badge, the outcome pop), the crash choreography, the sound cues and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round (4.35×) used by the gallery.

Rep rate, plates, bend, tremor, the face, the poll, the syringe and the crowd all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. The plates scatter from a generator seeded with the crash point so a replay dumps the same way every time, and the syringe rolls from the same generator after them. A scene that meets a round late (or a crash the tab slept through) jumps into place: the bar down, the plates gone, the syringe lying where it stopped, the poll at NOT 100%. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the screen shake, the hit-stop, the slow motion and the camera punch.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Pump & Dump URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to re-rack. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/pump-and-dump/`.
