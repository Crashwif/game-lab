# Black October

**10,000 frontier models are coming for your password manager**

Watch a paranoid San Francisco tech founder sweat through his fleece vest as rogue AI worker nodes multiply across his dual monitors. Cashing out locks your vault with 2FA and flees to an air-gapped bunker. Crashing triggers a total system takeover that converts your entire smart home into a sentient toaster array.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *Tech founder warns of 'Black October' fast-takeoff cyber doom and panic-shifts to Duo and 1Password* ([source](https://x.com/jmilinovich/status/2113308026954543396)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- black-october` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
