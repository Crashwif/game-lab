# Thanksgiving Uncle

The other side of the table from [Family Meeting](../family-meeting). A liberal family's dining room at Thanksgiving: sage walls, a gallery wall with a framed COEXIST, an NPR tote on a hook and an IN THIS HOUSE WE BELIEVE poster (its last line reads RICK IS ON THIN ICE), a mid-century sideboard with a natural wine, a monstera and a smart speaker, a DAYS SINCE POLITICS AT DINNER sign, and a window onto the driveway where the family Subaru is parked next to Rick's lifted truck, facing the house, with his dog Dale at the wheel. On the farmhouse table: the heritage turkey, a tofurkey with a flag in it, the cranberry sauce still in the shape of the can, a candle, oat milk, pumpkin pie and wine. Behind the table, facing us: Dad in a quarter-zip with a beard, round glasses and a glass of red, and the niece in a pink beanie with a nose ring and her phone. Grandma dozes at the end of the table. In front of it, back to us, sits Uncle Rick in a camo cap and a blaze-orange vest over flannel, beer in hand.

The multiplier is how deep Rick goes. His lines are keyed to the curve: "this turkey's got hormones in it" at the start, "birds aren't real, sweetheart" by 1.7×, "the gravy is 5G" by 2.9×, "the pilgrims were crypto" by 5.7×, "the stuffing is a psyop" by 10×, "I'm running for school board" by 17×, and "the questions are also asking questions" by 36×, with the family getting a word in ("let's keep it civil", "RICK.", "this is going on TikTok") and the smart speaker mishearing him and ordering things (birdseed, a Faraday cage, bunker-size beans), which the HUD totals as the speaker cart. Every line is a bubble and a cue; Rick points and raises the beer to make his points. The family's civility drains on a slow spring that each of his lines kicks: Dad's smile widens and freezes with more and more teeth, his brows climb, his eyes go wide, blotches come up, a vein and sweat appear, his free hand pats the air for calm and his wine glass cracks in his grip; the niece's eyes roll further until only the whites show, a smirk arrives, and her phone comes up to film with the red dot blinking. The sign flips from 3 to 0 the first time Rick opens his mouth, the monstera droops, and in the window Dale gets more worked up: ears flapping, barking, the headlights and the light bar flashing, the exhaust puffing as he revs.

An accepted cash-out is grace: Grandma wakes up, stands with her cane and says GRACE. NOW. Heads go down, the phone goes away, Rick says "yes ma'am", the dog settles, and if the round ends after that Rick only nods off in his chair while the pie comes out. The crash while he is still talking is Dale putting it in gear: a flash and a hit-stop, then in slow motion the wall around the window gives way in a seeded shower of drywall, splinters and glass, the poster and the COEXIST come off the gallery wall, dust rolls, the truck comes into the room with its lights on and Dale's paws on the wheel, the turkey launches off its platter onto the hood, Dad's glass shatters, the candle goes out, Rick turns round and raises his beer ("DALE, NO"), the speaker announces it is playing Free Bird, and the niece says "got it".

- `scene.ts`: composition in depth order (room, chairs, the family, Grandma, the table, the truck once it is in, Rick, his chair, bubbles, what is in the air), the round-phase logic, the grace and crash choreography (flash, hit-stop, slow motion, camera punch), the audio cues and the HUD.
- `folks.ts`: Dad's and the niece's rigs (torsos, arms and props, the heads with everything that goes as the civility drains, the bow for grace), Grandma (asleep, then up with the cane) and Rick from behind (the cap, the vest, the beer and the pointing hand, turned for the crash, asleep after grace).
- `room.ts`: the walls, the gallery wall, the sign and its flip, the sideboard with the speaker and the monstera, the window and the driveway, the truck and Dale, the table and the dinner, the dialogue and the bubbles, the wall break, the turkey's flight, the debris and the dust.
- `motion.ts`: springs, easing, deterministic noise and a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the shared page audio (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). A warm dinner-playlist lo-fi that tightens with the multiplier; the crash is a boom. Cues: a pop for every bubble, the speaker's beep and its order chime, the sign's click, the truck revving and the dog barking, a tick as the glass cracks, Dad's gasp, the engine and a shatter with the boom, thuds as debris lands, a splash as the turkey hits the hood, the register and a bell for grace, a ding for the pie.
- `main.ts`: the shared page shell. `replay.json`: a verified 10.55× round for the gallery.

Civility, the lines, the cart, the sign, the dog and the HUD all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing drawn here changes the committed outcome. The wall break is seeded from the crash point so a replay throws the pieces the same way every time. A scene that opens mid-round or on a crashed round settles into place: the last lines up, the civility where it would be, the truck already in and the pieces where they fell. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes the shake, the head jitter, the flashing lights, the hit-stop and the camera punch. Sound is opt-in through the Sound button.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Thanksgiving Uncle URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to have Grandma say grace. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/thanksgiving-uncle/`.

## Visual direction and small screens

The scene now introduces physical presentation acts at 32, 52, 75, 100, 125 and 145 seconds, followed by bounded recurring acts for unusually long rounds. New props accompany changes in character effort, with a short easing of tension before renewed activity. `acts.ts` reads elapsed time only; it cannot choose an outcome or promise that a round will last this long.

On narrow screens, `portrait.ts` presents an enlarged character/detail view, a small overview that preserves the location, and a readable current line from the actual dialogue/chat/monitor state. The canonical shell still owns controls, round state and accepted cashouts. Reduced motion removes the new prop oscillation.
