# Blanket Champ

A stamina crash game starring a couple tucked under a quilt. Two expressive heads peek out on pillows: a red-sweatband champ who goes from smug to sweaty to exhausted, and a partner supplying the side-eye. Four distinct feet kick at the other end: striped red socks and bare feet with heels, arches and toes. The quilt has a turned-down edge, stitched folds and two broad moving rises, so the characters read as a couple in bed. A visible arm connects the champ to his grip on the headboard. Behind the bed, three rows of Wojak fans with foam fingers and signs, a commentary booth with a LIVE light, a bookie under a live odds board, and a wall TV tuned to DEGEN NEWS. The quilt's tempo climbs with the displayed multiplier and the room reacts: the headboard knocks the wall and cracks it, the lamp wobbles, the glass of water walks off the nightstand, the cat leaves, the neighbour's fist comes through the wall, the bed legs buckle, the crowd does the wave, past 10× the fire brigade is at the window, and near the top the champ himself starts to tremble. The bookie's board prices FINISH BEFORE 2×, 5×, 10× and 20×, the odds shortening with the number and each line flipping to VOID as he passes it; the TV runs a BREAKING headline for every stage (HE HAS BEGUN, NEIGHBOURS FILE COMPLAINT, BED FRAME RATED JUNK, FIRE BRIGADE EN ROUTE) and a bulletin when the glass goes or the fist comes through. An accepted cashout walks your supporter to the bookie for the bag, the shades and a burst of confetti. The crash is the champ finishing: the picture holds for a hit-stop and runs slow while the quilt settles, the heads slump, the feet relax, a puff, an arm flops over the side and gives a thumbs-up, small falling confetti for a legendary number, the fans still holding tickets put their heads in their hands, the remaining lines on the board pay out, the TV breaks the news, and the cat walks back in holding a tiny placard (NGMI, or PAPER if you sold first).

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `room.ts`: the bedroom, quilt with moving seams and a hanging hem, character layering, connected headboard grip, lamp, glass, the cat and its placard, fist, cracks, buckling legs, the wall TV and its sliding headlines, the finish, puffs and confetti.
- `sleepers.ts`: pillows, expressive faces, sweatband, sweat drops, the champ's tremble near the top, staggered socked and bare-foot kicks, and changing speech bubbles ("socks stay ON.", "bro is buffering", "was that it?").
- `crowd.ts`: the bleachers, the fans (bob, wave, hype, sulk), your supporter's walk to the bookie, the bookie, the bag and the confetti, the live odds board, the commentary booth.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the fire brigade, the headline ladder, the finish choreography (hit-stop, slow motion, a camera punch toward the quilt), the HUD (captions, commentary line, multiplier, stamina clock, the secured badge) and the round-phase logic.
- `audio.ts`: the shared page audio (a copy of `scripts/shell/audio.ts`): a phonk set that climbs with the tempo, milestone dings and airhorns on the caption ladder, a thud on every headboard beat, a shatter for the glass, a punch for the fist, a siren for the brigade, a cheer for the wave, a notification for each headline, a squeak for the cat's return, a coin at the bookie, the cash-out fanfare, and a sad trombone (or a roar for a finish you were not holding through) with the tape stop.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round (10.31×) used by the gallery.

Everything below the faces and above the feet stays under the duvet; the humour is innuendo, expressions and the room. The result stamp sits on the quilt so it leaves both faces visible. Tempo, crowd noise, the props, the odds, the headlines and the commentary all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. A round met late settles into place quietly: the board at the number, the cat already back, no replayed finish. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the shake, the hit-stop and slow motion, the camera punch, the champ's tremble and the blinking lights. Sound is opt-in through the Sound button.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Blanket Champ URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to collect. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/blanket-champ/`.

## Visual direction and small screens

The scene now introduces physical presentation acts at 32, 52, 75, 100, 125 and 145 seconds, followed by bounded recurring acts for unusually long rounds. New props accompany changes in character effort, with a short easing of tension before renewed activity. `acts.ts` reads elapsed time only; it cannot choose an outcome or promise that a round will last this long.

On narrow screens, `portrait.ts` presents an enlarged character/detail view, a small overview that preserves the location, and a readable current line from the actual dialogue/chat/monitor state. The canonical shell still owns controls, round state and accepted cashouts. Reduced motion removes the new prop oscillation.
