# BRO-MAXXED

He came to mog. He stayed for the spotter.

**BRO-MAXXED** follows Rex, a man who turns self-improvement into a procurement department—calibrating his chin with a spirit level, financing his jawline, and hiring an entire industry of podcasters, wellness founders, and AI affirmation assistants—until one sincere compliment from his spotter Milo demolishes his entire theory of himself.

## What to study

- **Lead rigs and 3-stage gaze chain (`cast.ts`)**: Two-bone inverse kinematics (`solveLimb`), distance-driven foot gaits (`stepFoot`), 8 appearance bands morphing jaw, shoulders, bicep volume, tan, and halo ring lights, and a three-stage spring lag chain (`eyes -> head -> torso`) for Rex's delayed recovery after getting caught staring at Milo.
- **Shared hand-contact solver (`cast.ts`)**: Both Rex and Milo solve their two-bone IK arms toward a single stable world point (`sharedHandPoint`) during the romantic reveal and wedding without wrist snapping or palm drift.
- **Same-rig mirror reflection (`cast.ts`, `stage.ts`)**: The vanity mirror renders Rex's reflected pose from the same rig state, and lets his reflection take one extra flex pose (`E01`) or stay behind flexing alone (`R08`) when Rex leaves.
- **Deterministic comedy director (`director.ts`, `content.ts`)**: 96 authored dialogue and crowd lines (`D01`–`D96`) with optional clean-language variants, 12 physical comedy gags (`G01`–`G12`), 16 fictional products (`P01`–`P16`), 6 twenty-second late-round background programs, 8 `TOUCH GRASS` cashout departures (`E01`–`E08`), and 8 `MOGGED BY LOVE` romantic crash endings (`R01`–`R08`) plus the `1.00×` instant-crash payoff (`ALL THAT RESEARCH. ONE HELLO.`).
- **Portrait recomposition (`portrait.ts`)**: Responsive `960 × 540` landscape and `540 × 800` portrait layouts validated for `360px` and `390px` mobile screens.
