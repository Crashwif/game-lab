# Crust Cartel

**Bake 15 artisanal pies before the hungry mob breaches your hallway**

Dough flings across a cramped kitchen as thousands of desperate foodies storm the apartment stairs. Cash out to slide your slice into a takeout box and slip out the fire escape. Crash and your toaster-oven exploded under heavy marinara crossfire.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *San Diego apartment baker makes 15 pizzas a week with a thousands-long waitlist that sells out in 30s* ([source](https://x.com/Dexerto/status/2119958405267800464)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- crust-cartel` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
