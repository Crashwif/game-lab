# GTA Living Room

**Upgrade your living room for GTA 6 before the credit card explodes**

A gamer continually drags monstrous home theater gear into a tiny living room as the multiplier mounts. Cash out to kick back on the recliner with snacks ready. Crash and the giant 77-inch display rips through the drywall and drops onto the subwoofer.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *Redditor buys an entire 77-inch TV and home theater living room setup solely for GTA 6* ([source](https://x.com/pubity/status/2114115107575775265)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- gta-living-room` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
