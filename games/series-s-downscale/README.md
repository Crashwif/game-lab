# Series S Downscale

**Keep your frame rate alive before your console melts into pixels**

As the multiplier climbs, Vice City aggressively downsamples from 1080p to 480p, pixelated blobs, and retro sprites while the console fan shrieks. Cash out to preserve your crisp resolution and walk away with dignity. Crash out when the graphics chip gives up entirely, reducing the game to a single burning polygon potato.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *GTA 6 will run on Xbox Series S down to 720p resolution* ([source](https://x.com/ViceeCity6/status/2120066507665551725)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- series-s-downscale` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
