# Not Financial Advice

A shill video shot in front of a green screen. The influencer stands in a crypto hoodie with a clip mic and shades beside a rented Lambo, in front of a badly keyed beach; the follower counter, the 1000X thumbnail arrow and the promo code spin up with the displayed multiplier, and the PAID PROMOTION disclosure in the corner shrinks by a step at every milestone until it is one pixel. Tension: the green screen tears at the edges, the RENTAL sticker peels off the Lambo, the tow truck's lights show in his shades, PENDING SELL rows appear in the wallet on his second monitor, and he says "not financial advice" faster and faster. An accepted cashout is your subscriber taking a screenshot and leaving: UNFOLLOWED, the video minimises to a window in the corner of the desktop, and the badge reads DYOR. The crash is the reveal: the wallet posts SOLD, the green screen falls to show a garage, the tow truck backs in and hauls the Lambo out, the comments flood with RUG and DEV SOLD, and the sponsor card replaces his face.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `studio.ts`: the video frame: the keyed beach and its tearing edges, the garage behind it, the influencer's rig (point, nod, mouth flaps, hand shake), the Lambo and its sticker, the wallet monitor, the tow truck, the sponsor card and the minimised window.
- `overlay.ts`: the chrome around the video: the follower counter, the 1000X arrow, the promo chip, the shrinking disclosure, the comment column and its crash flood, and the subscriber row under the video with your subscriber's exit.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the HUD over the video (captions, multiplier, the follower count, the secured badge, the outcome pop) and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round used by the gallery.

Fictional influencer, fictional sponsor, no real platform or person. Nothing drawn here changes the committed outcome.
