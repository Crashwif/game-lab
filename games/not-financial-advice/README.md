# Not Financial Advice

A shill video shot in front of a green screen. The influencer stands in a crypto hoodie with a clip mic and shades beside a rented Lambo, in front of a badly keyed beach. He waves through betting, then points at the 1000X thumbnail arrow while the follower counter, the arrow and the promo code spin up with the displayed multiplier, and the PAID PROMOTION disclosure in the corner shrinks by a step at every milestone until it is one pixel. Every milestone is also a sponsor read: he dips below frame for the product, it springs up to camera in his hand, wobbles, and is held for a moment under a SPONSOR chyron, each dumber than the last (NFT toothpaste, a hardware wallet that is a notebook, AIRDROP energy drink, diamond hands gloves, a hopium vape, a Lambo air freshener in rental scent, a generational wealth candle, a seed phrase tattoo kit). Tension is `1 − 1/x` (0.5 at 2×, 0.67 at 3×), so the first few seconds escalate: the tow truck's hook peeks in at the right edge and its engine turns over about 1.2×, its strobe spills into frame from about 1.25× and its light bar edges in about 1.55×, and it creeps a notch closer with a reversing beep after every read; the green screen tears at the edges (still opening on a slower log driver in very long rounds), the RENTAL sticker peels, PENDING SELL rows appear in the wallet on his second monitor under a KOL ALLOC 5% · VESTED: NO line, with a COUSIN row on top from 1.67× dumping harder as it climbs, bot icons gather beside the follower count, the tow lights show in his shades, and he says "not financial advice" faster and faster. His head rides its own spring and nods harder as it climbs; from 2× he sweats, his hand shakes, and he glances at the wallet every 3 s. A quiet heartbeat quickens under the stream until you are out. An accepted cashout is the jeet: your subscriber takes a screenshot (a shutter flash with the frame's corners), the chat posts "you unfollowed · jeet 🧻", your avatar stands up and walks off to touch the grass at the end of your row, and the video then minimises to a window in the corner of the desktop. The caption follows how far he pumps past your exit (TOOK PROFITS, HE IS STILL SHILLING, CHAT CALLS YOU A JEET…), and the crash you missed reads DODGED THE RUG with DYOR stamped on the window. A spectator's row is a lurker's. The crash is the reveal, cut like a record scratch: the frame freezes on the wallet posting SOLD, drained of colour, with the camera punched in 12%, the product falls out of his hand, the green screen starts to fall in slow motion, he facepalms and his head drops, then time catches up: the tow truck backs onto the bumper beeping, lifts the Lambo's rear and hauls it out, the follower count is struck through and stamped 98% BOTS, the comments flood with RUG and DEV SOLD, and the sponsor card slaps over his face. A crash at 1.00× is RUGGED BEFORE THE INTRO. The next betting phase fades up from black as a new take.

- `audio.ts`: the shared page audio (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). Trap that tightens with the multiplier; the crash is a record scratch. Cues: a sell queued in the wallet (`notify`), each sponsor read (`kaching`), the disclosure's next step down (`tick`), the tow truck arriving and backing in (`engine`, `beep`), the subscriber's screenshot (`camera`), the video minimising (`whoosh`), the chat turning (`boo`), plus milestones on the caption ladder and the cash-out register.

- `studio.ts`: the video frame: the keyed beach and its tearing edges, the garage behind it, the influencer's rig (integrated talk, nod and shake phases, a head and a free hand on springs, a two-bone arm that points, fetches, holds, waves and facepalms), the sponsor reads and their chyron, the Lambo and its sticker, the wallet monitor with the KOL and cousin rows, the tow truck, its strobe and its haul, the screenshot flash, the sponsor card, the new-take fade, and the event edges the cues fire from.
- `overlay.ts`: the chrome around the video: the follower counter and its bots, the 1000X arrow, the promo chip, the shrinking disclosure and the chant, the comment column and its crash flood, and the subscriber row under the video (a lurker for spectators) with your walk out, its feet planted by distance.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the tension mapping, the crash choreography (desaturated freeze frame, slow motion, held punch-in), the cash-out beats and regret captions, the audio cues and heartbeat, the HUD over the video (captions, multiplier, the follower count, the secured badge, the outcome pop) and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round used by the gallery.

Fictional influencer, fictional sponsors, no real platform or person. Nothing drawn here changes the committed outcome: every warning is keyed to the displayed multiplier or the elapsed time. A scene that opens mid-round or on a crashed round settles into place (no product held up, the aftermath already there) without replaying it or playing its cues. The published game uses full animation regardless of browser motion preferences.

## Visual direction and small screens

The scene now introduces physical presentation acts at 32, 52, 75, 100, 125 and 145 seconds, followed by bounded recurring acts for unusually long rounds. The props stay inside the video: each act's new prop fades in and each re-pose eases on from the last act's, the recurring acts after 170 seconds keep the whole set up, and the props fade out over a crash or a cash-out. The act's calm-then-renewed `effort` curve no longer scales the round's tension, so nothing de-escalates at 32 seconds. `acts.ts` reads elapsed time only; it cannot choose an outcome or promise that a round will last this long.

On narrow screens, `portrait.ts` presents an enlarged character/detail view, a small overview that preserves the location, and a readable current line from the actual dialogue/chat/monitor state. The canonical shell still owns controls, round state and accepted cashouts.
