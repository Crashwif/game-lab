# Open Invite Rush

**An open wedding invite brings 30,000 uninvited guests to your backyard**

Watch a peaceful backyard ceremony get mobbed by thousands of strangers dumping live goats, toaster ovens, and used sedans onto the gift table. Cash out early to flee the reception in a getaway golf cart with your wedding cake intact. If you crash, thirty thousand hungry party crashers crush the marquee and initiate an uncontrollable conga line.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *Man accidentally hosts 30,000 guests after open wedding invitation goes viral* ([source](https://x.com/Dexerto/status/2117814311728882059)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- open-invite-rush` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
