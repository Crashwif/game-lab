# Vice Countdown Panic

**Hold out 9 more days without touching grass or getting grounded**

A sleep-deprived gamer white-knuckles his controller as calendar pages tear off and neon billboards flash outside. Cash out to lock in sick-leave approval and unbox the physical disc. Crash and your mom unplugs the router while the day-one patch corrupts.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *Gamers realize GTA 6 release is under 9 days away after 13 years of waiting* ([source](https://x.com/charlieINTEL/status/2119954970799714318)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- vice-countdown-panic` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
