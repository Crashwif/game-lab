# OnlyFrens

A coin launched on a livestream. The queen sits in a hoodie and cat-ear headset under LED strips and a ring light; the chat column scrolls faster with the displayed multiplier, tips light up in purple, money bags stack on her desk (the pile squashes as each one lands), and a tip goal promises a reveal at a multiplier that moves up every time it is reached, with a new post from the queen each time ("one more milestone frens", "almost there babes", "one more and the hoodie comes off"); past the last goal, 55×, the reveal is due any second. Two things escalate with the goal ladder: a **tip menu pinned at the top of the chat** whose top tier gets dumber and dearer every goal (SAYS GM · 1 SOL, HOODIE STRING PULL · 69 SOL, HOODIE STAYS ON · 69,000 SOL, SAYS GN · 1 KIDNEY, ACKNOWLEDGES U · NOT FOR SALE), and a **second monitor on her desk she forgot to angle away**, with a HIDE THIS sticky note on it, showing her SHORT on her own coin: the size grows and the loss deepens with the number. Tension: the mods nod off, her eyes flick to the door behind her, the handle starts turning, the door rattles in its frame, a shadow gathers under it, and the front row of simps under the video waves roses and cards harder.

An accepted cashout is your simp closing the tab: the LED strips flash green, a small burst of confetti from his seat, an UNSUBSCRIBED badge, shades, and a walk out of frame toward the grass. The crash is the boyfriend reveal: the door opens, a grayscale gigachad walks through carrying the bag, the short on the monitor flashes green and closes in profit (CLOSED · TP HIT · +42,504 SOL), the tip menu flips to REFUNDS · NO, the picture holds on a freeze frame for a beat, then the feed hard-cuts to static and STREAM ENDED (with her closed short restated on the card), the goal bar drains, the chat floods with RUGGED and the simps put their heads in their hands.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the shared Web Audio engine (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). This game plays it in the `synthwave` style with the `static` stinger: the music drops with the first running frame and rises with the multiplier; cues are `notify` for tips (at most a couple a second), `kaching` and a milestone ding at each goal, `creak` as the handle works round, `door` and a `gasp` when he walks in, the cash-out fanfare with a `click` as the tab closes and a `whoosh` as your simp leaves, and the stinger lands with the cut to static, not the door. Sound is opt-in from the Sound button.
- `stream.ts`: the video feed: the room, the queen's rig (wave, kiss, glance, shock), the bags, the second monitor and her short, the door and its rattle, the boyfriend, the hearts, the freeze frame, the static and the end card.
- `chat.ts`: the chat panel with its message flow, the mods, the pinned tip menu, the moving tip goal, the crash flood, and the front row of simps with your simp's exit and his confetti.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the HUD over the video (captions, multiplier, the bag count, the secured badge, the outcome pop), the sound cues and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round (10.12×) used by the gallery.

Chat rate, tips, the goal ladder, the tip menu, the short, the door and the viewer count all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. A scene that meets a round late (or a crash the tab slept through) jumps into place: the short already closed, the menu already flipped, the card already up. Innuendo only: nothing is ever revealed but him. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the shake, the LED pulse, the door rattle, the freeze frame and the static.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the OnlyFrens URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to unsubscribe. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/onlyfrens/`.
