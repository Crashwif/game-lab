# OnlyFrens

A coin launched on a livestream. The queen sits in a hoodie and cat-ear headset under LED strips and a ring light; the chat column scrolls faster with the displayed multiplier, tips light up in purple, money bags stack on her desk (the pile squashes as each one lands), and a tip goal promises a reveal at a multiplier that moves up every time it is reached, with a new post from the queen each time ("one more milestone frens", "almost there babes", "one more and the hoodie comes off"); past the last goal, 55×, the reveal is due any second. Two things escalate with the goal ladder: a **tip menu pinned at the top of the chat** whose top tier gets dumber and dearer every goal (SAYS GM · 1 SOL, HOODIE STRING PULL · 69 SOL, HOODIE STAYS ON · 69,000 SOL, SAYS GN · 1 KIDNEY, ACKNOWLEDGES U · NOT FOR SALE), and a **second monitor on her desk she forgot to angle away**, with a HIDE THIS sticky note on it, showing her SHORT on her own coin: the size grows and the loss deepens with the number.

Tension follows `1 − 1/x` (0.33 at 1.5×, 0.5 at 2×, 0.67 at 3×), so it builds through the 1×–3× window where most rounds end, and a slower log driver keeps long rounds changing. She waves the stream in as it goes live and posts first in chat. From about 1.3× her eyes flick to the door behind her. At 6 s someone knocks (she flinches and looks round), and at 7 s and 12 s the handle is tried and let go, with captions that fire as each happens (WAS THAT A KNOCK?, WHO MOVED THE HANDLE?). From about 1.6× a silhouette gathers behind the door's frosted pane, and from 2× the handle is worked in bursts. From about 2.5× the door rattles in its frame and the mods nod off. She leans in toward the camera, chat unlocks bolder lines at 1.5×, 2× and 3× ("did the handle just move"), and the front row of simps under the video waves roses and cards harder. In long rounds the bursts come more often, the rattle grows and the silhouette presses closer to the glass. A quiet heartbeat quickens under the music until you cash out. All of this is keyed to the displayed multiplier and the elapsed time, never to the outcome.

Only a player has a simp in the front row: the gold shirt with YOU printed on it. Spectators see an ordinary simp in that seat. An accepted cashout is your simp closing the tab: the LED strips flash green, a small burst of confetti from his seat, an UNSUBSCRIBED badge, shades, and chat calls it ("unsubbed. paper hands, full bags", JEET SPOTTED). He stands up and walks out of frame toward the grass, his feet planted as he goes, while the stream carries on. The caption runs TOUCH GRASS, then CHAT CALLS U A JEET, then STILL TOUCHING GRASS as the number climbs past your exit. The crash after it reads DODGED IT. The crash is the boyfriend reveal: the door is flung open, the camera pushes in on the doorway, and a grayscale gigachad steps through from the hallway carrying the bag, the short on the monitor flashes green and closes in profit (CLOSED · TP HIT · +42,504 SOL), the tip menu flips to REFUNDS · NO, the picture holds on a freeze frame for a beat, then the feed hard-cuts to static and STREAM ENDED (with her closed short restated on the card), the goal bar drains, the chat floods with RUGGED and the simps put their heads in their hands. When the next betting phase opens, the room fades back up from the card.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the shared Web Audio engine (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). This game plays it in the `synthwave` style with the `static` stinger: the music drops with the first running frame and rises with the multiplier; cues are `notify` for tips (at most a couple a second), `kaching` and a milestone ding at each goal, a `heartbeat` that quickens with tension until you cash out, two `thud`s for the knock, `creak` as the handle works round, `door` and a `gasp` when he walks in, the cash-out fanfare with a `click` as the tab closes and a `whoosh` as your simp leaves, and the stinger lands with the cut to static, not the door. Sound is opt-in from the Sound button.
- `stream.ts`: the video feed: the room, the queen's rig (wave, kiss, glance, shock, a head that lags her sway, the lean toward the camera), the bags, the second monitor and her short, the door with its frosted pane, knock, handle and rattle, the boyfriend, the hearts, the push-in, the freeze frame, the static and the end card.
- `chat.ts`: the chat panel with its message flow and unlocking lines, the mods, the pinned tip menu, the moving tip goal, the crash flood, and the front row of simps with your simp's walk out (distance-driven steps) and his confetti.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the tension mapping, the HUD over the video (captions, multiplier, the bag count, the secured badge, the outcome pop), the sound cues and the round-phase logic.
- `acts.ts`: the late-round act props, keyed to elapsed time only.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round (10.12×) used by the gallery.

Chat rate, tips, the goal ladder, the tip menu, the short, the door and the viewer count all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. A scene that meets a round late (or a crash the tab slept through, or the next round already running) jumps into place: the silhouette already gathered, the short already closed, the menu already flipped, the card already up. Innuendo only: nothing is ever revealed but him. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the shake, the LED pulse, the knock jolt, the door rattle, the silhouette's drift, her sway, bounce and breathing, the push-in, the freeze frame and the static.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the OnlyFrens URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to unsubscribe. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/onlyfrens/`.

## Visual direction and small screens

The scene introduces physical presentation acts at 32, 52, 75, 100, 125 and 145 seconds, followed by bounded recurring acts for unusually long rounds. New props arrive with each act. Its `effort` dips between acts only to turn her eyes to the door; it never lowers tension. The props fade out over the crash instead of cutting. `acts.ts` reads elapsed time only; it cannot choose an outcome or promise that a round will last this long.

On narrow screens, `portrait.ts` presents an enlarged character/detail view, a small overview that preserves the location, and a readable current line from the actual dialogue/chat/monitor state. The canonical shell still owns controls, round state and accepted cashouts. Reduced motion removes the new prop oscillation.
