# Honeypot

A picnic by a beehive, with a sign on a stake promising FREE HONEY, NO RUG, TRUST. A bear in a bucket hat has his paw in a jar labelled `$POT`. The honey level follows the displayed multiplier, the surface wobbles, drips run down the glass, and the lid turns by itself. The label's sell tax steps up every two or three seconds through the first 3× (5% at 1.2×, 12%, 28%, red at 49% from 2.3×, 69% at 3×), then 99%, 420% from 8×, 1337% from 50× and 9001% from 500×, while buy tax stays at 0%. The bear keeps tugging at the paw, more often as the tension climbs, the jar rocking each time; his eyes widen from about 1.3× and he sweats from about 1.45×. The lid lurches toward shut at 1.38× and 2.05×, then about every 5 s, with a creak, and backs off again; it creeps lower through a long round. Guests hop over to a puddle on the blanket and stick: a raccoon (ser, is this safe?), Pepe (few understand) and a goose (bought the top). The bees swarm in growing numbers, each on its own wobbling path. The auditor arrives from 1.5×: a fox in a suit with an AUDIT briefcase (100/100) who walks in from the right, says tokensniffer: 100/100, raises the stamp over his head, holds it, slams AUDITED ✓ onto the glass (the jar takes the knock), then turns and walks out at 2.3×. The bear's phone lies face up on the blanket and buzzes with texts from the DEV from 1.3×: trust me bro, the lid is a feature, sell tax is love, sell button wen? soon™. An accepted cashout pulls the paw free: the pull eases in and tears out of the neck with a snap, a capped handful of honey flies off the paw, the jar wobbles, honey strings snap, shades drop, and he scrambles back along the crate, steps off its edge, lands and strolls off with a small jar while everyone else stays. The captions then run a jeet's ladder as the jar keeps filling without him (TOOK PROFITS, then PAPER PAWS, FREE PAWS, then SHOULD HAVE HELD? and NAH. PROFIT IS PROFIT), and a crash after it reads DODGED THE LID. The crash screws the lid shut with a hit-stop (a 0.15 s freeze, the screw-down in slow motion, the camera punched 10% in on the lid and held); the lid seats with a clang and the jar squashes, CAN'T SELL comes down past size under IT WAS A HONEYPOT (RUGGED AT LAUNCH on an instant bust), the AUDITED mark now reads LOL, the honey turns to glue, the swarm drifts over and takes the picnic, the phone reads DEV left the chat, and the beekeeper, DEV stencilled on his suit, walks away with the hive, flies circling him. A spectator sees HONEYPOT and NOT YOUR PAW instead. The crash's last frame dissolves into the next round's picnic.

- `audio.ts`: the shared page audio (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). A recorded sunny eurodance score whose filter opens with the tension; the crash is a sad trombone. Cues: the swarm's buzz every 1.4 s at 1×, every 0.9 s by 2× and every 0.35 s at the top, for as long as the paw is in the jar (`buzz`), the honey rising a band (`glug`), the tax stepping up (`ratchet`), the bear's tugs (`bubble`), the lid's lurches (`creak`), a guest leaning in (`pop`), the auditor's stamp (`stomp`), the dev's texts (`phone`), the paw tearing free (`pop`), the lid seating (`clang`), the dev's laugh on the way out (`laugh`), plus milestones on the caption ladder and the cash-out register.
- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `jar.ts`: the glass and its squash spring, the honey volume, drips and bubbles, the lid and its lurches, the strings, the tax label, the auditor's mark (AUDITED, then LOL) and the CAN'T SELL stamp's pop.
- `picnic.ts`: the blanket, the lure sign, the bear rig and his tugs, the guests and their lines, the hive, the swarm, the auditor fox and his stamp arm, the bear's phone and the dev's texts, the honey off the freed paw, the beekeeper and his flies. The bear's and the fox's feet are placed by the ground they cover, so they plant instead of skating.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the tension (1 - 1/x: 0.33 at 1.5×, 0.5 at 2×, 0.67 at 3×), the crash choreography (hit-stop, slow motion, punch-in), the audio cues, the glue grade, the HUD (captions, multiplier, the sell tax, the secured badge, the outcome), the dissolve into the next round and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round used by the gallery.

The level, the tax, the lid and every warning follow the displayed multiplier or the round's time, never the crash point. Nothing drawn here changes the committed outcome. Ambient motion runs on its own clock, so nothing jumps when a round starts, and a long round's patrols (the bees over the jar from 45 s, the safety band from 60 s) only run during that round. A scene that opens mid-round or on a crashed round settles into place (the auditor where the multiplier has him, his mark already on the glass, the texts already read) without replaying it or playing its cues. `prefers-reduced-motion` slows the ambient motion (the swarm, the guests, the sweat and the drips too), holds the lid still at a tilt that follows the tension instead of turning or lurching it, makes the tugs smaller without knocking the jar, and turns off the shake, the hit-stop and the punch-in.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Honeypot URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to pull the paw. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/honeypot/`.
