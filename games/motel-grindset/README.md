# Motel Grindset

**Dodge suspicious missed calls while barricaded with energy drinks**

Stack empty pizza boxes and tactical extension cords across a cheap motel bed as your multiplier climbs. Cash out to answer the FaceTime call holding up a souvenir receipt with clean hands. Crash and your spouse kicks the door off its hinges with a private investigator.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *Gamer rents roadside motel room for a full week just to play GTA 6 undisturbed while spouse suspects infidelity* ([source](https://x.com/GTAVI_Countdown/status/2121987444945867237)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- motel-grindset` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
