# PTO Heist

**Fakest cough in history vs. a $1 billion national productivity sinkhole**

Dial in your phoniest sick-day rasp while watching corporate cubicles empty across the nation into neon Vice City lobbies. Cash out to dodge HR's surprise wellness inspection with a clean excuse note. Crash when your webcam turns on live revealing dual controllers, pizza boxes, and zero pants.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *GTA 6 release to drain $1B from US economy via mass sick calls* ([source](https://x.com/FearedBuck/status/2112581439212708082)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- pto-heist` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
