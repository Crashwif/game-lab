# Seed Round

The first 3D reference: a sperm race rendered in raw WebGL2, no libraries. Three hundred million degens swim down a pulsing tunnel toward an egg that glows at the far end like the moon. Your swimmer wears a lime propeller beanie. Snipers sprint off at the launch and dump by 1.4×. Jeets turn back in waves at each milestone, crying. The SEC's white blood cells drift off the walls and swallow stragglers. From 3.3× a sperm whale crosses in front of the egg, a side-on silhouette against the moon, chomping as it goes. A pump.fun coin card in the corner fills its bonding curve toward graduation at 6.9× (trembling as it gets close), shows the dev's status (online, typing…, afk, went dark, deleted), grows a TOP HOLDER bar from 42% to 69% by 2× and on toward all of it, throbbing red once it is most of the supply, and puts a crown on you once you lead the pack. A live trade feed across from it posts buys, sells and every beat: DEV BOUGHT 69.00 SOL at the launch, SNIPER BOTS SOLD, JEETS SOLD, WHALE BOUGHT 420.69 SOL, SEC FROZE 3 WALLETS, and the rumours each rung of the multiplier lets out: 300M HOLDERS · 1 WALLET 69%, INFLUENCER SOLD HIS MOM'S BAG, DEV WENT DARK, DEV BACK: "WAS AT THE GYM", TOP HOLDER SENT 69% TO A CEX, MOM WANTS HER BAG BACK. An accepted exit swerves your swimmer into a glowing SPERM BANK portal on the wall, with a burst of confetti over the badge that reads BANKED. The crash has four beats. First, a latex wall stamped SAFU catches the light just ahead of the leaders, and the pack piles into it: a thud, a short freeze, a moment of slow motion and the lens punching in. Next, the camera is yanked back down the tunnel into a white-out: the dev pulled out. Then the reveal: a knotted condom with everyone still wriggling in the tip is dropped at a bin, bounces off the rim and lands on a rug. Last, the rug is pulled too, and the bin goes over with it. If you cashed out, your swimmer stands beside it all, frozen in a vial and wearing shades. Captions: LOADING…, FAIR LAUNCH, SWIM FASTER, RAW DOGGING THE CHART, NO PULLING OUT, DIAMOND TAILS, KING OF THE HILL, NICE, WEN EGG, THE EGG IS BIDDING, with SNIPERS DUMPED, JEETS OUT, SEC IS HERE and WHALE ALERT as they happen. Crash captions: PULLED OUT and FUNDS ARE SAFU, RUGGED and NGMI, CALLED IT and FROZEN ASSETS, PREMATURE, then RUG PULLED TOO.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the page's sound, the shell's copy (see the repository README): opt-in through the Sound button, synthesised, nothing loaded. The scene runs it in `dnb` with a `splash` crash, and cues it from what it already tracks: a `whoosh` at the launch, a `bubble` on each beat of the tunnel's pulse, the milestone dings up the caption ladder, `boo` for a jeet wave, `kaching` as the snipers dump, `siren` for the SEC, `glug` for the whale, `notify` for a rumour on the feed, `zap` as the bank takes you, the cash-out fanfare, then `thud` as the pack hits the wall, `squeak` as the condom bounces off the bin's rim and `clang` as the bin goes over.
- `math3d.ts`: vectors as tuples, column-major matrices, look-at and perspective, and projection to HUD pixels.
- `path.ts`: the tunnel's centre line and radius. The constants are written once and evaluated both in TypeScript (swimmers, camera, labels) and in the generated GLSL (walls), so the two agree.
- `meshes.ts`: every model built at start-up from lathes, ellipsoids and boxes. That covers the swimmer, whose tail takes the wave, the beanie and propeller, the shades, the sperm whale with its hinged jaw, the latex wall, the condom, the bin, the rug, the vial and the tunnel grid.
- `atlas.ts`: one Canvas 2D texture with eight meme faces for the heads and the SAFU print for the latex.
- `gl.ts`, `shaders.ts`, `render.ts`: WebGL2 plumbing and four programs. The first is the tunnel wall, a grid bent onto the path in the vertex shader with a travelling contraction, veins and fog. The second draws the instanced lit meshes: a travelling wave on the tails and a vertical one on the whale, faces and print from the atlas, a moon-cratered egg, translucent latex and glass. The others are billboard sprites (motes that streak at speed, glows, rings) and a backdrop gradient for the reveal.
- `pack.ts`: the race. The crowd thins in jeet waves, the snipers, chads, whale and SEC act their parts, and the motes drift. Your swimmer weaves, swerves into the sperm bank, and everyone piles into the wall at the crash.
- `crash.ts`: the crash beats (the wall, the pull-out, the reveal and the rug pull), seeded from the crash point so a replay falls the same way. The reveal keeps playing for a few seconds into the next round's betting when that opens sooner.
- `feed.ts`: the pump.fun-style live trade feed, seeded so a replay prints the same trades, and the rumours that come out at each rung of the multiplier (a scene joining late skips the ones already out).
- `hud.ts`: meme captions, the coin card with its bonding curve, dev status and top holder bar, labels pinned to things in the tunnel, the vignette.
- `fallback.ts`: a Canvas 2D side view of the same race for browsers without WebGL2, so a round is never blank. It also covers a lost WebGL context (a GPU reset, a phone backgrounding the tab) until the browser restores it and the renderer uploads everything again.
- `scene.ts`: the camera (an orbit round your swimmer while the round loads, a chase cam once it runs), composition, the HUD and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round (6.90×) used by the gallery.

The page keeps a single Canvas 2D element. WebGL2 renders into an offscreen canvas at up to 1.5× device pixels, and each frame is copied in underneath the HUD, so the integration, the poster capture and the Studio upload are the same as for the 2D games. Everything in the picture follows the displayed multiplier, and nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. Everyone in it is a cartoon, and nothing is shown but swimmers and latex. The published game uses full animation regardless of browser motion preferences.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Seed Round URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to bank your swimmer. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/seed-round/`.
