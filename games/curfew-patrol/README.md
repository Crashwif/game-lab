# Curfew Patrol

**Kick your feet up on the cruiser dash as Vice City keeps the streets empty**

A sleepy patrol officer reclines in an empty precinct parking lot while city streets empty out for launch night. As the multiplier climbs, the scanner goes silent and the officer stacks endless glazed donuts on the steering wheel. Cashing out clocks you out on paid snooze time, but crashing brings a citywide power outage and millions of gamer screams outside.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *US police look forward to GTA 6 release so criminals stay home and play instead* ([source](https://x.com/Dexerto/status/2119829956851085799)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- curfew-patrol` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
