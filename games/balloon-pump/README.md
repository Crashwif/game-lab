# Balloon Pump Test

A complete Canvas 2D reference with meme energy: a pale, increasingly worried dev in a pink knitted hat pumps his own memecoin with a floor pump. The coin is `$HOTAIR`, a balloon with a face of its own that fills with the displayed multiplier, and the crash event blows it up in his face and knocks him over. While bets are open he primes the pump with half strokes and regrips the handle, and something rustles in the bush.

An SEC intern with a WELLS NOTICE on his lanyard lives in that bush. He peeks out at 1.2× and ducks back (probably nothing), stands up by 1.5×, and ratchets a slingshot back a notch at 1.5×, 2×, 3× and 5×: half drawn at 2×, 70% at 3×, trembling as the pull gets long. The crash is his shot: the stone leaves the pouch at his cheek, he pops up with it, and the balloon goes a few frames later with a 0.15 s hit-stop, a 10% camera cut-in held for 0.3 s and a moment of slow motion for the shreds. A round that busts at 1.00× is RUGGED BEFORE THE FIRST PUMP.

An accepted cashout drops a pair of sunglasses on him, pins a green SOLD tick to the chart at the exit, and starts a jeet's caption ladder (DEAL WITH IT, JEETED THE TOP (SMART), PROFIT IS PROFIT, DON'T LOOK AT THE CHART) while the round carries on without the player. If the balloon goes after that, it reads DODGED. Spectators get POP! and IT WAS ALL HOT AIR instead of REKT.

- `motion.ts`: exact closed-form damped springs, the asymmetric pump stroke (push, squeeze, lift, regrip), easing and a seeded generator.
- `pumper.ts`: the rig. The hands are pinned to the handle, two-bone joints solve the arms and legs, and the torso, head and pom-pom (on a short tether, with a little gravity) trail the stroke on springs of different stiffness. The betting wind-up, and a fall whose landing squashes the torso rather than sinking the hip into the grass. Also the pump with its `PUMP` label and gauge, and the face: sweat, laser eyes, tears, shades.
- `balloon.ts`: the balloon on its tether. Limp on the grass until the first strokes lift it, then buoyant sway, a puff with every gulp of air, squash and stretch, the `$HOTAIR` ticker printed on the rubber (it widens as the rubber thins), a scared face, and a seeded burst of rubber shreds. Its resting size eases off softly below the headline and the gulp goes on top, so even the biggest balloon swells with every stroke; in very long rounds the overstretched rubber shivers.
- `sniper.ts`: the SEC intern in the bush: the peek, the duck and the rise, the aim that tracks the balloon (always the short way round), the notched draw and the tremble, both arms solved as two-bone limbs from the fork at arm's length to the pouch at his cheek, the shot and the stone's flight.
- `audio.ts`: the page's shared procedural music and effects (the same file in every game). Balloon Pump plays chiptune that speeds up and fills out with the multiplier, chuffs and squeaks with every stroke, zaps when the laser eyes come on, ratchets with each notch of the slingshot, rings a register on the cash-out, and tape-stops into the pop. The scene adds the dev's heartbeat, every 1.4 s at the start down to 0.7 s by 3×, until the player cashes out.
- `scene.ts`: composition and the meme layer: dusk sky and moon, the round's own "stonks" chart, the hose with air packets travelling down it, captions (a new one every two or three seconds through the first fifteen), the multiplier readout and the burst text.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round used by the gallery.

The pump cadence, the gauge, the sweat, the lasers and both faces follow a presentation tension of 1 - 1/x on the displayed multiplier (a third at 1.5×, half at 2×, two thirds at 3×), so the escalation lands in the window where most rounds end; a slower driver keeps the rubber, the gauge and the intern's hold changing from 10× to 1000×. The endurance acts of a long round only add props and pace, never calm anything down. Nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. All motion is stepped with the real frame time, so a slow tab and a fast one draw the same trajectories. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the screen shake, flicker and twinkle, the hit-stop and the punch-in, the bush shake, the intern's tremble and the rubber's shiver. Sound stays off until the player turns it on with the Sound button.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round. The backend decides whether the exit arrives before the crash. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/balloon-pump/`.
