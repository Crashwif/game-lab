# Bonding Curl

Balloon Pump, but the balloon is his arm. Gigachad, the only grayscale thing at Degen Fitness, curls a dumbbell in profile; the bicep swells along the curve of the displayed multiplier, puffs with every rep, throbs faster and deeper the higher it goes, goes veiny, then red, then shiny, tears its sleeve at 2.3×, and reads its own pressure on a cuff whose readout is a **market cap** (MCAP $636K, $1.39M, …) on an LCD hanging under the gauge. His face never moves: the tension lives in the arm, the sweat, the cracking mirror, the crowd with their phones, and the **protein shaker on the floor** beside him, which rattles with the arm's tremble and hops with the heavier reps. Past 8× a **paramedic wheels a stretcher in** from the right and waits by the cooler, checking his watch (WHO CALLED HIM). Past 10× an arrow points at the legs he never trained. An accepted cashout drops the weight (it cracks the floor), flashes green, turns his head to kiss the peak, drops the shades, and sends hearts and MARRY ME up from the girls while the arm keeps growing. The crash is snap city: a hit-stop, then the bicep bursts in slow motion into a seeded cloud of protein powder with the camera punching in, the arm hangs like a noodle with the cuff reading MCAP RUGGED, the dumbbell sinks through the floor, the shaker goes over, loses its lid and spills, the paramedic rolls the stretcher over, the girls' phones flip to IS HE OK and CALL 911 as they walk out, and one tear crosses the stone face.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the shared Web Audio engine (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). This game plays it in the `phonk` style with the `boom` stinger: the music drops with the first running frame and rises with the multiplier; cues are `creak` on each rep (pitched up with the strain), `tick` as the shaker hops, `ratchet` for the sleeve tearing, a milestone ding at each heckle, `siren` when the paramedic arrives, on the cash-out a `thud` for the dropped weight with the fanfare, and at the burst the `boom` stinger with a `hiss` of powder and a `scream` from the girls. Sound is opt-in from the Sound button.
- `gym.ts`: the set shared with Pump & Dump (this game's own copy): the mirror wall with the round's chart and its cracks, posters, rack, cooler, the crowd with heckles and hearts, and the live phone overlay.
- `curler.ts`: the profile rig with stick legs, the fixed face, the curling forearm, the bicep as a pressure volume with veins, sheen and throb, the sleeve tear, the cuff gauge and its market-cap LCD, the shaker, the kiss and the shades, the seeded burst and the noodle.
- `medic.ts`: the paramedic and his stretcher: in from the right at 8×, idling and checking his watch, rolling over at the burst.
- `scene.ts`: composition, milestone heckles, the leg-day arrow, the HUD (captions, multiplier, the market cap and peak readout, the secured badge, the outcome pop), the crash choreography, the sound cues and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round (4.70×) used by the gallery.

Rep rate, radius, throb, colour, veins, cracks, the market cap, the shaker, the paramedic and the crowd follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. The burst is seeded from the crash point so a replay scatters the same way every time. A scene that meets a round late (or a crash the tab slept through) jumps into place: the noodle, the spilled shaker, the paramedic already parked beside him. The published game uses full animation regardless of browser motion preferences.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Bonding Curl URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to drop the weight. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/bonding-curl/`.
