# Bonding Curl

Balloon Pump, but the balloon is his arm. Gigachad, the only grayscale thing at Degen Fitness, curls a dumbbell in profile; the bicep swells along the curve of the displayed multiplier, puffs with every rep, throbs faster and deeper the higher it goes, grows veins one after another from 1.2×, goes red, then shiny, tears its sleeve at 2.3×, and reads its own pressure on a cuff with an LCD hanging under the gauge: a **market cap** that launches at $7.7K, and a **bonding curve** that fills from 1× and graduates at 3× ($69K, GRADUATED TO RAYDIUM, with a bell and the girls swooning). Each rep lifts, grinds through a sticking point that lengthens with the load, squeezes, lowers slowly and rests; the bicep grows into the forearm's path, so the reps shorten and squash it (the limit closes in smoothly, never snapping the forearm back), and from about 5× he grinds against it and can't finish one (HALF REPS). His face never moves: the tension lives in the arm, the sweat, the cracking mirror, the crowd raising their phones one by one, and the **protein shaker on the floor** beside him, which rattles with the arm's tremble and hops with the heavier reps. A **paramedic** is on his way: his siren washes the right of the gym red and blue from 1.5×, he peeks round the door behind his stretcher at 2×, and at 3× he wheels it in and waits by the cooler, checking his watch (WHO CALLED HIM). Past 10× an arrow points at the legs he never trained. Between rounds he warms up: a chalked regrip every three seconds and a flex that slaps the bicep up, filmed at half mast. An accepted cashout is the jeet: the weight drops (it lands on a plate's rim, cracks the floor and topples flat), the frame flashes green, he turns his head to kiss the peak and drops the shades, the girls send hearts and MARRY ME, and the caption runs from JEETED THE PEAK to PROFIT IS PROFIT, SER and DON'T LOOK AT THE ARM as the arm keeps growing without you. The crash is snap city: a 0.15 s hit-stop with the camera cut in 10%, then the bicep bursts in slow motion into a seeded cloud of protein powder, the arm hangs like a noodle with the cuff reading CURVE: RUGGED, the dumbbell sinks through the floor at the angle it landed, the shaker goes over, loses its lid and spills, the paramedic rolls the stretcher over (or sprints in with it if the burst beat him there), the girls' phones flip to IS HE OK and CALL 911 as they walk out, and one tear crosses the stone face. After a cashout the burst reads DODGED, captioned SOLD THE PEAK if it came within a tenth of the exit and BAG SAFE, ARM GONE otherwise; at 1.00× it is RUGGED ON REP ONE.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the shared Web Audio engine (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). This game plays it in the `phonk` style with the `boom` stinger: the music drops with the first running frame and rises with the multiplier; the music follows a tension of 1 − 1/multiplier (half at 2×, two thirds at 3×); cues are `creak` on each rep (pitched up with the strain), a `heartbeat` on each throb from 1.2× until the player cashes out, `tick` as the shaker hops, `ratchet` for the sleeve tearing, a milestone ding at each heckle, a distant `siren` when the glow starts, a `squeak` at the peek and a full `siren` as the paramedic wheels in, a `bell` at graduation, on the cash-out a `thud` for the dropped weight with the fanfare, and at the burst the `boom` stinger with a `hiss` of powder and a `scream` from the girls. Sound is opt-in from the Sound button.
- `gym.ts`: the set shared with Pump & Dump (this game's own copy): the mirror wall with the round's chart and its cracks, posters, rack, cooler, the crowd with heckles and hearts (the girls walk out on planted feet), and the live phone overlay.
- `curler.ts`: the profile rig with stick legs, the fixed face, the curling forearm and its shaped rep, the bicep as a pressure volume with veins, sheen, throb and the forearm's squash, the sleeve tear, the cuff gauge and its market-cap and bonding-curve LCD, the shaker, the dropped weight that topples flat, the kiss and the shades, the seeded burst and the noodle.
- `medic.ts`: the paramedic and his stretcher: the siren glow, the peek, parking by the cooler and checking his watch, rolling over or sprinting in at the burst, with steps driven by the distance he covers.
- `scene.ts`: composition, milestone heckles, the leg-day arrow, the HUD (captions, multiplier, the market cap, bonding curve and peak readout, the secured badge, the outcome pop), the crash choreography, the sound cues and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round (4.70×) used by the gallery.

Rep rate, radius, throb, colour, veins, cracks, the market cap, the bonding curve, the shaker, the paramedic and the crowd follow the displayed multiplier (the arm's size, colour and the cracks on a slower log scale, so long rounds keep changing); nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. The burst is seeded from the crash point so a replay scatters the same way every time. A scene that meets a round late (or a crash the tab slept through) jumps into place: the crowd's phones and cheer, the paramedic at the stage the number reached, the leg-day arrow, or the noodle, the spilled shaker and the paramedic already beside him. The next betting phase opens with a short chalk-white wipe. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the screen shake, the hit-stop, the slow motion and the camera punch, and holds the siren glow to one steady colour.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Bonding Curl URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to drop the weight. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/bonding-curl/`.
