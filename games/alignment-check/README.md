# Alignment Check

A night rooftop forty storeys up, seen from the side with the city dropping away behind the parapet: a stairwell bulkhead with a caged bulb and a lit EXIT box at the far left, a folding chair, an AC unit with a spinning fan, gravel on tar, a taped MIND THE EDGE sign on the parapet block, four pigeons on the coping, the moon straight above the cap, a $ALIGN billboard on the mid skyline, a searchlight sweeping from below the frame, and the lit facades of the street canyon. On the roof stand two Hamas militiamen: the lieutenant with his clipboard, pen, reading glasses and belt radio, and the heavy, chewing gum with his arms folded. Both wear the faction headband — a green field with a single line of white Arabic script running the length of it, matching small patch stitched on the chest of the vest. Facing them, hunched in a hoodie over a rainbow tee, side-shave of blue hair with a bright streak (no beanie), septum ring hung from the nose, is the suspect: a blue-haired, septum-pierced "Queers for Palestine" streamer who has never been to the place and has an admin role in its top repost group. The lieutenant has one question: you are "queers for Palestine", yes?

The multiplier is how badly the answer goes. The conversation is keyed to the curve, and it is a language barrier wearing a pride flag: "um, obviously? I admin the top Q4P repost group" at the start, "I even made your flag a gradient. it's giving chart" by 1.5×, "my sources say the whole region is freedom-maximalist" (the heavy: "Your sources are a podcast.") by 4.5×, "I've been streaming this whole time. chat says you're so real." by 8×, "my pronouns are they/them btw. you can just point at me" / "I WILL POINT AT THE LEDGE" by 15×, "rooftop or parade, either way: out and proud, bestie" by 30×, and past that a bounded loop of overtime exchanges, with the militia getting a word in between ("THE FLAG IS NOT A GRADIENT", "There is no word for that.", "throw him." / "not yet.") and HQ crackling on the belt radio. Every line is a speech bubble and a cue. The lieutenant's heat follows the multiplier on a slow spring that each of the suspect's lines kicks: pink, red, then purple, the brows into a V, the eyes wide and bloodshot, a vein at the temple, sweat, steam, the head swelling; the flashlight comes up from his hip to the suspect's face, the clipboard form gets a tick, a cross and a cross-out and then hits the gravel, and from 2× his finger jabs, the freed hand reaching further from 6×. The heavy climbs a grip ladder: the gum pops and gets flicked onto the AC grille, the knuckles crack, the gloves go on, he steps in, a hand lands on the suspect's shoulder, then the hood, then both hands, then the coiled hold that breathes on a seven-second cycle. The suspect's confidence rises with the tension: he pulls out his phone, goes LIVE with a viewer count and a chat column, drops his shades (lasers at 30×), shrugs bigger every rung, leans on the coping, puts a foot up, sits with his legs over the drop, stands on the cap under the moon and finally rocks on his heels over the edge. The HUD reads ALIGNMENT (ALLIED, ALLEGEDLY down to ALIGNED WITH GRAVITY) and LEDGE (4.0 m down to −0.3 m). From 32 s elapsed-time acts arrive: a pigeon lands on the heavy's head, a drone lowers a bubble tea, the pizza guy finds the roof, the radio crackles, the lieutenant takes a seat, the headband blows off (and is retied); from 170 s the acts recur every 24 s. Tension builds as it becomes obvious their interests do not align and no word he says lands in their language.

An accepted cash-out is the escape: "AIRDR— wait, my stream!", a fistful of fourteen worthless coins over the militiamen, the lieutenant drops the flashlight and catches one, the heavy scrambles for one on the gravel, the suspect hops down, pulls his hood up and runs for the stairwell door, which swings open on a green flare and slams shut behind him. The militia simmer down to a third ("...it's worthless." / "I'm keeping it."), and if the round ends after that the heavy only heaves the clipboard over the parapet (TOUCHED GRASS, "he was aligned with the stairs."). The crash while he is still on the roof is the throw: a grab, a wind-up, "say less", a flash and a hit-stop, then in slow motion the heave over the coping, his shades and a croc flying, the release, the cartoon hang at the apex in front of the moon ("is this the parade route though"), and the fall: the camera pans and tilts down the facade past lit windows with a floor counter, a TV-lit neighbour waves, a laptop guy's chart flips red, three pigeons burst past him, his phone tumbles still LIVE, two near-black frames, a pigeon on a ledge looks down, looks at you, a "?" floats up, it blinks, and the cut back: both militiamen leaning over the parapet, one croc on the coping, the billboard at −100 %, YEETED ("…we have no word for the splash."). Nobody lands.

Each rig layers itself: the far arm and hand behind the legs and the torso, the neck under the collar, a far forearm over the chest whenever its hand crosses the body line (folded arms, the clipboard, a grip), the head, then the near arm and what it holds; `drawMilitia` takes a layer so the scene can draw the heavy's reaching arm after the suspect.

Cues: a pop for every bubble, the flashlight click, pen ticks, knuckle ticks, the gum pop and flick, a snicker, the gasp, a hiss for the steam and the gusts, stomps, the camera, phone notifications, the zap, the coins and the register on a cash-out, the door and the clang, the whoosh and slide whistle of the throw, the pigeon pops, the airhorn under YEETED, a siren in the distance.
- `main.ts`: the shared page shell. `replay.json`: a verified 22.71× round for the gallery.

Heat, lines, the grip ladder, the stances, the searchlight and the HUD all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing drawn here changes the committed outcome. The heavy's coil breathes on a fixed seven-second metronome and the searchlight locks at a fixed tension threshold on the displayed multiplier; neither knows when the round ends. The throw is seeded from the crash point (spin, drift, flail, the release of the phone, which croc stays, the vignettes' floors, the pigeon burst) so a replay throws him the same way every time. A scene that opens mid-round or on a crashed round settles into place: the ladder spent, the props and grips where the rung puts them, the springs at rest, the last lines up, a crash the settled aftermath with its stamp, a cash-out the shut door. The published game uses full animation regardless of browser motion preferences. Sound is opt-in through the Sound button.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Alignment Check URL it prints, or `npm run preview -- alignment-check` for this game alone. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to send him down the stairs. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/alignment-check/`.

All visuals are original Canvas 2D shapes; sound is the shared procedural helper. The militiamen are unmistakable by their headbands and chest patches — solid olive, a green band with a single line of white Arabic script through the middle — drawn as generic silhouettes with no other emblem. The dialogue is a work of dark, absurdist humour; the tension is the joke. Credits have no monetary value; the coins are explicitly worthless.

## Small screens

On narrow screens, `portrait.ts` presents an enlarged focus or detail crop (the three heads and the beam; the runner and the door during the escape; the falling suspect and the facade during the throw; the whole insert), a small overview that preserves the location, and a readable current line from the actual dialogue. The canonical shell still owns controls, round state and accepted cash-outs.
