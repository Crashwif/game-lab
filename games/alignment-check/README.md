# Alignment Check

A night rooftop forty storeys up, seen from the side with the city dropping away behind the parapet: a stairwell bulkhead with a caged bulb and a lit EXIT box at the far left, a folding chair, an AC unit with a spinning fan, gravel on tar, a taped MIND THE EDGE sign on the parapet block, four pigeons on the coping, the moon straight above the cap, a $ALIGN billboard on the mid skyline, a searchlight sweeping from below the frame, and the lit facades of the street canyon. On the roof stand two militia members in solid olive fatigues, blank vests and plain green headbands: the lieutenant with his clipboard, pen, reading glasses and belt radio, and the heavy, chewing gum with his arms folded. Facing them, hunched in a hoodie, beanie, cargo shorts and crocs with his shades pushed up, is the suspect, a degen who has never been aligned with anything but the bag. The lieutenant has one question: are you aligned with us?

The multiplier is how badly the answer goes. The conversation is keyed to the curve: "aligned? bro, I'm basically a founding member" at the start, "loyalty is a vesting schedule, chief" by 1.5×, "I sold your coin at the top. you're welcome btw." by 4.5×, "I've been streaming this whole time. chat says you're ngmi." by 8×, "I just shorted you both. 50x. liquidation price: this roof." by 15×, "gravity is just a bearish narrative. I'm up only." by 30×, and past that a bounded loop of overtime exchanges, with the militia getting a word in between ("THAT'S NOT AN ANSWER", "DO NOT TALK ABOUT THE HEADBANDS", "throw him." / "not yet.") and HQ crackling on the belt radio. Every line is a speech bubble and a cue. The lieutenant's heat follows the multiplier on a slow spring that each of the suspect's lines kicks: pink, red, then purple, the brows into a V, the eyes wide and bloodshot, a vein at the temple, sweat, steam, the head swelling; the flashlight comes up from his hip to the suspect's face, the clipboard form gets a tick, a cross and a cross-out and then hits the gravel, and from 2× his finger jabs, the freed hand reaching further from 6×. The heavy climbs a grip ladder: the gum pops and gets flicked onto the AC grille, the knuckles crack, the gloves go on, he steps in, a hand lands on the suspect's shoulder, then the hood, then both hands, then the coiled hold that breathes on a seven-second cycle. The suspect's confidence rises with the tension: he pulls out his phone, goes LIVE with a viewer count and a chat column, drops his shades (lasers at 30×), shrugs bigger every rung, leans on the coping, puts a foot up, sits with his legs over the drop, stands on the cap under the moon and finally rocks on his heels over the edge. The HUD reads ALIGNMENT (LAWFUL GOOD down to ALIGNED WITH GRAVITY) and LEDGE (4.0 m down to −0.3 m). From 32 s elapsed-time acts arrive: a pigeon lands on the heavy's head, a drone lowers a bubble tea, the pizza guy finds the roof, the radio crackles, the lieutenant takes a seat, his headband blows off and he catches it.

An accepted cash-out is the escape: "AIRDROP!", a fistful of fourteen worthless coins over the militia, the lieutenant drops the flashlight and catches one, the heavy scrambles for one on the gravel, the suspect hops down, pulls his hood up and runs for the stairwell door, which swings open on a green flare and slams shut behind him. The militia simmer down to a third ("...it's worthless." / "I'm keeping it."), and if the round ends after that the heavy only heaves the clipboard over the parapet (TOUCHED GRASS, "he was aligned with the stairs."). The crash while he is still on the roof is the throw: a grab, a wind-up, "say less", a flash and a hit-stop, then in slow motion the heave over the coping, his shades and a croc flying, the release, the cartoon hang at the apex in front of the moon ("ok this is actually a great view"), and the fall: the camera pans and tilts down the facade past lit windows with a floor counter, a TV-lit neighbour waves, a laptop guy's chart flips red, three pigeons burst past him, his beanie floats away and his phone tumbles still LIVE, two near-black frames, a pigeon on a ledge looks down, looks at you, a "?" floats up, it blinks, and the cut back: both militia leaning over the parapet, one croc on the coping, the billboard at −100 %, YEETED. Nobody lands.

- `scene.ts`: composition in depth order (sky, skylines, searchlight, street and facades, roof back, rigs, beam, roof front, props in the air, acts, bubbles), the round-phase logic, the clocks (hit-stop, slow motion, the escape, the throw, the harmless heave), the camera (parallax pan and tilt, shake, punch), every audio cue, the HUD and the portrait hand-off.
- `rigs.ts`: the three rigs on one shared 15-segment skeleton (the lieutenant, the heavy, the suspect), the heat face, the grip ladder, the stances, the props by rung, the escape run, the seeded throw and the aftermath poses.
- `roof.ts`: the roof set (bulkhead, door, EXIT box, bulb, chair, AC unit, parapet, sign), the coping pigeons, the loose props and their seeded landings, the wind streaks, the escape wash and the flash.
- `city.ts`: the sky and moon, the far and mid skylines with the $ALIGN billboard, the searchlight, the canyon and the across-the-street building, the two cached facade strips the fall scrolls past with their vignettes and the pigeon burst, the camera model and the pigeon-eye insert.
- `lines.ts`: the dialogue ladder, the extras, the HQ radio, the overtime loop, the escape and aftermath sets, the caption and readout tables, the chat messages, the speech bubbles and the meme lettering.
- `acts.ts`: the elapsed-time presentation acts and their props.
- `motion.ts`, `kinematics.ts`, `portrait.ts`: springs, easing, deterministic noise and a seeded generator; limb solving and foot cycles; the portrait compositor.
- `audio.ts`: the shared page audio (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). Drift phonk that tightens with the multiplier; the crash is a record scratch. Cues: a pop for every bubble, the flashlight click, pen ticks, knuckle ticks, the gum pop and flick, a snicker, the gasp, a hiss for the steam and the gusts, stomps, the camera, phone notifications, the zap, the coins and the register on a cash-out, the door and the clang, the whoosh and slide whistle of the throw, the pigeon pops, the airhorn under YEETED, a siren in the distance.
- `main.ts`: the shared page shell. `replay.json`: a verified 22.71× round for the gallery.

Heat, lines, the grip ladder, the stances, the searchlight and the HUD all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing drawn here changes the committed outcome. The heavy's coil breathes on a fixed seven-second metronome and the searchlight locks at a fixed tension threshold on the displayed multiplier; neither knows when the round ends. The throw is seeded from the crash point (spin, drift, flail, the release of the beanie and the phone, which croc stays, the vignettes' floors, the pigeon burst) so a replay throws him the same way every time. A scene that opens mid-round or on a crashed round settles into place: the ladder spent, the props and grips where the rung puts them, the springs at rest, the last lines up, a crash the settled aftermath with its stamp, a cash-out the shut door. The published game uses full animation regardless of browser motion preferences. Sound is opt-in through the Sound button.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Alignment Check URL it prints, or `npm run preview -- alignment-check` for this game alone. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to send him down the stairs. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/alignment-check/`.

All visuals are original Canvas 2D shapes; sound is the shared procedural helper. The militia are generic: plain green headbands with nothing on them, solid olive, blank vests, no emblem, no real organisation. Credits have no monetary value; the coins are explicitly worthless.

## Small screens

On narrow screens, `portrait.ts` presents an enlarged focus or detail crop (the three heads and the beam; the runner and the door during the escape; the falling suspect and the facade during the throw; the whole insert), a small overview that preserves the location, and a readable current line from the actual dialogue. The canonical shell still owns controls, round state and accepted cash-outs.
