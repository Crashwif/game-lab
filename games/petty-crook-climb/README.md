# Petty Crook Climb

**Steal loose change and rusty mopeds before the Vice PD busts your hustle**

Two broke stick-up rookies scrounge convenience store registers while eyeing luxury yachts across Vice City. Cash out in time to peel out in a smoking sedan with a trunk full of rolls of quarters. Crash and an undercover cruiser clips your getaway bicycle into a display rack of cheap flip-flops.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *GTA 6 reportedly starts Jason and Lucia as broke petty thieves locked out of supercars* ([source](https://x.com/Dexerto/status/2121724473137352759)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- petty-crook-climb` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
