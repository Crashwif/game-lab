# MAFO

**Meth Around and Find Out.** *Cookin' with Junior* is a cooking show shot at night in the kitchen of a single-wide trailer, seen in cutaway with the kitchen's front wall off. Behind a harvest-gold counter stands **Junior**: a trucker cap that says CHEMIST, swim goggles, a KISS THE COOK apron over a stained tank top, yellow dish gloves, a mullet, a horseshoe moustache and a cigarette behind his ear. He stirs his Mamaw's turkey fryer with a canoe paddle. The fryer sits on a burner stand. Its lid is bungeed down and duct-taped at the seam, and stuck through it are a funnel, a jiggler valve, a copper coil that drips blue rock candy into a mason jar, and a pressure gauge on a pipe. The first gauge is a tire gauge. The jugs on the counter say XXX and MYSTERY, his beer is in a koozie, and the propane tank on the floor says FLAMMABLE · NO SMOKING. In a webbed lawn chair at the left of the kitchen, with a beer and a cooler, sits **Cuz**, his cousin. Cuz is the player.

Around them:

- **The kitchen:**
  - An avocado fridge with Mamaw's photo and the recipe on a napkin. A possum lives on top.
  - A feed store calendar still on March, and a mounted bass over the chair.
  - Christmas lights up since 2009, a bare bulb, gingham curtains and a box fan in the window.
- **Across the road**, through that window: Miss Darlene's trailer with her TV on.
- **Behind their front walls:**
  - The living room: the couch, the TV, Mamaw's portrait and a deer head.
  - The bathroom: the toilet and the water heater.
- **The front yard:** the pickup, a kiddie pool, a tree, the satellite dish, a tire, and a hound asleep by the truck.

The multiplier is the cook. Every step up the curve:

- Junior adds something to the fryer.
- The brew changes colour, from creek water to country blue and on past it.
- The gauge pins and he swaps it for a bigger one.
- The jars fill and the trailer rocks.
- The neighbourhood calls the sheriff.
- Cuz armours up in his chair.
- The whole place glows brighter.

## Direction

The comedy is a man with total confidence and no idea, hosting a cooking show about something he should not be cooking, in his grandmother's turkey fryer, while everyone around him can see where it is going. Every step of his "recipe" is a bad decision announced as a technique.

- **Junior never worries. He explains.** Every warning sign gets a confident, wrong explanation:
  - "It's a candle."
  - "It's a night light."
  - "It's a science project."
  - "Northern lights, Mamaw."
- **The gauge is the only honest instrument in the trailer.** He keeps replacing it because it keeps telling him the truth.
- **Cuz is the voice of reason, and he keeps sitting there.** He reports what he sees: the walls are breathing, the possum is praying, his fillings are humming. He armours up instead of leaving. Leaving is the cash-out: he goes to get ice.
- **The outside world can see the glow and nobody can stop it.**
  - Miss Darlene across the road.
  - Sheriff Boyd on his bullhorn, who decides to wait in the car.
  - Mamaw on speaker from bingo.
- **The title is the joke and the ending.** You meth around and you find out. The game is a cartoon of a bad idea, not a guide to one (see [Lines not to cross](#lines-not-to-cross)).

## Dialogue ladder

Lines are keyed to the displayed multiplier, never to the crash point.

- **Bubbles:** each line is a speech bubble beside its speaker. Darlene's, the sheriff's and Mamaw's carry the speaker's name and where they are calling from.
- **Queue:** lines are queued so each bubble stays up at least 2.6 s and a beat passes between lines. Half of all rounds end before 2×, so the first steps come a second or two apart.
- **Beats:** a `beat` stages a prop or a gesture when its line is said. If a fast curve overfills the queue, asides drop out first.
- **Caption:** the caption is the last captioned line said.

## Suspense layer

Tension is `1 − 1/x` on the displayed multiplier: a third at 1.5×, half at 2×, two thirds at 3×. A slower log driver, `log10(x) / 3`, carries what keeps growing in long rounds. All of it follows the displayed multiplier and the round clock, never the outcome.

- **The brew:**
  - The sight tube on the fryer, the seam and the glow over the kitchen change colour through the shades the SHADE readout names: CREEK WATER, SWAMP, PICKLE JUICE, ANTIFREEZE, TEAL and SKY.
  - It reaches COUNTRY BLUE at 4.5×, then goes ELECTRIC, NUCLEAR, ALIEN, HEAVENLY, GOD'S OWN BLUE, BLUE 2: BLUER and BEYOND BLUE.
  - The glow grows from the pot and spills onto the yard when the camera is outside.
- **The pressure:**
  - The readout shows PSI = 15 × the multiplier. The needle climbs its dial and pins at the top.
  - Each gauge reads ten times the last: 60 on the tire gauge, 600 on the water heater's, then the submarine, NASA, volcano and god gauges, then GOD ×10, GOD ×100, and so on. Each one is a size bigger, cracks as it nears its limit and is swapped every tenfold.
  - The jiggler rattles faster, the lid domes and rattles, the seam glows and steam puffs come faster.
  - A whistle comes in around 2× and climbs.
- **The jars:**
  - A little over two jars of blue rock candy drip from the coil per doubling of the multiplier (`2.2 · log2 x`).
  - Each finished jar goes up on the shelf, with a marker tally past seven.
- **Junior:**
  - His grin widens, he flushes and he sweats from about 1.7×.
  - Past 2× he wipes his brow every few seconds.
  - Past 2.5× he glances at the gauge.
  - Between lines he sips his beer, and he looks at whoever is talking.
- **The trailer:**
  - It rocks on its blocks harder and faster with the tension, creaks past about 2.2× and lurches at each milestone.
  - The bulb swings and, past 2.5×, flickers.
  - The mounted bass starts to sing between 2.2× and 4×.
  - Past 100× the whole trailer floats off its blocks.
- **Cuz armours up:** sunglasses at 2×, oven mitts at 3.2×, a motorcycle helmet at 6×, and a fire extinguisher in his lap at 15×.
- **The neighbourhood:**
  - Darlene goes from her TV to her window, then her porch, then a lawn chair with popcorn.
  - The sheriff's lights wash the kitchen red and blue.
  - The saucer hovers from 62×.
  - The possum faints on cue and stays fainted from 10×.
- **The readouts:** for example, PSI 136 · WATER HEATER GAUGE and SHADE: ELECTRIC · 6 JARS.

## Accepted cash-out

Only a confirmed `cashoutX100` triggers this.

1. Cuz: "I'm gonna go get ice." He stands, picks up the cooler, walks out through the living room and the front door, goes down the steps and crouches behind the pickup.
2. Footsteps and the door play as cues. The camera pulls out from the kitchen to the yard, with the truck on the left and the kitchen in cutaway on the right.
3. Junior, a moment later: "Cuz? CUZ! ...Fine. More purity for me."
4. The badge reads **WENT TO GET ICE**, with the exit multiplier and the credits. The tag over Cuz reads YOU · OUT.
5. The caption alternates WENT TO GET ICE and OUTSIDE, BEHIND THE TRUCK. From twice your exit it reads THE COOK GOES ON WITHOUT YOU.

If the round keeps going without you, the regret lines come at 1.4×, 2×, 3×, 5× and 10× past your exit:

- "Cuz is missin' the best part!"
- "Ice don't take this long, Cuz!"
- Darlene: "SMART BOY!"
- "CUZ! It's a hundred percent now! You're missin' history!"
- The sheriff: "SON, YOU DID THE RIGHT THING."

A crash after a sale reads as dodged:

- Cuz ducks behind the truck while the blast goes over him.
- He says "Got the ice." and his tag reads YOU · SAFE.
- The caption is **GOT OUT — AND GOT THE ICE** and the stamp is **GOT OUT**.

## The crash

The trailer finds out. Everything runs on the scene's crash clock, carried on through the encore below.

- **Seeds:** the crash is deterministic from the crash point, which the scene uses only once the crash has happened.
  - `mulberry32(x100 · 13 + 7)` picks the last bad idea, the landing, where the chair comes down and Junior's line.
  - `· 7919 + 17` lays out the blast.
- **Replays:** a replay, a late entry and a live viewer all see the same crash.
- **Timing:** times below are real seconds after the crash, in a live hold that moves on to the next betting phase two seconds after the crash.

1. **0 s, the record scratch.** The music, the boil and the whistle stop dead. The frame freezes for a tenth of a second and the camera punches in on Junior. The caption names the last bad idea.
2. **0.1 s, the last bad idea.** It is one of four, seeded:
   - **Hold my beer:** "Cuz. Hold my beer." He holds the can out to his cousin.
   - **A cigarette:** "Just a quick one." He takes the cigarette from behind his ear and flicks his lighter. The tank on the floor says NO SMOKING.
   - **A sneeze:** "Ah... ahh... CHOO!"
   - **A tap on the gauge:** "Huh." Two taps, and the glass cracks.

   A crash under 1.05× is the burner itself: "Whoops."
3. **0.5 s, the cut outside.** The camera shows the trailer from the yard, at night and quiet. A cricket chirps twice. The trailer swells.
4. **0.65 s, the blast.** A hit-stop and a white flash, then a beat of slow motion. Everything goes at once:
   - A fireball of billowing puffs and a shockwave across the yard.
   - A mushroom of smoke in the brew's colour.
   - The walls falling out and back.
   - The roof going straight up, flipping over and coming down flat on its back behind the deck.
   - The fridge flying into the yard and landing upright, with the possum still playing dead on top.
   - The pot, the lid, the paddle, the gauge, the TV, the couch, the bass, the box fan, the jugs, beer cans, planks, siding, pink insulation and a toilet roll unrolling.
   - A glitter of blue rock candy.
   - The propane tank going up like a rocket and becoming a star.
5. **About 2.5 s:** Cuz, unless he went for ice, has flown across the yard still in his lawn chair. He comes down upright in one of three seeded spots.
6. **About 2.6–2.8 s:** Junior comes down on a seeded arc into the kiddie pool, the truck bed or the tree, where he hangs from a branch. Past 70× the saucer takes him instead. He goes up its beam ("TELL MAMAW I'LL BE LATE!") and it leaves.
7. **Once he is down,** the camera pushes in on the wreck, where he landed and where Cuz is.
   - Junior sits up, coughs, and his cap floats down onto his head.
   - **Cuz:** "...I'm gonna go get ice."
   - **Junior**, one of: "...Nailed it.", "...Mamaw's gonna kill me." or "...Same time next week."
8. **About 4.4 s:** the stamp lands, and the sheriff's siren starts.
   - **FOUND OUT** if you held.
   - **GOT OUT** if you went for ice.
   - **JUST WATCHIN'** if you watched.

   Then come Miss Darlene, phone up and filming from her porch ("I GOT IT ON VIDEO!"), and the possum, who rolls onto its feet, hops off the fridge and walks away unhurt. Only the scorched deck, small fires and the toilet are left standing.

Each line in the aftermath waits for the camera to settle and comes a beat after the last, so the aftermath speaks in order and every line is placed where the stamp will not cover it.

**The encore.** If the next betting phase arrives while the ending is still playing, and this scene saw the crash live, the ending plays on as an encore on the scene's own clock:

- It runs at most 3.5 s, until the ending is complete at 5.4 s, and is cut short if the next round starts running.
- The crash caption, the stamp, the crash multiplier and the crashed music hold, and the lines and cues continue.
- A short blink to black then covers the trailer reappearing for the next show.

A crash met late, or on a first frame, settles into its final image with no cues and the quiet crash.

## Lines not to cross

- **No recipe or method.** Nothing in the game is a recipe, a method or an equipment list, and nothing Junior does would make anything.
  - The ingredients are a jug marked XXX and a jug marked MYSTERY.
  - The product is "blue rock candy".
  - The apparatus is a turkey fryer, a canoe paddle and a tire gauge.
  - No real substance, chemical, quantity, temperature or procedure is named or shown.
- **The joke is on the cook.** Every decision he makes is wrong, and the trailer blows up. The game rewards leaving, never staying, and nothing in it presents the activity as a good idea, a living or a way to make money.
- **Slapstick, not injury.** Nobody is hurt in a way the game shows:
  - Junior lands in the pool, the truck or the tree, coughs, and has a line.
  - Cuz comes down upright in his chair.
  - The possum is playing dead and walks off.

  There is no gore, no arrest and no violence, and the sheriff waits in the car.
- **Generic cartoons only.** Junior, Cuz, Miss Darlene, Sheriff Boyd and Mamaw have no likeness to any real person, show, brand or place.
- **Credits have no monetary value.** Nothing in the game implies the player's own money is at stake, and going to get ice is the whole reward for leaving.

**On narrow screens**, `portrait.ts` presents a close-up of the kitchen, the whole frame once Cuz has gone or the trailer has, a small overview and the current line.

Run `npm run preview -- mafo` from the repository root and open the printed URL to watch the recorded 9.07× round. To play live against the local emulator, drop `?mode=replay` from the URL, join with 50 local credits, and cash out during a running round to go get ice.

All visuals are original Canvas 2D shapes and text. Sound uses the repository's shared procedural helper and the game's own procedural boil, roar and whistle (`clips.json` is `{}`). Junior, Cuz, Miss Darlene, Sheriff Boyd and Mamaw are fictional. Credits have no monetary value. No third-party assets are included. The game is an open remix origin (`gallery.json`).
