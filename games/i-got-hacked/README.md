# I Got Hacked

A washed celebrity launches a coin from a mansion balcony. A fictional star in a bathrobe and shades types the launch post on a gold phone; the pool party below fills with fans on the multiplier curve, the follower ticker spins, the manager whispers in his ear and the yacht in the bay gets bigger. Tension runs on `1 - 1/x` (0.33 at 1.5×, 0.5 at 2×, 0.67 at 3×), so the first 15 seconds, where most rounds end, escalate all the way: the first whisper at 1.5 s and faster ones after it; at 1.33× the phone's close-up starts a draft that cycles through excuses (MY NEPHEW DID IT, I WAS PHISHED, IT WAS THE INTERN, DOG ATE MY SEED PHRASE), faster as it climbs; a paparazzi drone arrives over the bay at 1.56× and flashes more often the higher it goes; from 1.75×, and every ×1.4 after, his thumb comes down on POST?! and pulls back; the fans go hype one by one between 1.4× and 3×; at 2× the assistant packs bags behind the curtains while a second one walks out to barbecue hard drives on the lawn from a stack marked EVIDENCE; he sweats from 2.5×, the yacht starts its engine near 3× and the PR crisis team in suits walks to the gate at 4×. All of it follows the displayed multiplier, never the outcome. A long round keeps going: the yacht keeps growing, the evidence stack grows faster than he can burn it, the lawyers join the PR team at 40×, the captions move on to LAWYERS ARE IN THE CHAT, NOT A SECURITY, A VIBE and I WAS NEVER HERE, and past a billion the followers are mostly bots.

An accepted cashout is your fan leaving the party: a towel, a burst of confetti and a green flash, a NOT HACKED badge, and a strut to the rental Lambo that pulls in, waits by the door and drives off with them. The crowd keeps partying, and the caption keeps a jeet's regret ladder on how far it runs without you (JEETED IN PEACE, FANS SAY JEET, STILL NOT HACKED). The crash is the post: I GOT HACKED goes out with a sad trombone, the world freezes for a beat under a punch-in on the balcony and comes back through slow motion, the phone punches in, the fans jump in shock one after another and then, fan by fan, put their hands on their heads, the chart on the phone falls, the star shrugs on the balcony and squeezes one fake tear from a bottle marked TEARS while the drone dives in for the money shot (EXCLUSIVE: HE CRIED), the yacht leaves with the manager, the pool drains and the fans stand in a dry pool. If you got out first, the crowd still sulks and the post reads DODGED. Spectators see the same party without a YOU tag. The aftermath dissolves into the next betting phase, when the first three guests turn up. The star is a generic cartoon with no likeness of anyone.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator, and the tension curve.
- `audio.ts`: the page's sound, the shell's copy (see the repository README): opt-in through the Sound button, synthesised, nothing loaded. The scene runs it in `trap` with a `trombone` crash, and cues it from what it already tracks: a `pop` and the milestone ding at each champagne pop, a `tick` as the draft's excuse changes, `camera` for the drone's flash, `hiss` for a drive on the grill, `engine` when the yacht starts, `gasp` and `glug` as the post lands and the pool drains, and the cash-out fanfare. Until you cash out, his phone `buzz`es every 1.4 s at 1×, faster as it climbs (0.88 s by 2×), and a `heartbeat` marks each near miss on POST.
- `mansion.ts`: the sky and the bay, the yacht, the house, the balcony rig (typing, wave, shrug, glance, the tear), the manager, the assistant behind the curtains, the barbecue on the lawn, the paparazzi drone (hover, flashes, the dive), the PR team at the gate (the cook and the suits walk on planted feet) and the phone close-up with its cycling draft, its throbbing POST button and the thumb's near misses.
- `party.ts`: the lawn and the pool: the fans on the curve, the water that drains, the follower ticker, the champagne pops at milestones, the staggered jump of shock and the sulk after it, and your fan's exit with its confetti and the Lambo. Each fan keeps its own dance phase and hype point, and the arms swing between the drink, the hype and the sulk about the shoulder with the elbows kept outward.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the HUD (captions, multiplier, the pop count, the secured badge, the outcome pop), the round-phase logic, the hit-stop at the post, the phone's buzz and the dissolve into the next round.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round used by the gallery.

Fans, followers, the yacht, the whispers, the drone's flashes and the draft post all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. The published game uses full animation regardless of browser motion preferences.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the I Got Hacked URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to leave the party. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/i-got-hacked/`.

## Visual direction and small screens

The scene now introduces physical presentation acts at 32, 52, 75, 100, 125 and 145 seconds, followed by bounded recurring acts for unusually long rounds. Each act's props fade in and out with it, settle during a calm beat mid-act and fuss again after; the acts never lower the round's tension, so the packing, the engine and the PR team stay through them, and the props fade out as the post lands. `acts.ts` reads elapsed time only; it cannot choose an outcome or promise that a round will last this long.

On narrow screens, `portrait.ts` presents an enlarged character/detail view, a small overview that preserves the location, and a readable current line from the actual dialogue/chat/monitor state. The canonical shell still owns controls, round state and accepted cashouts.
