# I Got Hacked

A washed celebrity launches a coin from a mansion balcony. A fictional star in a bathrobe and shades types the launch post on a gold phone; the pool party below fills with fans on the multiplier curve, the follower ticker spins, the manager whispers in his ear and the yacht in the bay gets bigger. Tension: the whispers get faster, a paparazzi drone arrives over the bay and flashes more often the higher it goes, the phone's close-up shows a draft that cycles through excuses (MY NEPHEW DID IT, I WAS PHISHED, IT WAS THE INTERN, DOG ATE MY SEED PHRASE) faster and faster, the fans lean, the yacht starts its engine, the assistant packs bags behind the curtains while a second one barbecues hard drives on the lawn from a stack marked EVIDENCE, and the PR crisis team in suits arrives at the gate. An accepted cashout is your fan leaving the party: a towel, a burst of confetti and a green flash, a walk off to a rental Lambo, and a NOT HACKED badge. The crash is the post: I GOT HACKED goes out with a sad trombone, the phone punches in, the fans jump in shock one after another, the chart on the phone falls, the star shrugs on the balcony and squeezes one fake tear from a bottle marked TEARS while the drone dives in for the money shot (EXCLUSIVE: HE CRIED), the yacht leaves with the manager, the pool drains and the fans stand in a dry pool. The star is a generic cartoon with no likeness of anyone.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the page's sound, the shell's copy (see the repository README): opt-in through the Sound button, synthesised, nothing loaded. The scene runs it in `trap` with a `trombone` crash, and cues it from what it already tracks: a `pop` and the milestone ding at each champagne pop, a `tick` as the draft's excuse changes, `camera` for the drone's flash, `hiss` for a drive on the grill, `engine` when the yacht starts, `gasp` and `glug` as the post lands and the pool drains, and the cash-out fanfare.
- `mansion.ts`: the sky and the bay, the yacht, the house, the balcony rig (typing, wave, shrug, glance, the tear), the manager, the assistant behind the curtains, the barbecue on the lawn, the paparazzi drone (hover, flashes, the dive), the PR team at the gate and the phone close-up with its cycling draft and throbbing POST button.
- `party.ts`: the lawn and the pool: the fans on the curve, the water that drains, the follower ticker, the champagne pops at milestones, the staggered jump of shock at the post, and your fan's exit with its confetti.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the HUD (captions, multiplier, the pop count, the secured badge, the outcome pop) and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round used by the gallery.

Fans, followers, the yacht, the whispers, the drone's flashes and the draft post all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the shake, the phone's punch, the full-screen flashes (the drone's and the green of an exit), the party bounce and the particles.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the I Got Hacked URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to leave the party. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/i-got-hacked/`.
