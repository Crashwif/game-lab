# HOA Heist

**Dodge the manic subdivision board before your monthly dues hit orbit**

Watch suburban lawn signs multiply into aggressive assessment demands as an unhinged board president drones over Zoom. Cash out to slip into an unincorporated township tax-free before foreclosure notices paper your front door. If it crashes, your entire driveway gets repoed and turned into a mandatory tennis court.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *HOA hikes monthly neighborhood dues from $158 to an eye-watering $2,500 over Zoom* ([source](https://x.com/mattvanswol/status/2115517516151771436)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- hoa-heist` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
