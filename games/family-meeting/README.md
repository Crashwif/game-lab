# Family Meeting

A suburban kitchen at six o'clock: beige wallpaper, a gingham window onto the lawn, a sampler that reads BLESS THIS HOME, a fridge with the church bulletin, a crayon rainbow and her school photo on it, a kettle on the stove, a wooden cross on the wall, and a red gingham table with the casserole, the potatoes, the sweet tea and the family bible. Behind the table, facing us, sit her parents: Dad in plaid with his glasses and his fork, Mom in a cardigan with her pearls and her #1 MOM mug. In front of the table, back to us, sits their teenage daughter with rainbow hair down to the shoulders of her hoodie, and she has something to tell them.

The multiplier is how far she takes it, and what she is confessing is her portfolio. While the bets go in, Mom asks who is saying grace, Dad says "not it", and she has an announcement. Then it is keyed to the curve: "so… I've been thinking about my portfolio" at the start, "I put the college fund in a dog coin" by 1.3×, "it's not gambling, it's a DAO" by 1.7×, "I'm a KOL now. 40k followers. mostly bots" by 2.2×, "so I aped the church building fund" by 2.9×, "few understand" by 3.8×, and on through Pastor Rick in her discord, the leveraged minivan, Grandma the whale, the family bible as an NFT and the house sold to buy the dip, with the parents getting a word in between ("pass the potatoes", "what in god's name is a DAO", "WHAT DID YOU DO WITH THE CHURCH FUND"). Each line is a speech bubble and a cue, queued so a bubble stays up at least 2.6 s before the same speaker's next line replaces it; six seconds of silence bring a filler exchange. She explains with her hands, more with the tension.

The tension is 1 − 1/x (0.33 at 1.5×, 0.5 at 2×, 0.67 at 3×), so the first 15 s, where most rounds end, carry the build, with a beat every one to three seconds. The parents' heat follows it on a slow spring that each of her lines kicks, and each line gets a take: Dad nods and his brows shoot up, Mom's take lands a beat later. Their skin goes pink by 1.5×, they sweat and the temple veins show by 2×, the brows drop into a V, the eyes widen and go bloodshot, Mom clutches her pearls by 2×, steam comes out of the ears by 2.6×, Dad's fist comes down on the table from 2.9×, the kettle whistles by 3.3×, Dad's head cracks by 3.8× and Mom's by 5×, while Dad's combover lifts, his glasses slide, his fork bends and her mug sloshes. A slower log driver keeps long rounds changing: past 10× the heads keep swelling, shaking and cracking. They blink, look at whoever is talking and move their mouths when they speak. The cross starts to rattle and creak on its nail by 1.5×. The caption names each of those beats when it happens, between the captions her lines put up. The HUD reads the blood pressure, which thumps with a heartbeat that quickens with the tension, and her bags, which go from +400% to −97% to DEV GONE while the multiplier climbs.

An accepted cash-out is her exit: "anyway I'm taking profits in my room". She stands, lifts her plate from the table onto her right hand and walks off (her feet follow the distance she covers, getting up to speed over 0.4 s), the parents simmer down to a third, and the caption runs from SHE TOOK PROFITS through PAPER HANDS, FULL PLATE and SHE'S NOT CHECKING THE CHART to OK, SHE'S CHECKING THE CHART and PROFIT IS STILL PROFIT as the number keeps going without her. If the round ends after that she dodged it (SHE TOOK PROFITS UPSTAIRS, DODGED): the parents deflate, close their eyes and sigh ("we'll pray about it", "pass the potatoes"). The crash while she is still at the table is RUGGED AT DINNER and blows both heads off: a flash, a 0.16 s hit-stop with a 10% cut-in held 0.3 s, then in slow motion a seeded burst of skin, hair tufts, teeth, an eye each, Dad's glasses, Mom's pearls and hairband into the room and into the casserole (a piece lands on the cloth only if the cloth is under it when it gets there), the cross jumps off its nail and topples to lie across the dinner, smoke rises from the collars, the bodies slump, and within the two seconds the crash is shown the stamp reads MIND BLOWN, she shrugs ("…so anyway") and raises her phone for a picture; held longer, as in a replay, she takes it and asks "can I borrow the car". A bust at 1.00× catches her at "so, um—". Someone watching without a stake gets THOUGHTS & PRAYERS instead of MIND BLOWN. The next dinner fades up from dark.

- `scene.ts`: composition in depth order (room, chairs, parents, table, daughter, her chair, bubbles, what is in the air), the round-phase logic, the tension mapping, the beat captions, the cash-out and crash choreography (flash, hit-stop, slow motion, camera punch), the audio cues and the HUD.
- `family.ts`: the two parents' rigs (torsos, arms and props, the head with everything that heats up, the takes, blinks and glances, the explosion and the deflation) and the daughter's rig (the hoodie, the gesturing hands, the rainbow hair in bands, the mouth that moves as she talks, standing up and leaving with her plate, the selfie).
- `kinematics.ts`: two-bone limbs (a pole between −1 and 1 swings an elbow or knee through depth instead of flipping it) and the distance-driven step.
- `kitchen.ts`: the room and its props, the cross on its nail and its fall, the kettle, the table and the dinner, the dialogue and the speech bubbles, the thrown bits and their landings, the clouds and the smoke.
- `motion.ts`: springs, easing, deterministic noise and a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the shared page audio (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). Dinner muzak with an organ under it that tightens with the multiplier; the crash is a boom. Cues: a pop for every bubble, a heartbeat under the blood pressure, a thud for the fist, Mom's gasp, a hiss when the ears first steam, the cross creaking, the kettle's whistle, a clang as the cross lands and a splash as pieces hit the casserole, the register and a chair creak on a cash-out, the door as she leaves, the camera for the picture.
- `main.ts`: the shared page shell. `replay.json`: a verified 10.97× round for the gallery.

Heat, lines, captions, the cross, the kettle and the HUD all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing drawn here reads or changes the committed outcome, and her bags are dinner-table fiction, not a prediction. The burst is seeded from the crash point so a replay throws the pieces the same way every time. A scene that opens mid-round or on a crashed round settles into place: the last lines up, the heat where it would be, the pieces where they fell. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes the shake, the head jitter, the nod, the hit-stop, the camera punch, her fidget and the heartbeat thump. Sound is opt-in through the Sound button.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Family Meeting URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to send her to her room with her profits. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/family-meeting/`.

## Visual direction and small screens

The scene now introduces physical presentation acts at 32, 52, 75, 100, 125 and 145 seconds, followed by bounded recurring acts for unusually long rounds. Each act's props fade in, and a short breather sets the teapot steaming; the acts never cool the parents down, and their props fade out at a crash or a cash-out. `acts.ts` reads elapsed time only; it cannot choose an outcome or promise that a round will last this long.

On narrow screens, `portrait.ts` presents an enlarged character/detail view, a small overview that preserves the location, and a readable current line from the actual dialogue/chat/monitor state. The canonical shell still owns controls, round state and accepted cashouts. Reduced motion removes the new prop oscillation.
