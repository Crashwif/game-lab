# Family Meeting

A suburban kitchen at six o'clock: beige wallpaper, a gingham window onto the lawn, a sampler that reads BLESS THIS HOME, a fridge with the church bulletin, a crayon rainbow and her school photo on it, a kettle on the stove, a wooden cross on the wall, and a red gingham table with the casserole, the potatoes, the sweet tea and the family bible. Behind the table, facing us, sit her parents: Dad in plaid with his glasses and his fork, Mom in a cardigan with her pearls and her #1 MOM mug. In front of the table, back to us, sits their teenage daughter with rainbow hair down to the shoulders of her hoodie, and she has something to tell them.

The multiplier is how far she takes it. The conversation is keyed to the curve: "so… I've been thinking about my gender" at the start, "gender is a social construct, mom" by 1.7×, "the binary is a colonial import" by 4×, "my identity is a superposition until observed" by 8×, "there is no self, only vibes" by 17×, and on into the concept of Tuesday, with the parents getting a word in between ("pass the potatoes", "is this from the internet", "WHAT DID YOU SAY ABOUT CHURCH"). Every line is a speech bubble and a cue; she explains with her hands, more with the tension. The parents' heat follows the multiplier on a slow spring that each of her lines kicks: their skin goes pink, red, then purple, the brows drop into a V, the eyes widen and go bloodshot with a twitch, veins pulse at the temples, sweat runs, steam comes out of their ears, the heads swell and shake, Dad's combover lifts, his glasses slide, his fork bends and his fist comes down on the table, Mom's hand goes to her pearls and her mug sloshes, cracks appear on both heads near the top. The cross rattles on its nail, the kettle comes to a boil and whistles, and the HUD reads BLOOD PRESSURE and the pronouns of the moment.

An accepted cash-out is her exit: "anyway I'm eating in my room", she stands up and walks off with her plate, the parents simmer down to a third, and if the round ends after that they only deflate, close their eyes and sigh ("we'll pray about it", "pass the potatoes"). The crash while she is still at the table blows both heads off: a flash and a hit-stop, then in slow motion a seeded burst of skin, hair tufts, teeth, an eye each, Dad's glasses, Mom's pearls and hairband into the room and into the casserole, the cross jumps off its nail and lands in the dinner, smoke rises from the collars, the bodies slump, and she shrugs ("…so anyway"), picks up her phone and takes a picture ("can I borrow the car").

- `scene.ts`: composition in depth order (room, chairs, parents, table, daughter, her chair, bubbles, what is in the air), the round-phase logic, the cash-out and crash choreography (flash, hit-stop, slow motion, camera punch), the audio cues and the HUD.
- `family.ts`: the two parents' rigs (torsos, arms and props, the head with everything that heats up, the explosion and the deflation) and the daughter's rig (the hoodie, the gesturing hands, the rainbow hair in bands, the mouth that moves as she talks, standing up and leaving, the selfie).
- `kitchen.ts`: the room and its props, the cross on its nail and its fall, the kettle, the table and the dinner, the dialogue and the speech bubbles, the thrown bits and their landings, the clouds and the smoke.
- `motion.ts`: springs, easing, deterministic noise and a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the shared page audio (a copy of the shell's, never edited here). Dinner muzak with an organ under it that tightens with the multiplier; the crash is a boom. Cues: a pop for every bubble, a thud for the fist, Mom's gasp, a hiss when the ears first steam, the cross creaking, the kettle's whistle, a clang as the cross lands and a splash as pieces hit the casserole, the register and a chair creak on a cash-out, the door as she leaves, the camera for the picture.
- `main.ts`: the shared page shell. `replay.json`: a verified 10.97× round for the gallery.

Heat, lines, the cross, the kettle and the HUD all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing drawn here changes the committed outcome. The burst is seeded from the crash point so a replay throws the pieces the same way every time. A scene that opens mid-round or on a crashed round settles into place: the last lines up, the heat where it would be, the pieces where they fell. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes the shake, the head jitter, the hit-stop and the camera punch. Sound is opt-in through the Sound button.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Family Meeting URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to send her to her room. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/family-meeting/`.

## Visual direction and small screens

The scene now introduces physical presentation acts at 32, 52, 75, 100, 125 and 145 seconds, followed by bounded recurring acts for unusually long rounds. New props accompany changes in character effort, with a short easing of tension before renewed activity. `acts.ts` reads elapsed time only; it cannot choose an outcome or promise that a round will last this long.

On narrow screens, `portrait.ts` presents an enlarged character/detail view, a small overview that preserves the location, and a readable current line from the actual dialogue/chat/monitor state. The canonical shell still owns controls, round state and accepted cashouts. Reduced motion removes the new prop oscillation.
