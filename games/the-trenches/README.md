# The Trenches

A launch seen from a trench. Frog soldiers in helmets crouch in the mud under a sky of shell bursts and a chart-shaped ridge line; the whistle blows, the squad goes over the top and advances with the multiplier while the loot on the ridge (bags, a Lambo, a moon) gets closer. Tension: the ridge steepens, the mud sucks at boots, a scope glint appears in the shell holes, the artillery whistles get closer, rats and jeet-frogs run back past the line, and the sergeant's field phone rings with orders like "buy the dip" and "funds are safu". A DAYS SINCE LAST RUG sign on the trench wall counts up with the multiplier over ration crates stencilled HOPIUM and COPIUM, and past 2× the paper-hands frog in the squad starts raising a white flag (PAPER HANDS) that climbs with the tension until the sergeant's glare (a dotted line of sight and OI. FLAG DOWN.) snaps it back down, after which it creeps up again. An accepted cashout is your frog diving back into the trench with a bag: a register, a green wash, a helmet clang with a spray of mud and cash, a cigar, a SURVIVED THE TRENCHES badge. The crash is the nuke: a hit-stop on the flash frame, the blast in slow motion with the camera punched in on the ridge, a seeded mushroom cloud, the frogs flung back into the trench in silhouette (screaming), helmets raining down and clanging, the ridge a crater, the sign's card flipping over to a red 0 with a ratchet, and the field phone reading DEV SOLD.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the page's shared procedural music and effects (the same file in every game). The Trenches plays a military band that quickens on the march, blows the whistle as the squad goes over, stomps with your frog's plants, rings the field phone with each order, whistles the incoming shells and yelps with each jeet past the middle, squeaks when the sergeant glares the flag down, clangs the helmet and rings the register on your dive back, and on the crash tape-stops into the boom, the squad's scream, the helmets' clangs and the sign's ratchet.
- `field.ts`: the battlefield: the parallax sky and shell bursts, the ridge line, the loot, the mud and shell holes, the rats and jeets, the nuke with its shockwave, silhouettes, helmet rain and crater, and the one-frame events the scene turns into sound.
- `squad.ts`: the trench and the squad: the frog marching rig, the white flag and the sergeant's glare, the DAYS SINCE LAST RUG sign and its flip, the ration crates, the sergeant and his field phone, the whistle, your frog's dive back with its mud, and the KIA state.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the HUD (captions, multiplier, metres advanced, the secured badge, the outcome pop), the crash choreography (hit-stop, slow motion, camera punch-in), the sound cues and the round-phase logic.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round used by the gallery.

March cadence, ridge steepness, the whistles, the rats and the scope glint all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. Comedy only: no insignia, no gore. The published game uses full animation regardless of browser motion preferences.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Trenches URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to dive back into the trench. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/the-trenches/`.
