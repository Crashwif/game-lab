# The Trenches

A launch seen from a trench. Frog soldiers in helmets wait in the mud under a sky of shell bursts and a chart-shaped ridge line; the whistle blows and the frogs vault the parapet one by one from the sergeant's end, then march up the field toward the ridge (halfway there at 2×) while the mud lines and shell holes scroll past under their feet and the loot on the ridge (bags, a Lambo, a moon) gets closer. Tension is 1 − 1/x, so most of the escalation lands between 1× and 3×, where most rounds end: rats, then jeet-frogs, run back past the line from 1.15×; the sergeant's field phone rings from 1.2× with orders that escalate from GM SOLDIERS and BUY THE DIP to FUNDS ARE SAFU and DEV WALLET MOVING. PROBABLY NOTHING; incoming shells whistle from 1.4×; a scope glint shows on the ridge from 2.2× (a second sniper from 4×); the ridge steepens and the mud sucks at boots. Captions and stingers land every two or three seconds through the first 15 s, and your heartbeat quickens under them while your bet rides. A DAYS SINCE LAST RUG sign on the trench wall counts up with the multiplier over ration crates stencilled HOPIUM and COPIUM, and from about 1.2× the paper-hands frog in the squad starts raising a white flag (PAPER HANDS); once it is readable the sergeant's glare (a dotted line of sight and OI. FLAG DOWN.) snaps it back down, after which it creeps up again. The sky and the moon also follow a slow log driver, so a 100× or 1000× round keeps changing.

An accepted cashout is your frog crouching and diving back into the trench with a bag: a register, a green wash, the sergeant's JEET! ...NICE EXIT THO, a helmet clang with a spray of mud and cash, a cigar, a SURVIVED THE TRENCHES badge, and TOOK PROFIT. FROG JEET., followed by a regret ladder as the squad marches on without you; the nuke after it reads DODGED THE NUKE. The crash is the nuke: a 0.16 s hit-stop on the flash frame, the blast in slow motion with the camera punched 10% in on the ridge and held, a seeded mushroom cloud, the frogs on the field flung tumbling back over the sandbags (screaming) to land on their sides on the trench floor, the sergeant standing in shock and then toppling over, helmets raining down and clanging, the ridge a crater, the sign's card flipping over to a red 0 with a ratchet, and the field phone reading DEV SOLD. A bust before anyone has left the trench squashes the squad flat where it stands (RUGGED BEFORE THE WHISTLE). Only a player with a stake gets the YOU marker over their frog and the KIA tag; a spectator gets SPECTATING · NO SKIN IN THE GAME. A new round fades in with fresh frogs climbing into the trench.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `audio.ts`: the page's shared procedural music and effects (the same file in every game). The Trenches plays a military band that quickens on the march, blows the whistle as the squad goes over, stomps as each frog lands and with your frog's plants, beats your heartbeat faster with the tension until you cash out, rings the field phone with each order, whistles the incoming shells and yelps with each jeet past the middle, squeaks when the sergeant glares the flag down, clangs the helmet and rings the register on your dive back, and on the crash tape-stops into the boom, the squad's scream, the helmets' clangs and the sign's ratchet.
- `field.ts`: the battlefield: the parallax sky and shell bursts, the ridge line and its scope glints, the loot, the mud and shell holes that scroll with the march, the rats and jeets, the nuke with its shockwave, helmet rain and crater, and the one-frame events the scene turns into sound.
- `squad.ts`: the trench and the squad: the frog rig (marching, vaulting, diving, flung, flattened, lying), the staggered vault over the top, the white flag and the sergeant's glare, the DAYS SINCE LAST RUG sign and its flip, the ration crates, the sergeant and his field phone, the whistle, your frog's dive back with its mud, and the KIA state. Each frog's flight leaves from exactly where it was drawn and lands exactly on its resting pose, crossing the sandbags at the top of its arc.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the HUD (the caption ladder and the cash-out's regret ladder, multiplier, metres advanced, the secured badge, the spectator tag, the outcome pop), the crash choreography (hit-stop, slow motion, held camera punch-in), the reset fade, the sound cues and the round-phase logic.
- `portrait.ts`: the close phone composition; it keeps the live multiplier (the crash point once crashed) up after a cash-out, with your exit beside it.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round used by the gallery.

March cadence, ridge steepness, the whistles, the rats, the phone and the scope glints all follow the displayed multiplier or the round's elapsed time; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. Strides advance with the ground covered, not the clock, and planted feet slide back with the scrolling field. Comedy only: no insignia, no gore. The published game uses full animation regardless of browser motion preferences.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Trenches URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to dive back into the trench. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/the-trenches/`.
