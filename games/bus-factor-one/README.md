# Bus Factor One

**Keep your phone on Do Not Disturb while your entire corporate office melts**

Sip iced latte on your veranda while your desk phone explodes with desperate boss calls as the client vendor contract ticks down. Cash out to collect an emergency five-figure raise on Monday morning. Crash and the vendor expires at midnight, locking everybody out forever.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *Sole system employee takes day off, office implodes with 31 missed calls and panic contract renew* ([source](https://x.com/empress_md/status/2115538976987623576)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- bus-factor-one` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
