# Coach Challenge

**Beg your coach to challenge the whistle before the replay burns your timeout**

A sweaty forward drops to his knees on court side wood pleading for a video review while the multiplier ticks up. Cashing out triggers a successful coach review spin and standing ovation from the bench. Crashing reveals a definitive slow-mo angle showing your foul as the referee tosses you out.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *Yaxel Lendeborg desperately begs Steve Kerr to throw challenge flag* ([source](https://x.com/HaterReport/status/2116368178918093173)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- coach-challenge` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
