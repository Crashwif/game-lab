# Self-Deport

A strip-mall street at dawn, drawn in code: a car wash, a laundromat, a county courthouse whose sign says `IMMIGRATION CHECK-IN →` and `← EXIT (THEY WAIT HERE)`, a payday lender, a taqueria, the Hardware Depot, `CITY HALL · SANCTUARY CITY* · *BUDGET PENDING`, `HOLE FOODS · $9 KALE · HARVESTED BY MEMO · $5/HR`, `PATRIOT PAWN · WE BUY GOLD · WE SELL FREEDOM`, `YOGA & YOUR RIGHTS`, the DMV (`NOW SERVING: 3,000 · ESTIMATED WAIT: 19 YEARS`), `LUXURY LOFTS · FORMERLY AFFORDABLE`, `BAIL BONDS & CROSSFIT`, `ESQUIRE & ESQUIRE · DEPORTATION DEFENSE · ALSO DUI`, `THE DISPENSARY · LAND ACKNOWLEDGMENT INSIDE · ID REQUIRED (IRONIC)` and the `ICE FIELD OFFICE · NOW HIRING · ALSO DETAINING · TAKE A NUMBER`. On the sidewalk, **Memo**: bucket hat, mustache, hi-vis vest over a flannel, work boots, a leaf blower he never puts down. A billboard behind the skyline says `NOW HIRING · ICE · $50,000 BONUS · NO EXPERIENCE · VIBES CHECKED`. The bus bench ad says `SELF-DEPORT APP · GET $1,000* · *terms: lol`.

The multiplier is how long Memo has stayed ahead of the quota, and how much he has stacked doing it. The longer the round runs, the more of the federal apparatus arrives for one landscaper, and the dumber it gets. **Cash out and he self-deports**: phone out, the app, the $1,000 stipend, a suitcase, off to the right under a plane towing `ADIÓS · “VOLUNTARY”`. **Get caught and the game crashes**: a van screeches in, three agents in gaiters and `POLICE` vests pile on, `DETAINED` slams down, the picture tears, and the whole canvas blue-screens with `Stop code: DUE_PROCESS_NOT_FOUND`.

It is a skill game (`interactive: true`) with two ways to hide. When something on the street looks at the sidewalk, press **↓**, **S**, **C** or tap the picture and Memo goes **cone mode**: he crouches under a traffic cone with his eyes showing through the stripe. If the cone is up for the look, the sweep is `UNSEEN` (`INFRASTRUCTURE`, `JUST A CONE`, `NOT A PERSON`) and he pockets a bonus. If it is not, `SPOTTED!` (`THAT CONE HAS EYES`, `A CONE. RUNNING.`, `HE BLINKED`): he trips, rolls, the hat flies, paychecks spill. Under the cone he can only shuffle, so the paychecks along the sidewalk pass him by slower.

Or press **↑**, **W**, **A** or swipe up and call **the ally**: a woman with a tote bag, a push mower and an `IN THIS HOUSE WE BELIEVE · NO HUMAN IS ILLEGAL · $5 LAWNS` yard sign runs in, unrolls a strip of sod, plants the sign between Memo and the street, and he does her lawn behind it. Nobody looks at the help, so the sweep is always `UNSEEN` (`NOBODY SEES THE HELP`, `THE HELP IS INVISIBLE`, `SIGN-BLOCKED`, `ALLY SHIELD: $5`). The price is $5. The price is never $5: she takes a paycheck off his stack each time, more each time he comes back, and renegotiates in a bubble every time (`$5 but also the hedges. and the gutters.`, `ok $4. inflation. you understand.`, `exposure? i have 300 followers. 40 are bots.`, `i'll pay you in a land acknowledgment.`, `can i post you? for awareness. don't smile.`, `my HOA says you can't park here. so, $3.`, `venmo's down. next time. (there is no next time)`, `i marched for you. in 2017. once.`, `would you take a kombucha? it's unpasteurized.`, `my husband votes the other way. he also needs the lawn.`, `this is what an ally looks like. $2.`). While he mows, the street stops and no paychecks come. A bot hides for him, late and sometimes not at all, cone or ally, until the player presses anything. Being spotted never touches the bet: collisions are presentation, the backend crash still ends the round.

## Direction

Both sides of the street are performance art and the outrage is scheduled. The right wants his labor, the left wants his selfie, both want the photo, nobody wants the guy. The HUD keeps a `FAKE OUTRAGE INDEX · SPONSORED BY A MATTRESS`: the `LEFT` and `RIGHT` needles climb with the tension and pin at 100%; `ACTUAL HELP` reads 0%, 1% while the ally is charging him, 3% while the taco truck is open.

The comedy is the apparatus: an unmarked van with a cardboard `NOT ICE` sign in the windshield, a man in a neck gaiter in July reading a newspaper upside down, a drone for a leaf blower, a cruiser that slows and holds beside him (he is on his phone), facial recognition that matches him at 100% and also everyone, an alligator on a leash, the National Guard directing traffic, a judge's injunction shredded mid-air by a drone, agents who detain a tourist from Iowa, a mariachi band and finally each other. Memo is never the joke. He pays taxes (ITIN, look it up), he has been here nineteen years, the pickup parked on the street says `MEMO'S LAWN CARE · EST. 2007 · LICENSED · LOVED`, and the cousin's taco truck is a sanctuary with salsa. The crowd on the sidewalk is on his side: the teens are streaming him to 2.4 million people, one of them is duetting, none of them are helping.

- Both sides get it, by the multiplier. The `DEPORT 'EM ALL · CONCRETE & LAWN` truck pulls up and needs six guys Monday, cash, the hat is for the customers. The farmer's `HELP WANTED · NO PAPERS NO PROBLEM` truck wants an exception for his guys; his guys are you; he voted for the wall and the wall needs weeding. The flag guy watering his lawn says Memo is one of the good ones, Thursdays, deport the rest, not him, he does the edging. The protester with the `ABOLISH ICE` sign wants him in the shot and scared, for the algorithm, in front of a ring light. The NGO's drone drops `KNOW YOUR RIGHTS` pamphlets that say `DONATE`. Two suits, red tie and blue tie, run in for a bipartisan photo-op, thumbs up on either side of him, flash, and run off (`strong border. strong lawn.` / `dignity. also the lawn.`). A `COEXIST` bumper sticker passes at 90 mph and honks. A land acknowledgment is read from a `MIMOSA BRUNCH` podium outside the Home Depot. Cable news splits the screen between `OUTRAGED` and `ALSO OUTRAGED`, same sponsor, a mattress. Two rallies, `DEPORT` / `LAW & ORDER` / `BUILD IT` against `NO HUMAN IS ILLEGAL` / `ABOLISH` / `LOVE (CONDITIONS)`, film each other across his path: `we're trending!`, `no, WE'RE trending!`, same parking lot, same phone plan.
- Every enforcement beat is keyed to the displayed multiplier, never to the outcome: the scene does not know the crash point. The tension (`1 − 1/x`) builds through the 1×–3× window where most rounds end; a slower log driver keeps long rounds changing.
- No real person is named or drawn. The agency is named because the game is about it; the people in it are generic cartoons.
- The voice is deadpan. Captions read like a memo. The horror is in the paperwork: `WARRANT: ADMINISTRATIVE (THEY SIGNED IT THEMSELVES)`.
- The blue screen is the punchline the whole round sets up. Caught means the round is over, so the game is over, so the game is over.

## Memo

Drawn entirely in Canvas 2D in the catalog's thick-ink style: two-bone legs and arms solved from world targets, a gait driven by distance so his planted sole never skates on the sidewalk, a hip bob and a forward lean from the same stride phase, a glance over his shoulder when something is looking, eyes that widen and brows that climb with tension, sweat from about 1.7×, blinks on a noise schedule. The leaf blower hangs from his front hand and streams leaves behind him while he runs. The paychecks along the sidewalk bob; he scoops them as he passes (`STACKED $N`), spills some when spotted, and banks them when he self-deports (`SENT HOME`, kept in this browser's stash with a rank from `DAY LABORER` to `EMPLOYS 40 AMERICANS`; a gold chain and a tejana unlock on the way; none of it is worth anything or touches a round).

- **Cone mode**: the crouch and the cone are springs; the cone overshoots as it lands. His eyes show through the white stripe. His boots stick out of the bottom.
- **The ally**: she runs in from the left at a planted gait, unrolls a roll of sod in front of him, stands with the sign between him and the street and bargains in a bubble; he takes the mower with both hands and walks in place behind it, the blower on his shoulder, clippings flying, `$5. for the lawn. and my dignity.` The fee floats off his stack in red. After 1.8 seconds she runs off with the sign and the sod stays.
- **The stumble**: a trip, a full roll with squash, the hat on a ballistic arc that lands on the sidewalk, back on his head as he gets up (0.72 s), on his feet at one second.
- **The exit** (after an accepted cash-out, timed from the round's own clock so a replay or a late entry puts him in the same place): he stops, the phone comes up with `ICE HOME™ · SELF-DEPORT · GET $1,000* · *terms: lol`, he taps it at 1.35 s, `+$1,000*` floats up with confetti at 1.55 s, the suitcase arrives in his back hand at 2.4 s, shades on, and at 3 s he walks off to the right on planted feet with the blower on his shoulder. A note stays on the sidewalk: `GONE HOME. WATER THE PLANTS.` A plane crosses towing `ADIÓS · “VOLUNTARY”`. The street stops; the ladder keeps arriving for an empty sidewalk. The caption keeps score, always on his side: `TOOK THE STIPEND. CLEAN HANDS. CLEAN LAWN.` → `THE VAN SHOWED UP LATER. HE IS AT THE BEACH.` → `OPENED A LANDSCAPING EMPIRE BACK HOME. THRIVING.` → `AMERICA’S LAWNS ARE FOUR FEET TALL. HE IS NOT COMING BACK.`
- **Caught** (the crash with no cash-out): a 0.16 s frozen beat with a red-then-blue wash and a punch-in on Memo. The van screeches in from the right with skid marks and tire smoke and stops in the street; the blower drops; his hands go up. The door slides open at 0.42 s and three agents hop out and run up the sidewalk on planted feet, one filming. The tackle at 0.95 s is a cloud of dust with boots, a hat and stars in it: `!!`, `STOP RESISTING`, `HE ISN’T`. `ICE · ENFORCEMENT & REMOVAL · QUOTA +1 · DETAINED` slams down at 1.12 s. The door slams at 1.5 s, the van peels out left at 1.62 s, the picture tears at 1.8 s (slices of the last frame thrown sideways, bars, static, scanlines) and at 2.15 s the blue screen takes the whole canvas, HUD included: `:(  Memo ran into a problem and needs to be removed. We’re just collecting some error info, and then we’ll start over for you. The bus is already running. 37% complete. Stop code: DUE_PROCESS_NOT_FOUND. What failed: FOURTH_AMENDMENT.SYS.` The percentage climbs to 37 and sticks. A room that moves on to betting before the blue screen lands does not cut it short: the raid keeps playing on the scene's own clock for up to about five seconds (the catalog's encore), and the last crash frame then dissolves into the new round's street.
- **Dodged** (the crash after a cash-out): the same van and agents, who find the note, hold it up (`…“water the plants”?`, `he self-deported. voluntarily.`) and stand around under `ICE · NOTICE UNSERVED · NOBODY HOME` while the van's hazards blink. No blue screen: the game only crashes when the round takes him.

A spectator with no stake sees `WATCHING` over Memo and runs a paper Memo; the cone still works, nothing banks. A round met late (a hidden tab, a page opened mid-round) settles Memo, the street and the ladder straight into their poses from `elapsed` and `crashAge`, with the quiet crash and no cues.

## Sound

The shared helper's `phonk` style with the `static` stinger: the music tightens with the tension, a heartbeat under it quickens while you are in, a stomp per footfall, a pop as the cone lands, a gasp and a thud when spotted, a ding when unseen, a coin per paycheck, cues for each arrival (the van's engine, the drone's whoosh, the cruiser's siren, the scanner's beep, the tourist's scream, the helicopter, the guard's whistle, the gator's squeak, the crowd, the injunction's zap, the fax), the cash-out register with a notification, a click, a ka-ching and a whoosh through the exit, and at the crash a siren, an engine and a screech, a punch and a thud for the tackle, the stamp, the door, the getaway, a buzz as the picture tears, then the static as the blue screen lands. Sound is opt-in through the Sound button and stays off until it is pressed.

## Play and preview

From the repository root, use Node 24 and npm 11:

```sh
npm ci --include=dev --bin-links=true
npm run preview -- self-deport
```

The preview prints the replay address. Remove `?mode=replay` to join the emulator with local credits. **Run with Memo**, then **Self-deport · cash out**, or press **Space** while the game has focus. **↓**, **S**, **C**, a tap or a downward swipe on the picture is cone mode; **↑**, **W**, **A** or a swipe up calls the ally. Embedded play uses the host's stake and credit controls. **Restart replay** repeats the verified fixture (9.07×, the starter's recorded round with this game's ID), which runs the ladder through the helicopter, the guard, the gator, the stream, the injunction and the fax and ends in the raid and the blue screen.

`npm run build` writes the publishable HTML, JavaScript and CSS to `dist/self-deport/`. Validate with `npm run typecheck`, `npm run check` and `npm test`. For the rig's own regressions (bone lengths, planted soles at 30, 60 and 120 fps, the stumble's recovery, the cone covering the crouch, late entry, the exit and the raid pose), run:

```sh
node --test games/self-deport/memo.test.mjs
```

The test file is excluded from the published bundle and the source pack.

## Source and artwork

- `scene.ts` composes the street, Memo's drive, the ladder with its gaze windows and verdicts, the bot, the cone, the stash, the exit choreography, the raid, the tear and the blue screen, the camera, captions, cues and the HUD, and the portrait presentation.
- `memo.ts` is Memo: the rig state and its springs, the two-bone solver and the distance-driven gait (shared with everyone else on the street), the pose for each mode including the mower, the stumble, the face, the hat, the blower and the cone.
- `ally.ts` is the second hide: her arrival and exit, the sod, the sign, the clippings, the escalating fee and the bargain lines.
- `street.ts` draws the dawn sky, the far skyline with the billboard, the strip-mall facades, the sidewalk and its props (mulch, a hydrant, a mailbox, cones for the taking, the bus bench ad, a planter, a news box, a trash can), the asphalt and the parked cars, the paychecks, the leaves and the spilled bills, and the note.
- `threats.ts` is the ladder: the kinds, their spans and layers, the shared positions the gaze tests and the drawing both read, a generic person (`figure`) for the agents, the gaiter guy, the tourist, the teens, the mariachi, the guardsman, the protester, the suits, the flag guy, the speaker and both rallies, placards and pickups, and the van, cruiser, Humvee, Tesla and taco truck.
- `raid.ts` is the crash: the van's arrival and getaway, the agents' approach, the tackle cloud, the stamps, the tear and the blue screen.
- `input.ts` wires the cone and the ally from the game's own files (keys, tap, swipes), leaving Space to the shell. `stash.ts` keeps the paychecks sent home. `lines.ts` holds every word.
- `drawing.ts` supplies the inked shapes and lettering; `motion.ts` is the shared toolkit (exact damped springs, easing, noise, a seeded generator); `portrait.ts` the shared compositor for tall screens.
- `main.ts`, `audio.ts` and the base styles are the canonical Game Lab shell. All music and effects are synthesized by its audio engine; `clips.json` is empty.
- `replay.json` uses the repository's verified example round with this game's ID.

Everything on the page is drawn in code; the game ships no images and makes no artwork requests. Memo is an original character. No real person, badge, seal or logo is reproduced. The gallery's `open` setting permits Game Lab remixing with no derivative royalty. Credits have no monetary value, and the scene never determines or changes a crash or cashout result.

## Visual direction and small screens

On narrow screens `portrait.ts` presents a close-up of Memo and the sidewalk ahead of him, with the full street as an inset and the caption and his line beneath; the crash close-up takes in the van and the pile, the blue screen takes the whole frame. The canonical shell still owns controls, round state and accepted cashouts.
