# Exit Liquidity

The liquidity pool is an actual pool. A backyard party at golden hour: a DJ Wojak, an LP booth, loungers, an empty lifeguard chair whose sign reads NO LIFEGUARD (HE SOLD), and the dev on his lounger with the plug chain round his wrist. Your degen floats on an inflatable flamingo (a spectator's floats empty, with no YOU tag). The build is paced on a tension of 1 − 1/x, so it carries the 1×–3× window where most rounds end. Degens run in along the far deck behind the dev and cannonball off the rim while the water rises and turns murkier. The dev starts sitting up by about 1.3× and is three quarters of the way up at 2×, with the chain wound round his fist. He worries it on a ticking pulse that quickens with the tension, and it creaks a notch tighter at 1.2×, 1.5×, 2×, 3× and 6×. He trembles from about 1.7× and smirks at 4×. At 1.85×, 4.5×, 12× and 40× he fakes a yank: the chain snaps taut, the plug lifts and bubbles, the crowd flinches, and he says "jk". Each holder cracks on their own nerve, nervous from about 1.2–1.8× and panicking from about 1.9–4×, and glances at the dev when they do. From 2.5× an AIRDROP helicopter crosses the sunset at every milestone and tips three fresh degens down into the pool; past 5× a whale surfaces under the crowd. An accepted cashout paddles you to the ladder while the nearest holders call "jeet" and "paper hands", leaves your flamingo bobbing, and walks you onto a lounger with a towel, a cocktail and shades, to a burst of confetti. The caption then climbs a regret ladder as the number runs on past your exit. The crash is the dev pulling the plug: he is on his feet yanking the chain, and the plug gives a few frames later. The picture holds for a hit-stop and punches in on the drain, then runs slow as the water funnels away. Everyone still floating spins down with it, one sad Wojak is left in the puddle (you, if you stayed in), and the dev steps to the rim for a selfie with the empty pool ("say RUGGED", flash), all inside the two-second crashed phase. In a replay he then strolls off with a bag, gloating "thx for the liquidity". The stamp reads REKT if you stayed in, JEETED IN TIME (caption DODGED THE DRAIN) if you cashed out, and RUGGED for a spectator. Between rounds the pool refills, the dev strolls back to his lounger and a fresh flamingo drops in; your degen hops on when you bet. A POOL RULES board on the fence has warned everyone: no selling, no lifeguard, dev may pull plug.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `pool.ts`: the cutaway water body with its level, slosh, murk, whale, drain funnel, refill and splashes; the rim, ladder, plug (and its lift on a fake yank) and chain.
- `party.ts`: holders (the run-in, crouch and tucked cannonball, the helicopter drops, each holder's nerve, floating, the drain spiral), your avatar's paddle, climb, walk and lounge on a blended two-bone rig with distance-driven feet, the confetti, the dev's grin, lean, chain tugs, fake yanks, yank, selfie, exit and return with his speech bubbles, the AIRDROP helicopter, the DJ booth, the loungers, the lifeguard chair with its swinging sign, and the Wojak busts.
- `scene.ts`: the sunset yard and its POOL RULES sign, composition, the tension pacing (the chain pulse, notches and fake yanks), the crash choreography (the yank, a fuse, a hit-stop, slow motion and a camera punch toward the drain), the HUD (captions, multiplier, holders and LP readout, the secured badge) and the round-phase logic.
- `audio.ts`: the shared page audio (a copy of `scripts/shell/audio.ts`): a recorded poolside score whose filter opens with the tension, milestone dings and airhorns on the caption ladder, a tick each time the dev worries the chain (quiet once you are out), creaks as it notches tighter, a ratchet and a gasp for each fake yank, splashes for every landing, a whoosh and a scream for the helicopter, an airhorn for the whale, a yeet for the yank, a splash stinger, glugs while the pool drains, a camera shutter for the selfie, and the cash-out fanfare.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round (5.68×) used by the gallery.

Water level, holder count, slosh, murk, the chain, the helicopter's passes and the whale all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. A round met late (or a crash missed while the tab was hidden) settles into place quietly: the crowd afloat, the pool drained, the dev already gone. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the screen shake, the hit-stop and slow motion, the camera punch, the dev's tremble and chain tugs, the crowd's flinch, the flamingo's drop (it fades in instead), the rotor blur and the selfie flash, and it slows the snap of a fake yank to a lift. Sound is opt-in through the Sound button.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Exit Liquidity URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to climb out. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/exit-liquidity/`.
