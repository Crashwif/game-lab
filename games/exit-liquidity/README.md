# Exit Liquidity

The liquidity pool is an actual pool. A backyard party at golden hour: a DJ Wojak, an LP booth, loungers, an empty lifeguard chair whose sign reads NO LIFEGUARD (HE SOLD), and the dev on his lounger with the plug chain round his wrist. Your degen floats on an inflatable flamingo. As the displayed multiplier rises the water rises with it and turns murkier, more degens cannonball in, the holder count ticks up, the chain goes taut, the dev's grin widens and he sits up with the chain wound round his fist, trembling near the top; from 2.5× an AIRDROP helicopter crosses the sunset at every milestone and tips three fresh degens straight down into the pool; past 5× a whale surfaces under the crowd. An accepted cashout paddles you to the ladder, out onto the deck and onto a lounger with a towel, a cocktail and shades, to a burst of confetti. The crash is the dev pulling the plug: he is on his feet yanking the chain, the plug gives a few frames later, the picture holds for a hit-stop and runs slow as the water funnels down the drain, everyone still floating spins down with it, one sad Wojak is left in the puddle, and the dev steps to the rim for a selfie with the empty pool ("say RUGGED", flash) before he strolls off with a bag, gloating "thx for the liquidity". A POOL RULES board on the fence has warned everyone: no selling, no lifeguard, dev may pull plug.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `pool.ts`: the cutaway water body with its level, slosh, murk, whale, drain funnel and splashes; the rim, ladder, plug and chain.
- `party.ts`: holders (cannonball arcs, the helicopter drops, floating, the drain spiral), your avatar's paddle, climb, walk and lounge, the confetti, the dev's grin, lean, yank, selfie and exit with his speech bubbles, the AIRDROP helicopter, the DJ booth, the loungers, the lifeguard chair with its swinging sign, and the Wojak busts.
- `scene.ts`: the sunset yard and its POOL RULES sign, composition, the crash choreography (the yank, a fuse, a hit-stop, slow motion and a camera punch toward the drain), the HUD (captions, multiplier, holders and LP readout, the secured badge) and the round-phase logic.
- `audio.ts`: the shared page audio (a copy of `scripts/shell/audio.ts`): a eurodance set that tightens with the multiplier, milestone dings and airhorns on the caption ladder, splashes for every landing, a whoosh and a scream for the helicopter, an airhorn for the whale, creaks as the chain notches tighter, a yeet for the yank, a splash stinger with the tape stop, glugs while the pool drains, a camera shutter for the selfie, and the cash-out fanfare.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round (5.68×) used by the gallery.

Water level, holder count, slosh, murk, the chain, the helicopter's passes and the whale all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. A round met late (or a crash missed while the tab was hidden) settles into place quietly: the crowd afloat, the pool drained, the dev already gone. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the screen shake, the hit-stop and slow motion, the camera punch, the dev's tremble, the rotor blur and the selfie flash. Sound is opt-in through the Sound button.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the Exit Liquidity URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to climb out. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/exit-liquidity/`.
