# King of the Hill

Sisyphus, but the boulder is your token. A degen ape pushes a giant coin up a hill whose profile is the bonding curve, so the slope steepens exactly as the market cap grows. The coin swells with the displayed multiplier, holders hop onto its rim and bail at milestones, a crown lands on it at King of the Hill, a cap and gown at graduation and a Raydium flag after that, and the sky drains from dawn to space as the moon fills the frame. The dev watches from a throne on a cloud with a big red `SELL` lever beside him: the cloud drifts in over the ape and his hand drifts from the armrest toward the lever with the tension, trembling more as it closes in, and at fixed multipliers he feints: grabs the handle, clicks it, lets go and quips (`jk`, `relax`, `not yet`…). From the second milestone on an `AIRDROP` plane crosses the sky and pushes crates out on parachutes behind the coin, and airdrop farmers sprint in from downhill to fight over them and drag them away. A `YOU` tag marks the player's ape (`ANON` for a spectator). An accepted cashout has him let go at once and celebrate while a Lambo pulls up behind him and the community runs in to push (confetti as he hops in, then `IT'S A CTO NOW` and a short regret ladder as the coin climbs on without him); the crash is the dev grabbing and yanking the lever: the coin lets go, the ape rears back and throws his hands up at what is coming back down, and it squashes him flat. If he has cashed out but not yet left, he dives clear toward the camera and the community takes the hit: `DODGED`.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `hill.ts`: the slope as a function of distance (integrated for height), the camera mapping, the altitude sky with stars and a growing moon, and the hill body with contours, rocks and wooden roadside signs planted up the curve (BONDING CURVE → at the foot, WELCOME TO VALHALLA near the top).
- `audio.ts`: the page's sound (the shell's copy, the same in every game). The scene asks for the `eurodance` style with a `slam` crash: a pumping four-on-the-floor that tightens with the tension, a heartbeat that quickens with it while the ape is still pushing his own bag, a stomp for each foot the ape plants, a thud on every bump, a `yeet` for each holder who bails, a creak as the dev's hand crosses each notch toward the lever and a ratchet click for each feint, a rising ding and airhorn at the caption's milestones, the plane's engine and the crates' thuds, the cash-out's register and fanfare with the Lambo's engine, and at the crash the ratchet and slam of the lever, the ape's scream (or a whoosh as he dives clear), and the thud and punch of the pancake.
- `dev.ts`: the dev on his cloud: the throne, the `SELL` lever on its base, the hoodie, the shades, a grin that widens with the tension, the reaching hand (a spring toward the handle, hovering at up to 82 % of the way while the round runs, with a notch event every eighth for the sound), the tremble that grows from a fifth of the way there, the feints (the hand closes on the handle, clicks the lever a notch and lets go, with a quip bubble), the pull at the crash (his hand closes on the handle, winds the lever back for 60 ms, then throws it over on an under-damped spring with recoil through him) and the plaque that flips from `DEV · DIAMOND HANDS` to `DEV · SOLD · SEE YA`.
- `airdrop.ts`: the `AIRDROP` plane, flown in screen space under the caption band, the crates (three in the air at most) that drop, open a chute, swing down and land on the hill behind the coin, and the farmers (two per crate) who sprint in from downhill, speeding up and braking into the crate with legs that cycle with the ground they cover, squabble for a beat and carry the crate off.
- `coin.ts`: the coin: radius from the market cap, rolling from the distance pushed, rollbacks and wobble on bumps (quickening through the first few multiples), clingers who hop onto the rim and jeets that tumble off, milestone gear, and the crash roll: it keeps the speed it had (and any setback in progress), throws its remaining holders off, rolls down with dust, and rolls out across the flat below the foot of the hill to rock to a stop.
- `ape.ts`: the ape rig: uphill-bending knees, soles aligned to the ground, hands on the rim through two-bone arms, a lean that grows with fear, bracing on bumps, the cash-out (he lets go where he stands, arms up in a V with a fist pump) and the hop into the Lambo with feet following the hips, the brace (he lets go where he stands, rears back and throws both hands up as the coin comes back), the contact test against his torso, head and hands, the squash (120 ms, flattening across the slope and widening along it) into a pancake that jiggles to rest, and the dive clear for an ape who already cashed out.
- `gait.ts`: alternating uphill steps driven by actual travel. Support feet stay planted in world space while the other foot lifts and swings forward; a stopped ape finishes its step and stands still. Round resets and large position jumps replant the feet.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the cash-out (the Lambo starts 380 px behind him and eases in, 40 pieces of confetti as he hops in, then pulls away) and the community that runs in and pushes on once he lets go, camera following, the `YOU` / `ANON` tag, HUD (captions, multiplier, market cap, the secured badge, and a flash over each holder who bails: JEETED, PAPER HANDS, SEE YA NERD, SOLD FOR A SANDWICH, NGMI), the dev's feints (at 1.8×, 2.8×, 4.5×, 8× and 15×, then every 12 s from 51 s), the sound cues, the round-phase logic, and a short dip to dark over the cut to the next round's betting. The crash is choreographed: the dev grabs and yanks the lever, the coin lets go as it goes over (the fuse) and rolls back while the ape braces, and when it first touches him the picture holds for a 150 ms hit-stop while the camera punches in 10 % and holds for 0.3 s, the squash runs at a third speed, and only then does the payoff land: `REKT` for the player, `RUGGED` for a spectator, `DODGED` for a player who cashed out, as soon as he is clear (his community takes the hit when the coin reaches them). Whatever happens, the payoff lands within 1.5 s. A crash met late settles straight into the aftermath: the lever pulled, the coin at rest on the flat below the foot, the pancake, no effects.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round (5.09×) used by the gallery.

Distance up the hill, coin size, bump cadence, the market-cap readout, the milestones, the dev's hand, his feints and the airdrops all follow the displayed multiplier (or the round's clock); nothing in the presentation changes or anticipates the committed outcome. The tension the dev, the ape's face, the heartbeat and the music follow is `1 - 1/x`: a third of the way at 1.5×, half at 2×, two thirds at 3×, where most rounds end; the hill, the coin, the sky and the moon follow the slower `log2(x)`, so long rounds keep changing. In portrait the readout keeps the live multiplier, with `OUT` and the exit beside it after a cash-out. The published game uses full animation regardless of browser motion preferences. Sound is opt-in through the Sound button and stays off until it is pressed.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the King of the Hill URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to call the Lambo; cash out just before a crash to see him dive clear. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/king-of-the-hill/`.
