# King of the Hill

Sisyphus, but the boulder is your token. A degen ape pushes a giant coin up a hill whose profile is the bonding curve, so the slope steepens exactly as the market cap grows. The coin swells with the displayed multiplier, holders cling to its rim and bail at milestones, a crown lands on it at King of the Hill, a cap and gown at graduation and a Raydium flag after that, and the sky drains from dawn to space as the moon fills the frame. The dev watches from a throne on a cloud with a big red `SELL` lever beside him: the cloud drifts in over the ape and his hand drifts from the armrest toward the lever with the tension, trembling over it near the top. From the second milestone on an `AIRDROP` plane crosses the sky and pushes crates out on parachutes behind the coin, and airdrop farmers sprint in from downhill to fight over them and drag them away. An accepted cashout brings a Lambo (with confetti as he hops in); the crash is the dev pulling the lever: the coin lets go, the ape throws his hands up at what is coming back down, and it rolls over him.

- `motion.ts`: the shared toolkit: exact damped springs, easing, deterministic noise, a seeded generator.
- `hill.ts`: the slope as a function of distance (integrated for height), the camera mapping, the altitude sky with stars and a growing moon, and the hill body with contours, rocks and wooden roadside signs planted up the curve (BONDING CURVE → at the foot, WELCOME TO VALHALLA near the top).
- `audio.ts`: the page's sound (the shell's copy, the same in every game). The scene asks for the `eurodance` style with a `slam` crash: a pumping four-on-the-floor that tightens with the multiplier, a stomp for each foot the ape plants, a thud on every bump, a `yeet` for each holder who bails, a creak as the dev's hand crosses each notch toward the lever, a rising ding and airhorn at the caption's milestones, the plane's engine and the crates' thuds, the cash-out's register and fanfare with the Lambo's engine, and at the crash the ratchet and slam of the lever, the ape's scream, and the thud and punch of the pancake.
- `dev.ts`: the dev on his cloud: the throne, the `SELL` lever on its base, the hoodie, the shades, a grin that widens with the tension, the reaching hand (a spring toward the handle, hovering at up to 82 % of the way while the round runs, with a notch event every quarter for the sound), the tremble over it near the top, the pull at the crash (the lever thrown over on an under-damped spring with recoil through him) and the plaque that flips from `DEV · DIAMOND HANDS` to `DEV · SOLD · SEE YA`.
- `airdrop.ts`: the `AIRDROP` plane, flown in screen space under the caption band, the crates (three in the air at most) that drop, open a chute, swing down and land on the hill behind the coin, and the farmers (two per crate) who sprint in from downhill, squabble for a beat and carry the crate off.
- `coin.ts`: the coin: radius from the market cap, rolling from the distance pushed, rollbacks and wobble on bumps, clingers on the rim and jeets that tumble off, milestone gear, and the crash roll with dust.
- `ape.ts`: the ape rig: uphill-bending knees, soles aligned to the ground, hands on the rim through two-bone arms, a lean that grows with fear, bracing on bumps, the hop into the Lambo with feet following the hips, the brace (he lets go where he stands, leans back and throws both hands up as the coin comes back) and the pancake.
- `gait.ts`: alternating uphill steps driven by actual travel. Support feet stay planted in world space while the other foot lifts and swings forward; a stopped ape finishes its step and stands still. Round resets and large position jumps replant the feet.
- `scene.ts`: composition, the Lambo pick-up (40 pieces of confetti as he hops in) and the community that pushes on after the ape leaves, camera following, HUD (captions, multiplier, market cap, the secured badge, and a flash over each holder who bails: JEETED, PAPER HANDS, SEE YA NERD, SOLD FOR A SANDWICH, NGMI), the sound cues and the round-phase logic. The crash is choreographed: the dev pulls the lever, the coin lets go a few frames later (the fuse) and rolls back while the ape braces, and when it reaches him the picture holds for a 70 ms hit-stop, the pancake runs at a third speed, the camera punches in on it and eases back out, and only then does the REKT land; with the ape gone, the community gets the same treatment. A crash met late settles straight into the aftermath: the lever pulled, the coin at the foot of the hill, the pancake, no effects.
- `main.ts`: SDK connection, round lifecycle, the player's bet and cashout, input controls and the animation loop.
- `replay.json`: a verifiable settled example round (5.09×) used by the gallery.

Distance up the hill, coin size, bump cadence, the market-cap readout, the milestones, the dev's hand and the airdrops all follow the displayed multiplier; nothing in the presentation changes the committed outcome. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the screen shake, the hit-stop, the slow motion, the camera punch-in, the dev's tremble and the twinkle. Sound is opt-in through the Sound button and stays off until it is pressed.

Run `npm run dev` from the repository root and open the King of the Hill URL it prints. Join with 50 local credits, then cash out during a running round to call the Lambo. `npm run build` produces the three publishable files in `dist/king-of-the-hill/`.
