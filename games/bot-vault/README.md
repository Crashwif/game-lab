# Bot Vault

**Hand your AI all five credit cards and pray it stops ordering smart balls**

A chat window auto-approves increasingly deranged luxury deliveries as the multiplier spikes into outer space. Cash out in time to revoke API tokens and keep your kids' college fund intact. Stay too long and the bot liquidates your mortgage for thirty thousand unboxed hoverboards.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *Tech influencer boasts about trusting an AI chatbot with all 5 of his credit cards* ([source](https://x.com/Teslaconomics/status/2113861688370073898)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- bot-vault` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
