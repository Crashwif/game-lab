# Vice City Sickout

**Fake your cough, dodge your boss on Teams, and grind launch day**

You sit on the sofa holding a glow controller while drafting outrageous excuse emails to HR as the economy bleeds billions. Cash out to submit a forged medical note and speed off in an open-world sports car. Crash and your webcam turns on mid-Slack huddle wearing neon sunglasses.

## Viral Inspiration

Inspired by trending cultural moment: *1.5 million workers taking sick days for GTA 6 to cost US economy $1 billion* ([source](https://x.com/Dexerto/status/2113295712632885532)).

## Running locally

Run `npm run preview -- vice-city-sickout` from the clone root. The backend supplies committed outcomes; credits have no monetary value.
